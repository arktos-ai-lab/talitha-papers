| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Local/Native South African languages are classified as low-resource languages. As such, it is essential to build the resources for these languages so that they can benefit from advances in the field of natural language processing. In this work, the focus was to create annotated news datasets for the isiZulu and Siswati native languages based on news topic classification tasks and present the findings from these baseline classification models. Due to the shortage of data for these native South African languages, the datasets that were created were augmented and oversampled to increase data size and overcome class classification imbalance. In total, four different classification models were used namely Logistic regression, Naive bayes, XGBoost and LSTM. These models were trained on three different word embeddings namely Bag-Of-Words, TFIDF and Word2vec. The results of this study showed that XGBoost, Logistic Regression and LSTM, trained from Word2vec performed better than the other combinations. | Lugha za Asili za Afrika Kusini zinaainishwa kama lugha zenye rasilimali chache. Kutokana na uhaba wa data kwa lugha hizi asilia za Afrika Kusini, seti za data zilizoundwa ziliongezwa na kusampuliwa kupita kiasi ili kuongeza ukubwa wa data na kukabiliana na kukosekana kwa usawa wa uainishaji wa madarasa. | Globalisation and the increase in digital communications have created the demand for NLP systems that enable fast communication between people speaking different languages. However, some languages are missing in these systems. For instance, there are roughly 7000 spoken languages on the planet and Most of them still are not included in the NLP systems, primarily because they do not have the labelled corpora to build those NLP systems (Baumann & Pierrehumbert 2014). These languages with scarce or no resources are low-resourced languages (Whyatt & Pavlović 2019). The language resources include (but are not limited to) the annotated corpora and core technologies. Examples of core technologies include lemmatisers, part of speech tagger and morphological decomposers (Eiselen & Puttkammer 2014). On the other hand, the languages with high resources are the ones that have most of the resources needed to build the NLP technology (Xu & Fung 2013). | Utandawazi na ongezeko la mawasiliano ya kidijitali vimezua hitaji la mifumo ya NLP inayowezesha mawasiliano ya haraka kati ya watu wanaozungumza lugha tofauti. That is the low-resourced language gets translated into the resource-rich language (Tang et al. 2018). However, in most cases, this approach suffers from language biases and may be impractical to achieve in real life (Tang et al. 2018). Sometimes the direct translation may be impossible or inaccurate due to language differences. Hence, the translated data will require manual processing thereafter, which is tedious and time-consuming. Manually creating data for low-resourced languages is time-consuming but a good approach, moreover, it introduces minimal language biases and more accurate than translated datasets (Shamsfard n.d.). | Mbinu iliyopo inayotumika kupunguza changamoto za data za lugha zenye rasilimali chache, ni mbinu ya tafsiri ya lugha. | **2.2 Dealing with data imbalance** | **2.2 Kukabiliana na ukosefu wa usawa wa data** |
| The Synthetic Minority Oversampling Technique (SMOTE) is another technique that can be adopted when the learning is done on an imbalanced dataset, since it solves the problem of class imbalance (Fernández et al. 2018). SMOTE works by generating synthetic examples through inserting different values(words) in minority class, the values are randomly picked from a defined neighbourhood within feature space. Minority class is selected, then obtain the k-nearest neighbours of the same minority class and therefore utilises the k- neighbours to create the new synthetic examples (Fernández et al. 2018). | Mbinu ya Uongezaji Nafasi wa Wachache Sanisi (SMOTE) ni mbinu nyingine inayoweza kupitishwa wakati ujifunzaji unafanywa kwenye seti ya data isiyo na usawa, kwa kuwa inatatua tatizo la ukosefu wa usawa wa darasa (Fernández et al. 2018). SMOTE hufanya kazi kwa kuzalisha mifano sanisi kupitia kuingiza thamani tofauti (maneno) katika darasa la wachache, thamani hizo huchaguliwa kwa nasibu kutoka kwa ujirani uliobainishwa ndani ya nafasi ya vipengele. Darasa la wachache huchaguliwa, kisha hupata majirani wa karibu wa k wa darasa hilohilo la wachache na kwa hiyo hutumia majirani hao wa k kuunda mifano mipya sanisi (Fernández et al. 2018). |
| **2.3 Related work** | **2.3 Kazi Zinazohusiana** |
| Supervised learning models perform better on larger labelled datasets, which presents a challenge for low-resourced languages as they don't have enough data and annotating data can be expensive (Fang & Cohn 2017). Most prior studies focused on developing parallel corpora between low and resource-rich languages, but parallel corpora are often unavailable for some low-resourced languages (Fang & Cohn 2017). Work by Zoph et al. (2016) identified low-resourced languages and investigated the idea of distance learning on machine translation. Since English and French are resource-rich languages, the two languages trained a neural machine translation (NMT) (Zoph et al. 2016). An English-French neural machine translation (NMT) model was initially trained. Afterwards, the NMT model initialised another NMT model to be used on a low-resourced and high-resourced pair (e.g. Uzbek-English) (Zoph et al. 2016), as such utilising transfer learning. In this case, the low-resourced languages investigated for transfer were Uzbek, Hausa, Turkish and Urdu. The transfer learning was shown to improve the BLEU (bilingual evaluation understudy) for low-resourced Neural machine translation (Zoph et al. 2016). | Miundo ya Ujifunzaji elekezwa hufanya vizuri zaidi kwenye seti kubwa za data zilizo na lebo, jambo ambalo huleta changamoto kwa lugha zenye rasilimali chache kwani hazina data ya kutosha na kuweka lebo kwenye data kunaweza kuwa ghali (Fang & Cohn 2017). Tafiti nyingi za awali zililenga kuunda korasi sambamba kati ya lugha zenye rasilimali chache na zile zenye rasilimali nyingi, lakini korasi sambamba mara nyingi hazipatikani kwa baadhi ya lugha zenye rasilimali chache (Fang & Cohn 2017). Kazi ya Zoph et al. (2016) ilibainisha lugha zenye rasilimali chache na kuchunguza wazo la ujifunzaji wa mbali kwenye tafsiri ya mashine. Kwa kuwa Kiingereza na Kifaransa ni lugha zenye rasilimali nyingi, lugha hizo mbili zilifunza tafsiri ya mashine ya neva (NMT) (Zoph et al. 2016). Mfano wa tafsiri ya mashine ya neva (NMT) wa Kiingereza-Kifaransa ulifunzwa awali. Baadaye, mfano wa NMT ulianzisha mfano mwingine wa NMT utakaotumika kwa jozi ya lugha yenye rasilimali chache na yenye rasilimali nyingi (k.m. Kiuzbeki-Kiingereza) (Zoph et al. 2016), hivyo kutumia Ujifunzaji hamishi. Katika kisa hiki, lugha zenye rasilimali chache zilizochunguzwa kwa ajili ya uhamishaji zilikuwa Kiuzbeki, Kihausa, Kituruki na Kiurdu. Ujifunzaji hamishi ulionyeshwa kuboresha BLEU (bilingual evaluation understudy) kwa tafsiri ya mashine ya neva yenye rasilimali chache (Zoph et al. 2016). |
| Work by Nguyen & Chiang (2017) explored transfer learning between the two low-resourced languages Turkey and Uzbek by first pairing each language with English and then generating the parallel data. Then, split the words with Bytes Pair Encoding (BPE) to maximise the overlapping vocab (Nguyen & Chiang 2017). The model and word embedding are trained on the first language pair (Turkey-English) and then the same model parameters and word embeddings were transferred to the other model that trained the second language pair (Uzbek-English). This technique improved the BLEU by 4.3% (Nguyen & Chiang 2017). | Kazi ya Nguyen & Chiang (2017) ilichunguza Ujifunzaji hamishi kati ya lugha mbili zenye rasilimali chache, Uturuki na Kiuzbeki, kwa kwanza kuoanisha kila lugha na Kiingereza na kisha kuzalisha data sambamba. Kisha, gawanya maneno kwa Usimbaji wa Jozi za Baiti (BPE) ili kuongeza msamiati unaopishana (Nguyen & Chiang 2017). Mfano na upachikaji wa neno hufunzwa kwenye jozi ya lugha ya kwanza (Uturuki-Kiingereza) na kisha vigezo sawa vya mfano na upachikaji wa maneno vilihamishwa hadi kwenye mfano mwingine uliofunza jozi ya lugha ya pili (Kiuzbeki-Kiingereza). Mbinu hii iliboresha BLEU kwa 4.3% (Nguyen & Chiang 2017). |
| The datasets of low-resourced South African languages, isiZulu collected from isolezwe and National Centre for Human Language Technology (www.sadilar.org); and Sepedi collected from National Centre for Human Language Technology were used to evaluate the performance of open-vocabulary models on the small datasets, the evaluated models include n-grams, LSTM, RNN, FFNN, and transformers. The performance of the models was evaluated using the byte pair encoding (BPE). The RNN performed better than the rest of the models on both the isiZulu and Sepedi datasets (Mesham et al. 2021). This technique works by selecting the minority class and the total amount of oversampling to balance the classes, then the k-nearest neighbours for that particular class are obtained, therefore, iteratively the k nearest neighbours are randomly chosen to create new instances Fernández et al. (2018). This oversampling technique was used to balance the classes and increase the dataset. Note that SMOTE uses a different approach from the Data Augmentation approach presented earlier. | SMOTE ni mbinu ya sampuli zaidi inayotumika kusawazisha upya seti ya mafunzo asilia kupitia uundaji wa sampuli sintetiki za darasa la wachache Fernández et al.
