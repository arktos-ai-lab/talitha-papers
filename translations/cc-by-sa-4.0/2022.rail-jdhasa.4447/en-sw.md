| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Appropriate training data is a prerequisite for the development of natural language processing (NLP) techniques. Vast amounts of language data are typically required to develop NLP tools that perform at state-of-the-art level. Such abundant resources are currently only available in a few languages. The remaining languages have to find alternative ways to become "NLP-enabled". The aim of the study reported on here is to make more language data available to support NLP development in the official languages of South Africa. In this paper we present the idea of generating text data by means of translation. We also propose the use of topic modelling to identify text in a highly resourced source language that will yield meaningful translations in under-resourced target languages. More specifically, the paper describes how topic modelling was used to identify English Wikipedia articles that should be suitable for translation into South Africa's 10 other official languages. | Data ya mafunzo ifaayo ni sharti la awali kwa ajili ya ukuzaji wa mbinu za uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP). Lugha zilizobaki zinapaswa kutafuta njia mbadala ili kuwezeshwa kwa "NLP". Lengo la utafiti unaoripotiwa hapa ni kufanya data zaidi ya lugha ipatikane ili kusaidia maendeleo ya NLP katika lugha rasmi za Afrika Kusini. Katika makala hii tunawasilisha wazo la kuzalisha data za maandishi kwa njia ya tafsiri. | **1 Introduction** | **1 Utangulizi** |
| The resources required to implement state-of-the art performance in natural language processing (NLP) applications like text generation, question answering, automatic speech recognition, etc. are currently only available in a few well-resourced languages. The other 6 000-odd languages that are spoken in the world today have to find alternative ways to prepare themselves for a digital future society in which the ability to process language and speech automatically will be a prerequisite for participation. Applications like search engines and chat bots are developed using text data while others, like automatic dictation systems, require both text and speech data to be implemented. The current study is specifically aimed at developing text resources that are suitable for language modelling in South Africa's official languages. | Rasilimali zinazohitajika kutekeleza utendaji wa hali ya juu katika programu za uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP) kama vile uzalishaji wa maandishi, kujibu maswali, utambuzi wa usemi wa kiotomatiki, n.k. kwa sasa zinapatikana tu katika lugha chache zenye rasilimali nyingi. | **2 Background** | **2 Usuli** |
| Previous attempts to translate NLP tasks and data sets from highly resourced to under-resourced languages have encountered various challenges related to the differences between the source and target languages. For instance, during the development of the African Wordnet, it was found that many concepts in existing Wordnets were not lexicalised in South African languages (Griesel & Bosch 2020). This difference was overcome by using the SIL Comparative African Wordlist (SILCAWL) introduced by Snider & Roberts (2004) as a guideline to add new synsets to the African Wordnet. This list consists of a vast selection of words relating to various things, such as body parts, emotions and stages of life in English and French. The content of the entries in the list is - to a large extent - lexicalised in South Africa's official languages. Using the SILCAWL as a point of departure has the additional advantage that it does not perpetuate culturally and cognitively biased language resources. | Majaribio ya awali ya kutafsiri kazi za NLP na seti za data kutoka lugha zenye rasilimali nyingi kwenda lugha zenye rasilimali chache yamekumbana na changamoto mbalimbali zinazohusiana na tofauti kati ya lugha chanzi na lugha lengwa. Kwa mfano, wakati wa uundaji wa WordNet ya Afrika, iligundulika kuwa dhana nyingi katika WordNet zilizopo hazikuwa zimewekwa katika msamiati wa Lugha za Afrika Kusini (Griesel & Bosch 2020). Tofauti hii ilitatuliwa kwa kutumia SIL Comparative African Wordlist (SILCAWL) iliyoanzishwa na Snider & Roberts (2004) kama mwongozo wa kuongeza synseti mpya kwenye WordNet ya Afrika. Orodha hii ina uteuzi mkubwa wa maneno yanayohusiana na mambo mbalimbali, kama vile sehemu za mwili, hisia na hatua za maisha katika Kiingereza na Kifaransa. Maudhui ya viingizo katika orodha hii - kwa kiasi kikubwa - yamewekwa katika msamiati wa lugha rasmi za Afrika Kusini. Kutumia SILCAWL kama hatua ya mwanzo kuna faida ya ziada kwamba hakuendelezi rasilimali za lugha zenye upendeleo wa kitamaduni na kiakili. |

| **3 Topic modelling** | **3 Uundaji wa mada** |
| Topic modelling is a text mining technique used to uncover latent topics in large collections of documents. Most topic models are unsupervised, thus making them powerful tools in addressing real-world problems as data is typically unlabelled in practice. Topic models have found use in many applications, including sentiment analysis (Rana et al. 2016), text classification, document categorisation, summarisation, etc. (Boyd-Graber et al. 2017). | Uundaji wa mada ni mbinu ya uchimbaji wa maandishi inayotumika kufichua mada fiche katika mkusanyiko mkubwa wa nyaraka. Modeli za mada zimepata matumizi katika matumizi mengi, ikiwa ni pamoja na uchambuzi wa hisia (Rana et al. 2016), uainishaji wa maandishi, uainishaji wa nyaraka, ufupisho, n.k. (Boyd-Graber et al. 2017). |

| By virtue of being unsupervised, evaluating topic models is a challenging task. Although some authors use perplexity[4] or held-out likelihood as measures of topic model performance, it has been shown that such intrinsic measures of model performance do not correlate with human understanding of topics (Chang et al. 2009). In light of this, coherence measures have become more popular. | **5 Document selection process** | **5 Mchakato wa uteuzi wa hati** |
| The objective of this research is to create a collection of sentences that can be used for NLP research in under-resourced languages, in a manner that is both methodological and systematic. To this end, we propose using topic modelling as a selection method. This section provides the details of the selection process. | Lengo la utafiti huu ni kuunda mkusanyiko wa sentensi ambao unaweza kutumika kwa utafiti wa NLP katika lugha zenye rasilimali chache, kwa namna ambayo ni ya kimbinu na ya utaratibu. Kwa lengo hili, tunapendekeza kutumia uundaji wa mada kama njia ya uteuzi. Sehemu hii inatoa maelezo ya mchakato wa uteuzi. |
| **5.1 Topic model training** | **5.1 Mafunzo ya mfano wa mada** |
| Given the aim of our research, we sought to create a data set that is generally relevant and not domain specific by selecting sentences from Wikipedia articles. To this end, we first created a training set, based on articles from various general topics. These general topics were taken from the SILCAWL comparative word list introduced in Section 2. The English words in the list were used as search terms in Wikipedia and the associated articles were retrieved from the web. After cleaning the data and removing any potentially offensive words, the resulting corpus contained 1 698 documents with an average of 1 132 words per document. | Kwa kuzingatia lengo la utafiti wetu, tulijitahidi kuunda seti ya data ambayo kwa ujumla inafaa na si mahususi kwa kikoa fulani kwa kuchagua sentensi kutoka makala za Wikipedia. Kwa lengo hili, kwanza tuliunda seti ya mafunzo, kulingana na makala kutoka mada mbalimbali za jumla. Mada hizi za jumla zilichukuliwa kutoka kwa orodha ya maneno linganishi ya SILCAWL iliyoletwa katika Sehemu ya 2. Maneno ya Kiingereza katika orodha yalitumiwa kama istilahi za utafutaji katika Wikipedia na makala zinazohusiana zilipatikana kutoka kwenye wavuti. Baada ya kusafisha data na kuondoa maneno yoyote yanayoweza kukera, korasi iliyotokana ilikuwa na hati 1 698 zenye wastani wa maneno 1 132 kwa kila hati. |
| A topic model was then trained on this data using gensim. The hyperparameters were learned from the data, but the model was run for different numbers of topics, K = {100,200, ..., 1000}. The average coherence score for each model is shown in Figure 2. It can be seen that the coherence does not increase significantly beyond K = 800, thus K = 800 was chosen to be the ideal number of topics for the training data. | Kielelezo cha mada kisha kilifunzwa kwenye data hii kwa kutumia gensim. Parameta juu zilijifunzwa kutoka kwa data, lakini kielelezo kiliendeshwa kwa idadi tofauti za mada, K = {100,200, ..., 1000}. Kiwango cha wastani cha uwiano kwa kila mfano umeonyeshwa katika Kielelezo 2. Inaweza kuonekana kwamba uwiano hauongezeki kwa kiasi kikubwa zaidi ya K = 800, hivyo K = 800 ilichaguliwa kuwa idadi bora ya mada kwa data ya mafunzo. |
| Table 1 shows the top 25 topics from K = 800 arranged in order of decreasing coherence. It is evident from the table that the topic model was able to pick up various topics including, for example, poverty (topic 98), health (topic 224) and time (topic 551). Note that the choice of overall topic names are determined by the user and thus are subjective. The full set of topics can be found at https://github.com/jrmazarura/wikipedia-sentence-selection. | Jedwali 1 linaonyesha mada 25 za juu kutoka K = 800 zilizopangwa kwa mpangilio wa uwiano unaopungua. Ni dhahiri kutoka kwenye jedwali kwamba modeli ya mada iliweza kubaini mada mbalimbali zikiwemo, kwa mfano, umaskini (mada 98), afya (mada 224) na wakati (mada 551). Kumbuka kuwa uchaguzi wa majina ya mada kwa jumla huamuliwa na mtumiaji na hivyo ni wa kibinafsi. Seti kamili ya mada inaweza kupatikana kwenye https://github.com/jrmazarura/wikipedia-sentence-selection. |

| **5.2 Document selection using trained topic model** | **5.2 Uchaguzi wa hati kwa kutumia modeli ya mada iliyofunzwa** |
| The next step in the document selection process was to use the trained topic model to select articles from Wikipedia and then select sentences from them. For each article in Wikipedia, the model was used to determine the topic distribution and only articles satisfying the following conditions were retained: | Hatua iliyofuata katika mchakato wa uchaguzi wa hati ilikuwa kutumia modeli ya mada iliyofunzwa kuchagua makala kutoka Wikipedia na kisha kuchagua sentensi kutoka humo. Kwa kila makala katika Wikipedia, modeli ilitumika kubainisha usambazaji wa mada na ni makala yale tu yaliyokidhi masharti yafuatayo yalihifadhiwa: |
| 1. The dominant topic of the article had to be amongst the 500 topics with the highest coherence scores from the trained model. | 1. Mada kuu ya makala ilipaswa kuwa kati ya mada 500 zilizo na alama za uwiano wa juu zaidi kutoka kwa mfano uliofanywa mafunzo. |
| 2. The dominant topic needed to make up at least a quarter of the article's topic distribution. | 2. Mada inayotawala inahitajika kuunda angalau robo ya usambazaji wa mada ya makala. |
| The 500 topic cutoff used in #1 was selected by assessing the coherence scores. Based on inspection, the interpretability of the topics appeared to noticeably deteriorate as the coherence scores decreased below -2. The first 500 topics had coherence scores above this value. In addition, the 500-topic cutoff also ensured that a broad variety of topics were covered. Choosing The 25% cutoff used in #2 not only ensured that the topic made up a significant proportion of the article's topic, but it was also necessary to ensure that only some of the articles from Wikipedia were selected. | Kikomo cha mada 500 kilichotumika katika #1 kilichaguliwa kwa kutathmini alama za uwiano. Kulingana na ukaguzi, ufasirikaji wa mada ulionekana kuzorota kwa dhahiri kadiri alama za uwiano zilivyopungua chini ya -2. Mada 500 za kwanza zilikuwa na alama za uwiano juu ya thamani hii. Aidha, kikomo cha mada 500 pia kilihakikisha kwamba aina mbalimbali za mada zilishughulikiwa. Kuchagua kikomo cha 25% kilichotumika katika #2 hakukuhakikisha tu kwamba mada ilifanyiza sehemu muhimu ya mada ya makala, bali pia ilikuwa muhimu kuhakikisha kwamba ni baadhi tu ya makala kutoka Wikipedia zilichaguliwa. |
| After this step, the final collection of sentences was compiled by randomly selecting an equal number of articles from each topic and then randomly selecting one sentence from each article. For example, to create a collection of 5 000 sentences, 10 articles belonging to each of the 500 topics were randomly selected and one sentence was randomly selected from each of the articles. Only sentences with at least 15 words made up of 3 characters or more were selected. This was done to avoid retrieving sentences that were very short and did not contain much information. | Baada ya hatua hii, mkusanyiko wa mwisho wa sentensi ulikusanywa kwa kuchagua kwa nasibu idadi sawa ya makala kutoka kila mada na kisha kuchagua kwa nasibu sentensi moja kutoka kila makala. Kwa mfano, ili kuunda mkusanyiko wa sentensi 5 000, makala 10 zinazohusiana na kila moja ya mada 500 zilichaguliwa kwa nasibu na sentensi moja ilichaguliwa kwa nasibu kutoka kwa kila moja ya makala hizo. Sentensi zenye angalau maneno 15 yanayoundwa na herufi 3 au zaidi pekee ndizo zilizochaguliwa. Hii ilifanyika ili kuepuka kurejesha sentensi ambazo zilikuwa fupi sana na hazikuwa na taarifa nyingi. |

| **7 Discussion & future work** | **7 Majadiliano & kazi ya baadaye** |
| Our first attempt at using topic modelling to identify Wikipedia articles that would be suitable for translation from English into the 10 other South African languages yielded an extremely diverse set of sentences, many of which contain words that are probably not lexicalised in any of the target languages. Training the topic models on the SILCAWL was clearly not adequate to avoid this challenge. | Jaribio letu la kwanza la kutumia uundaji wa mada kutambua makala za Wikipedia ambazo zingefaa kutafsiriwa kutoka Kiingereza hadi lugha nyingine 10 za Afrika Kusini lilileta seti mbalimbali sana za sentensi, nyingi zikiwa na maneno ambayo huenda hayajawekwa katika leksika katika lugha lengwa yoyote. Mafunzo ya mifano ya mada juu ya SILCAWL ilikuwa wazi haitoshi ili kuepuka changamoto hii. |
| In the next phase of the research we will aim to find pruning criteria that could be used to "focus" the topic models on content that is more relevant to the South African context. We would also like to establish a relationship between the selected text and the resulting translation in the target language to ensure that both relevant and translatable text has been chosen. Such a measure will also allow a comparison between the proposed topic model approach to text selection and a fully random baseline. | Katika awamu inayofuata ya utafiti tutalenga kupata vigezo vya upogoaji ambavyo vinaweza kutumika "kulenga" mifano ya mada kwenye maudhui ambayo yanafaa zaidi kwa muktadha wa Afrika Kusini. Tungependa pia kuanzisha uhusiano kati ya maandishi yaliyochaguliwa na tafsiri inayotokana katika lugha lengwa ili kuhakikisha kwamba maandishi yanayofaa na yanayoweza kutafsirika yamechaguliwa. Kipimo kama hicho pia kitaruhusu ulinganisho kati ya mbinu iliyopendekezwa ya mfano wa mada kwa uteuzi wa maandishi na msingi wa nasibu kabisa. |
