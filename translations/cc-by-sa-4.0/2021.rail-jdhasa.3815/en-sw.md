| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| For this project, we collected and annotated data to develop language resources for the four official South African Nguni languages written with a conjunctive orthography. The data for these four languages is parallel to allow for comparative (computational) linguistic studies. The corpora have been annotated for three types of linguistic information (morphology, part-of-speech and lemma). The article focuses on the annotation procedure, design choices that were made along the way as well as the quality control steps used. Hopefully this description will give some guidance for similar projects on under-resourced languages in the future. | Kwa mradi huu, tulikusanya na kufafanua data ili kuendeleza rasilimali za lugha kwa lugha nne rasmi za Nguni za Afrika Kusini zinazoandikwa kwa tahajia unganishi. | **2 Background** | **2 Usuli** |
| South Africa has eleven official languages comprising nine Bantu languages and two Germanic languages (English and Afrikaans). The South African Bantu languages can generally be categorised into three language family groups: Four conjunctively written Nguni languages (isiNdebele, isiXhosa, isiZulu, and Siswati); five disjunctively written languages including four Sotho languages (Sepedi, Sesotho, Setswana, and Tshivenḓa) and one Tswa-Ronga language (Xitsonga). At least nine of these eleven languages are considered resource-scarce. | Afrika Kusini ina lugha rasmi kumi na moja zinazojumuisha lugha tisa za Kibantu na lugha mbili za Kijerumani (Kiingereza na Afrikaans). Lugha za Kibantu za Afrika Kusini kwa ujumla zinaweza kuainishwa katika makundi matatu ya familia ya lugha: Lugha nne za Kinguni zinazoandikwa kwa kuunganisha (isiNdebele, isiXhosa, isiZulu, na Siswati); lugha tano zinazoandikwa kwa kutenganisha zikiwemo lugha nne za Kisotho (Sepedi, Sesotho, Setswana, na Tshivenḓa) na lugha moja ya Kitwa-Ronga (Xitsonga). Angalau tisa kati ya lugha hizi kumi na moja zinachukuliwa kuwa na uhaba wa rasilimali. |
| Bantu languages have a few interesting linguistic characteristics that make it complex to deal with computationally: They are tone languages, have an elaborate system of noun classification (up to 21 classes), and the verbal morphology is complex and highly agglutinative. Especially the agglutinative nature of Bantu languages accounts for their complexity (Doke 1950): Words are formed by combining morphemes (distinct meaning bearing units), usually a root (for verbs) or a stem (for other word classes), with one or more affixes. These affixes are bound morphemes with a singular function within the word. Especially verbs are very productive through i.e. derivational morphology, resulting in a large vocabulary. | Lugha za Kibantu zina sifa chache za kuvutia za lugha ambazo hufanya kuwa vigumu kuzishughulikia kwa njia ya kompyuta: ni lugha za toni, zina mfumo mzuri wa uainishaji wa majina (hadi madaraja 21), na mofolojia ya maneno ni tata na yenye mchanganyiko mkubwa. Hasa asili ya uambishaji ya Lugha za Kibantu ndiyo inayochangia uchangamano wake (Doke 1950): Maneno huundwa kwa kuunganisha mofimu (vipashio bainifu vyenye kubeba maana), kwa kawaida mzizi (kwa vitenzi) au shina (kwa ngeli nyingine za maneno), pamoja na kiambishi kimoja au zaidi. Viambishi hivi ni morfemi vilivyoshikamana na kazi moja ndani ya neno. Hasa vitenzi ni vyenye tija sana kupitia mofolojia ya uambishaji, na hivyo kusababisha msamiati mkubwa. |
| Another factor to note is the writing system used for the different Bantu languages. A distinction is made between linguistic words and orthographic words as these two entities do not always coincide. For disjunctively written Bantu languages, several orthographic words can correspond to one linguistic word (Louwrens & Poulos 2006), whereas for conjunctively written Bantu languages (which we are working with here) generally one orthographic word corresponds to one or more linguistic words. | Kipengele kingine cha kuzingatia ni mfumo wa kuandika unaoitumika kwa lugha tofauti za Kibantu. Tofauti inafanywa kati ya maneno ya kiisimu na maneno ya kiothografia kwani hivi vitu viwili havilingani kila wakati. Kwa Lugha za Kibantu zinazoandikwa kwa njia ya kutenganisha, maneno kadhaa ya kiothografia yanaweza kulingana na neno moja la kiisimu (Louwrens & Poulos 2006), ilhali kwa Lugha za Kibantu zinazoandikwa kwa njia ya kuunganisha (ambazo tunashughulikia hapa) kwa ujumla neno moja la kiothografia linalingana na neno moja au zaidi ya kiisimu. |

| **4 Data** | **4 Data** |
| The dataset used for this project has been put together using randomly selected documents from the South African government domain websites (*.gov.za) and includes text on different topics, such as speeches, press releases, health information as well as other information about government departments and services. The usual mode of operation in translation departments is to translate from an English source document to one of the other languages. We therefore collected documents and websites that were available in English as well as the four Nguni languages, resulting in a parallel dataset with English as the source language. The reason for using government material was the relatively easy availability of data in general and parallel data in particular. The parallel nature of the data allows for comparative (computational) linguistic studies of these four Nguni languages. | Seti ya data iliyotumika kwa mradi huu imetengenezwa kwa kutumia hati zilizochaguliwa kwa nasibu kutoka tovuti za serikali za Afrika Kusini (*.gov.za) na inajumuisha maandishi juu ya mada mbalimbali, kama vile hotuba, taarifa za habari, habari za afya pamoja na habari nyingine kuhusu idara na huduma za serikali. Njia ya kawaida ya uendeshaji katika idara za utafsiri ni kutafsiri kutoka kwenye waraka chanzo wa Kiingereza hadi kwenye mojawapo ya lugha nyingine. Kwa hiyo tulikusanya nyaraka na tovuti ambazo zilikuwa zinapatikana kwa lugha ya Kiingereza pamoja na lugha nne za Nguni, matokeo yake ni seti ya data sambamba na Kiingereza kama lugha ya chanzo. Sababu ya kutumia nyenzo za serikali ilikuwa upatikanaji rahisi wa data kwa ujumla na data sambamba hasa. Asili sambamba ya data inaruhusu tafiti linganishi za kiisimu (kikokotozi) za lugha hizi nne za Kinguni. |
| We aimed for about 50,000 tokens for each language. This choice was based on experience with previous projects on the development of computational linguistics tools. Especially for conjunctively written languages with a large vocabulary enough data to train and test such tools is essential. At the same time, the project also needed to adhere to time and budget constraints. | Tulilenga takriban tokeni 50,000 kwa kila lugha. Chaguo hili lilikuwa kulingana na uzoefu wa miradi iliyotangulia katika maendeleo ya zana za lingustiki ya kompyuta. Hasa kwa lugha zinazoandikwa kwa muunganiko zenye msamiati mkubwa, data ya kutosha ya kufunza na kujaribu zana kama hizo ni muhimu. Wakati huo huo, mradi huo pia ulihitaji kufuata vikwazo vya muda na bajeti. |

| **5 Prerequisites for linguistic annotation** | **5 Mahitaji ya Awali kwa Uchambuzi wa Lugha** |
| Next to the collection of data, another goal of the project was to annotate each language corpus for three different types of linguistic information: morphology, part-of-speech (POS) and lemmas. | Kando na ukusanyaji wa data, lengo lingine la mradi lilikuwa kufafanua kila mkusanyiko wa lugha kwa aina tatu tofauti za taarifa za kiisimu: mofolojia, aina za maneno (POS) na lemmas. |
| Data annotated for morphology allows researchers to investigate morphological phenomena in real language corpora. The study of word creation processes can lead to a better understanding of these processes or even to new linguistic insights. | Data iliyofafanuliwa kwa mofolojia huwawezesha watafiti kuchunguza matukio ya kimofolojia katika mkusanyiko halisi wa lugha. Uchunguzi wa michakato ya uundaji wa maneno unaweza kusababisha uelewa bora wa michakato hii au hata maarifa mapya ya kiisimu. |
| Assigning categories to words according to their syntactic function in a sentence is called POS tagging. It is often used as a first step in syntactic analysis and can also be successfully leveraged for e.g. authorship analysis or writing style detection. | Kugawa kategoria kwa maneno kulingana na kazi yao ya kisintaksia katika sentensi kunaitwa uwekaji tagi wa POS. | The third type of linguistic annotation, lemmatisation, is generally seen as an indispensable source of linguistic information for spelling checkers, dictionaries, information retrieval systems, etc. | Aina ya tatu ya alama za lugha, yaani lemmatizesheni, kwa ujumla inachukuliwa kuwa chanzo muhimu sana cha habari za lugha kwa vipima vya imlaa, kamusi, mifumo ya utafutaji wa habari, n.k. |
| **5.1 Tag sets and protocols** | **5.1 Seti za tagi na itifaki** |
| Before starting the actual annotation of the data, we needed to develop protocols and tag sets for each type of annotation. The protocols explain how to annotate the data and what existing international standards apply (EAGLES 1996). The tag sets contain a list of permissible tags along with a description and examples for each tag. The current tag sets are refined versions of the ones developed for the National Centre for Human Language Technology (NCHLT) project described in (Eiselen & Puttkammer 2014). | Kabla ya kuanza ufafanuzi halisi wa data, tulihitaji kuunda itifaki na seti za lebo kwa kila aina ya ufafanuzi. Itifaki zinaeleza jinsi ya kufafanua data na ni viwango gani vya kimataifa vilivyopo vinavyotumika (EAGLES 1996). Seti za lebo zina orodha ya lebo zinazoruhusiwa pamoja na maelezo na mifano kwa kila lebo. Seti za lebo za sasa ni matoleo yaliyoboreshwa ya zile zilizoundwa kwa ajili ya mradi wa Kituo cha Kitaifa cha Teknolojia ya Lugha ya Binadamu (NCHLT) ulioelezwa katika (Eiselen & Puttkammer 2014). |
| For the morphological annotation layer the aim was to provide full morphological annotations labeling each morpheme. To achieve this, a total of 380 linguistically permissible morphology tags were defined, i.e. [VRoot] was used to indicate the verbal root or [SC15] for a subject concord of class 15. These were combined during annotation to yield full morphological analyses of the tokens present in the data. For example the isiXhosa word izinto ('things') was analysed as i[NPrePre10]- zin[BPre10]-to[NStem]. | Kwa ajili ya tabaka la alama za kimorfolojia, lengo lilikuwa ni kutoa alama kamili za kimorfolojia zinazoweka lebo kila morfimu. Ili kufanikisha hili, jumla ya lebo 380 za mofolojia zinazoruhusiwa kisarufi zilifafanuliwa, yaani [VRoot] ilitumika kuashiria mzizi wa kitenzi au [SC15] kwa upatanisho wa kiima wa ngeli ya 15. Hizi ziliunganishwa wakati wa ufafanuzi ili kutoa uchambuzi kamili wa kimofolojia wa tokeni zilizopo kwenye data. Kwa mfano neno la isiXhosa izinto ('vitu') lilichambuliwa kama i[NPrePre10]- zin[BPre10]-to[NStem]. |
| The POS tag set consists of 20 main word classes for all four languages, e.g. ADJ for adjectives or CONJ for conjunctions. Some tags include additional information on class numbers, e.g. N09 (noun class 9) or POSS06 (possessive class 6) resulting in a total of 107 unique POS tags available during annotation. | Seti ya lebo za POS inajumuisha makundi 20 kuu ya maneno kwa lugha zote nne, k.mf. ADJ kwa sifa au CONJ kwa viunganishi. Baadhi ya lebo zinajumuisha taarifa za ziada kuhusu nambari za ngeli, k.m. N09 (ngeli ya 9) au POSS06 (ngeli ya 6 ya kimilikishi) na hivyo kusababisha jumla ya lebo 107 za kipekee za POS zinazopatikana wakati wa ufafanuzi. |
| For lemmatisation, the aim was to identify the stem lemma for each token (Prinsloo 2009). The noun izinto in isiXhosa will be annotated with the stem lemma to. This stem lemma in combination with the POS tag N10 (noun class 10) encodes the essential syntactic information for the word izinto. | Kwa ulemishaji, lengo lilikuwa kutambua lemma ya shina kwa kila tokeni (Prinsloo 2009). Nomino izinto katika isiXhosa itafafanuliwa kwa lemma ya shina to. Lemma hii ya shina pamoja na lebo ya POS N10 (ngeli ya 10) inaandika taarifa muhimu ya kisintaksia kwa neno izinto. |
| **5.2 Linguistic experts** | **5.2 Wataalamu wa lugha** |
| Once the tag sets and protocols had been established, our corpora needed to be marked up with the relevant linguistic information. We considered the following two possibilities on how to accomplish the annotation of the data: linguistic experts or students could be recruited and trained or crowdsourcing could be used (Zaidan 2012). | Mara tu seti za lebo na itifaki zilipokuwa zimeanzishwa, kopusu zetu zilihitaji kuwekewa alama za taarifa muhimu za kiisimu. Tulichunguza uwezekano wafuatao mbili wa jinsi ya kufanya uainishaji wa data: wataalamu wa lugha au wanafunzi wangeajiriwa na kupewa mafunzo au utafutaji wa watu wengi (crowdsourcing) ungetumika (Zaidan 2012). |
| Especially for the morphological annotation, in-depth linguistic knowledge is needed to correctly annotate the languages covered in this project. For crowdsourcing, we did not expect the general public to have enough background knowledge nor to find enough people speaking the four Nguni languages. Using (linguistics) students would have been a possibility, but again the background knowledge was a reason for concern as well as the volume of data to annotate in the given time-frame. That was the main reason we decided to have linguistic experts perform the annotation of the corpora. | Hasa kwa ufafanuzi wa kimofolojia, maarifa ya kina ya kiisimu yanahitajika ili kufafanua kwa usahihi lugha zinazoshughulikiwa katika mradi huu. Kwa crowdsourcing, hatukutarajia umma kuwa na ujuzi wa kutosha wala kupata watu wa kutosha wanaozungumza lugha nne za Nguni. Kutumia wanafunzi (wa sayansi ya lugha) ingekuwa imewezekana, lakini tena maarifa ya msingi yalikuwa sababu ya wasiwasi pamoja na kiasi cha data cha kuainishwa katika muda uliopewa. Hiyo ndiyo ilikuwa sababu kuu tuliyoamua kuwa na wataalamu wa kiisimu wafanye ufafanuzi wa kopusi. |

Wakati wa ufafanuzi wa POS, wataalamu wa lugha pia walipewa uwezekano wa kusahihisha uchanganuzi wa kimofolojia ikiwa inahitajika. |
| For the lemmatisation task, the linguistic experts were given an alphabetical list where each unique lemma-morphological analysis combination was presented separately together with one sample context paragraph. | Kwa kazi ya ulemmatishaji, wataalamu wa lugha walipewa orodha ya kialfabeti ambapo kila mchanganyiko wa kipekee wa uchanganuzi wa lemma-kimofolojia uliwasilishwa kando pamoja na aya moja ya muktadha wa sampuli. |
| **6.1 Pre-annotation** | **6.1 Ufafanuzi wa awali** |
| As described in the annotation processing steps above, the data was automatically pre-annotated for each annotation task. Pre-annotation has been shown to speed up annotation (Lingren et al. 2014) as well as improve overall quality of the annotation (Rehbein et al. 2012). With morphologically complex languages such as the ones we are working with here, it is imperative to support the manual annotation process as much as possible (Puttkammer 2014). | Kama ilivyoelezwa katika hatua za uchakataji wa ufafanuzi hapo juu, data ilitolewa ufafanuzi wa awali kiotomatiki kwa kila kazi ya ufafanuzi. Ufafanuzi wa awali umeonyeshwa kuharakisha ufafanuzi (Lingren et al. 2014) na pia kuboresha ubora wa jumla wa ufafanuzi (Rehbein et al. 2012). Kwa lugha tata za kimaumbo kama zile tunazofanya kazi nazo hapa, ni muhimu kuunga mkono mchakato wa maelezo ya mikono iwezekanavyo (Puttkammer 2014). |
| Pre-annotation for morphology consisted in applying the NCHLT morphological decomposers (Eiselen & Puttkammer 2014) available for our four languages to generate all possible morphological analyses of the identified tokens. The linguistic experts could then choose the most accurate suggested analysis and make changes rather than annotate each token from scratch. | Ufafanuzi wa awali wa mofolojia ulihusisha kutumia vitenganishi vya kimofolojia vya NCHLT (Eiselen & Puttkammer 2014) vinavyopatikana kwa lugha zetu nne ili kuzalisha uchanganuzi wote unaowezekana wa kimofolojia wa tokeni zilizotambuliwa. Wataalamu wa lugha wangeweza kisha kuchagua uchanganuzi uliopendekezwa ulio sahihi zaidi na kufanya mabadiliko badala ya kufafanua kila tokeni kutoka mwanzo. |
| The statistics for the number of morphological analyses generated along with the average and maximum number of morphological analyses per token can be found in table 2. These numbers illustrate well the morphological complexity of the Bantu languages annotated in this project. Usually verbs will have the most generated possible analyses and closed class words like conjunctions will have only one analysis. Overall, the average number of morphological analyses is rather high. | Takwimu za idadi ya uchanganuzi wa kimofolojia uliozalishwa pamoja na wastani na idadi ya juu zaidi ya uchanganuzi wa kimofolojia kwa kila tokeni zinaweza kupatikana katika jedwali la 2. Nambari hizi zinaonyesha vizuri uchangamano wa kimofolojia wa Lugha za Kibantu zilizofafanuliwa katika mradi huu. Kwa kawaida vitenzi vitakuwa na uchanganuzi mwingi zaidi unaowezekana uliozalishwa na maneno ya tabaka funge kama viunganishi yatakuwa na uchanganuzi mmoja tu. Kwa ujumla, wastani wa idadi ya uchambuzi wa kimorfolojia ni wa juu kidogo. |
| For POS pre-annotation, a rule-based script produced a detailed POS tag per token using the morphological annotation as input. When possible errors in the morphological analysis were found, the token was either tagged as NERR (noun error), VERR (verb error), or ERR (general error). In these cases, the linguistic experts were asked to first correct the faulty morphological analysis and then add the correct POS tag. | Kwa ufafanuzi wa awali wa POS, hati iliyotegemea kanuni ilizalisha lebo ya kina ya POS kwa kila tokeni kwa kutumia ufafanuzi wa kimofolojia kama ingizo. Wakati makosa yanayowezekana katika uchanganuzi wa kimofolojia yalipopatikana, tokeni ilikuwa imewekwa lebo kama NERR (kosa la nomino), VERR (kosa la kitenzi), au ERR (kosa la jumla). Katika visa hivi, wataalamu wa isimu waliombwa kwanza kusahihisha uchanganuzi wenye kasoro wa kimofolojia na kisha kuongeza tagi sahihi ya POS. |
| Table 3 gives an overview of the generated POS error tags in our data. The statistics show that there were few morphological analyses that triggered error tags. This, however, does not imply that the rest of the generated POS tags were correct. | Jedwali la 3 linatoa muhtasari wa tagi za makosa za POS zilizozalishwa katika data yetu. Takwimu zinaonyesha kwamba kulikuwa na uchanganuzi mdogo wa kimofolojia uliosababisha tagi za makosa. Hata hivyo, hilo halimaanishi kwamba alama nyingine za POS zilizotokezwa zilikuwa sahihi. |
| For lemmatisation, we also used a rule-based script taking the morphological annotation as input, in combination with lookup tables for closed class tokens such as conjunctions, to produce the most likely stem lemma per token. As noted above, for the annotation of lemmas only unique combinations of a lemma and a morphological analysis were presented to the linguistic experts in alphabetical format. It also shows the reduction of tokens to annotate that was achieved by applying this strategy. | Kwa ulematishaji, pia tulitumia hati inayotegemea kanuni ikichukua ufafanuzi wa kimofolojia kama ingizo, pamoja na majedwali ya kuangalia kwa tokeni za tabaka funge kama vile viunganishi, ili kutoa lemma ya shina inayowezekana zaidi kwa kila tokeni. Kama ilivyobainishwa hapo juu, kwa ufafanuzi wa lemma ni michanganyiko ya kipekee tu ya lemma na uchanganuzi wa kimofolojia iliyowasilishwa kwa wataalamu wa isimu katika muundo wa kialfabeti. Jedwali la 4 lina idadi ya lemma zilizopaswa kukaguliwa kwa kila lugha. Pia inaonyesha upunguzaji wa tokeni za kufafanua uliopatikana kwa kutumia mkakati huu. |
| **6.2 Quality control** | **6.2 Udhibiti wa ubora** |
| Rigorous quality control (QC) has been carried out at various stages of the project. During the annotation of a type of linguistic information, QC was carried out to provide feedback to the linguistic experts, gather annotation as well as linguistic questions and resolve these issues in a structured, shared and documented way, for one language and also across languages. This process improved the quality of each type of linguistic annotation and made sure all linguistic experts applied the same rules and standards. | Udhibiti mkali wa ubora (QC) umefanyika katika hatua mbalimbali za mradi. Wakati wa uainishaji wa aina fulani ya habari za lugha, QC ilifanywa ili kutoa maoni kwa wataalamu wa lugha, kukusanya maswali ya uainishaji na ya lugha, na kutatua matatizo haya kwa njia iliyopangwa, ya pamoja na ya hati, kwa lugha moja na pia kati ya lugha. Mchakato huu uliboresha ubora wa kila aina ya maelezo ya lugha na kuhakikisha kwamba wataalamu wote wa lugha walitumia sheria na viwango sawa. |

| Once annotation on the data was finished for all linguistic levels, QC was done to ensure that there is a 99% agreement between the morphology, POS and lemma annotations. | **8 Conclusions and future work** | **8 Hitimisho na Kazi za Baadaye** |
| In the future, it would be interesting to collect data from more diverse sources and not just government text. Linguistically, a wider spread of genres will represent more types of real language use. Also, the core technologies developed on the basis of this data would be more generic. | Wakati ujao, lingekuwa jambo la kupendeza kukusanya habari kutoka kwa vyanzo mbalimbali na si maandishi ya serikali tu. Kilingvistiki, kuenea zaidi kwa aina za maandishi kutawakilisha aina zaidi za matumizi halisi ya lugha. Pia, teknolojia za msingi zilizotengenezwa kulingana na data hii zitakuwa za jumla zaidi. |
| We also learned that it is important to have quick feedback loops between the corrections done on an annotation layer and the QC carried out on the data. This helps to reach a good quality-level early in the annotation phase and minimizes the need for re-annotation and/or further correction of already annotated data. | Pia tulijifunza kuwa ni muhimu kuwa na mizunguko ya maoni ya haraka kati ya marekebisho yaliyofanywa kwenye tabaka la uainishaji na ukaguzi wa ubora (QC) uliofanywa kwenye data. Hii inasaidia kufikia kiwango kizuri cha ubora mapema katika awamu ya ufafanuzi na inapunguza hitaji la kufafanua upya na/au kurekebisha zaidi data iliyokwisha fafanuliwa. |
| One thing to point out is that it is very hard to find linguistic experts for South African languages who are available and qualified to do annotations for a project like ours. In the future, we might need to train new linguistic experts which will definitely influence the time-line as well as overall budgets of annotation projects. A conclusion to draw from this is that South African Bantu languages are not only under-resourced with regards to data, but that the development of human capital is also an important aspect of the development of resources for these languages. | Jambo moja la kutaja ni kwamba ni vigumu sana kupata wataalamu wa lugha kwa Lugha za Afrika Kusini ambao wanapatikana na wana sifa za kufanya ufafanuzi kwa mradi kama wetu. Katika siku zijazo, huenda tukahitaji kuwafunza wataalamu wapya wa lugha jambo ambalo kwa hakika litaathiri ratiba pamoja na bajeti za jumla za miradi ya ufafanuzi. Hitimisho la kuchukua kutokana na hili ni kwamba Lugha za Kibantu za Afrika Kusini sio tu kwamba hazina rasilimali za kutosha kuhusiana na data, bali pia maendeleo ya rasilimali watu ni kipengele muhimu cha ukuzaji wa rasilimali kwa lugha hizi. |
| Hopefully these parallel linguistically annotated corpora will prove interesting for researchers from different backgrounds and will help to gain more insight into the workings of these four Nguni languages, be it morphological processes, lemmatisation questions or syntactic structures. | Tunatumai kwamba hizi kumbukumbu za lugha zilizofafanuliwa kiisimu sambamba zitavutia watafiti kutoka asili tofauti na zitasaidia kupata ufahamu zaidi kuhusu utendaji kazi wa lugha hizi nne za Kinguni, iwe ni michakato ya kimofolojia, maswali ya ulematishaji au miundo ya kisintaksia. |
