| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Psycholinguistic research on isiXhosa and related Bantu languages is scarce. For research on lexical processing in particular, a prerequisite is data on lexical properties that impact word recognition, such as word frequency and neighbourhood density. This paper describes the derivation of these and related lexical statistics from a newly created 4.8-million-word isiXhosa corpus. It then reviews the potential applications of such a lexical database for research on language acquisition, language development, and language processing. The paper closes with recommendations for further work in this domain. | Utafiti wa kisaikolojia-lugha kuhusu isiXhosa na Lugha za Kibantu zinazohusiana ni haba. | isiXhosa is an agglutinating language, meaning that its words typically consist of multiple morphemes that are concatenated in a relatively transparent manner. It has two particular features that distinguish it from more commonly studied agglutinating languages such as Turkish, Basque, and Hungarian (see van de Velde et al. 2019 for discussion). Firstly, alongside suffixation, it makes widespread use of prefixation to produce morphologically complex words. Secondly, it has a rich grammatical gender or noun class system, whereby nouns are divided into 15 groups, with noun class agreement being marked on several syntactic constituents (e.g., verbs, adjectives/relatives, determiners). As language-specific properties of affixation (e.g., Boudelaa & Marslen-Wilson 2011) and grammatical gender (e.g., Colé, Pynte & Andriamamonjy 2003) are known to affect language processing, psycholinguistic examinations of isiXhosa and related languages have much to contribute to theories of lexical and morphological processing. Further, in terms of practical applications, an understanding of how language and literacy development proceeds in languages of this sort is indispensable in designing teaching and intervention materials for young learners (Pretorius 2019). | isiXhosa ni lugha ambatizi, ikimaanisha kwamba maneno yake kwa kawaida hujumuisha mofimu nyingi ambazo huambatanishwa kwa namna iliyo wazi kiasi. | **Frequency** | **Marudio** |
| Frequency is arguably the most important variable in studies of lexical processing (van Heuven et al. 2014), where more frequent words are processed more rapidly than less frequent words. This effect can be explained on the basis of lexical activation, whereby more frequently encountered words have higher resting activation levels and are thus accessed more quickly than their low-frequency counterparts. A word's frequency is calculated based on the number of its occurrences in a corpus. It can be expressed on the standardized Zipf frequency scale, where the lower half of the scale (1-3) represents low-frequency words and the upper half of the scale (4-6) represents high-frequency words (words with a Zipf frequency above 7 tend to be function words). Zipf frequency is calculated as log10 (frequency per million words) + 3 (van Heuven et al. 2014). | Marudio bila shaka ni kigezo muhimu zaidi katika tafiti za uchakataji wa kileksika (van Heuven et al. 2014), ambapo maneno yenye marudio zaidi huchakatwa kwa kasi zaidi kuliko maneno yenye marudio machache. Athari hii inaweza kuelezwa kwa msingi wa uamilishaji wa kileksika, ambapo maneno yanayokutana nayo mara nyingi zaidi yana viwango vya juu vya uamilishaji wa utulivu na hivyo hufikiwa kwa haraka zaidi kuliko maneno ya marudio ya chini yanayolingana nayo. Masafa ya neno yanahesabiwa kulingana na idadi ya matukio yake katika korasi. Inaweza kuonyeshwa kwenye kipimo sanifu cha mara kwa mara cha Zipf, ambapo nusu ya chini ya kipimo (1-3) inawakilisha maneno ya mara kwa mara ya chini na nusu ya juu ya kipimo (4-6) inawakilisha maneno ya mara kwa mara ya juu (maneno yenye mara kwa mara ya Zipf zaidi ya 7 huwa ni maneno ya kazi). Masafa ya Zipf yanahesabiwa kama log10 (masafa kwa kila milioni ya maneno) + 3 (van Heuven et al. 2014). |

| **Neighbourhood statistics** | **Takwimu za majirani** |
| A neighbour of a given word is any word that can be created by substituting, adding, or deleting a single letter (for example, the isiXhosa ubisi 'milk' has as neighbours ubusi 'honey ' and usisi 'sister' , among others). The neighbourhood density of a word is equal to the number of its neighbours. Neighbourhood density effects on lexical processing typically manifest as processing slowdowns for words with more neighbours (Andrews 1997), which is attributed to the fact that when recognizing a word with many neighbours, numerous candidate lexical items become activated and must consequently be inhibited for the correct item to be selected. A related variable that is also of importance is neighbourhood frequency, which is the average frequency of a given word's neighbours. Here, a word with high-frequency neighbours takes longer to recognize than a word with low-frequency neighbours (e.g., Brysbaert, Mandera & Keuleers 2018). | Jirani wa neno fulani ni neno lolote linaloweza kuundwa kwa kubadilisha, kuongeza, au kufuta herufi moja (kwa mfano, neno la isiXhosa ubisi 'maziwa' lina majirani ubusi 'asali' na usisi 'dada', miongoni mwa mengine). Msongamano wa ujirani wa neno ni sawa na idadi ya majirani zake. Athari za msongamano wa ujirani kwenye uchakataji wa kileksika kwa kawaida hujitokeza kama kupungua kwa kasi ya uchakataji kwa maneno yenye majirani wengi zaidi (Andrews 1997), jambo ambalo linahusishwa na ukweli kwamba wakati wa kutambua neno lenye majirani wengi, vipashio vingi vya kileksika vinavyowezekana huamilishwa na kwa hiyo lazima vizuiliwe ili kipashio sahihi kichaguliwe. Kigezo kinachohusiana ambacho pia ni muhimu ni marudio ya ujirani, ambayo ni wastani wa marudio ya majirani wa neno fulani. Hapa, neno lenye majirani wa masafa ya juu huchukua muda mrefu zaidi kutambuliwa kuliko neno lenye majirani wa masafa ya chini (k.m., Brysbaert, Mandera & Keuleers 2018). |

| **The corpus** | **Korasi** |
| The calculation of lexical statistics requires a sizeable corpus of contemporary language materials. The corpus used in this paper was created by combining the isiXhosa corpora provided in the Leipzig Corpora Collection (Goldhahn, Eckart & Quasthoff 2012) with a new corpus created by the author from the online isiXhosa newssite Isolezwe lesiXhosa. The Leipzig isiXhosa corpora consist of texts randomly collected from the web and Wikipedia and therefore cover a multitude of topics (see Goldhahn, Eckart & Quasthoff 2012 for discussion). The Isolezwe corpus, on the other hand, contains reports on general news, sports, entertainment, opinion, and agriculture. This corpus was created via web-scraping using the rvest package (version 1.0.2; Wickham 2021) in the R environment for statistical computing (version 4.2.1; R Core Team 2022). The entire history of articles that was available from the site's inception (26 June 2015) up until 24 June 2022 was scraped. | Hesabu ya takwimu za kileksika inahitaji korasi kubwa ya nyenzo za lugha za kisasa. | **Applications** | **Matumizi** |
| A database of lexical statistics such as that described in this paper has numerous applications for research on lexical processing, which requires careful control of word-level properties such as frequency and neighbourhood density. There is, for example, significant interest in whether recognition of morphologically complex words takes place at the whole-word level or whether it entails breaking a word down into its constituent morphemes. To the best of the author's knowledge, only one study has investigated this question in relation to a Bantu language (Setswana; Ciaccio, Kgolo & Clahsen 2020). The extent to which morphosyntactic processing differs across first- and second-language speakers of a language is also theoretically important and critically understudied in relation to Bantu languages (Spinner 2011). | Hifadhidata ya takwimu za kileksika kama ile iliyoelezwa katika makala haya ina matumizi mengi kwa utafiti wa uchakataji wa kileksika, ambao unahitaji udhibiti makini wa sifa za kiwango cha neno kama vile marudio na msongamano wa ujirani. Kuna, kwa mfano, hamu kubwa katika kujua kama utambuzi wa maneno yenye mofimu changamano unafanyika katika kiwango cha neno zima au kama unahusisha kugawanya neno katika mofimu zake za msingi. Kwa ufahamu wa mwandishi, ni utafiti mmoja tu ambao umechunguza swali hili kuhusiana na lugha ya Kibantu (Setswana; Ciaccio, Kgolo & Clahsen 2020). Kiwango ambacho uchakataji wa kimofosintaksia hutofautiana kati ya wazungumzaji wa lugha ya kwanza na wa lugha ya pili wa lugha fulani pia ni muhimu kinadharia na hakijafanyiwa utafiti wa kutosha kuhusiana na Lugha za Kibantu (Spinner 2011). |

| **Limitations and suggestions for further work** | **Vikwazo na mapendekezo kwa kazi zaidi** |
| There are several additional steps that can be taken to improve on and expand the database presented in this paper. For one, after compiling the corpus and before processing it to derive lexical statistics, it would be desirable to cross-reference the corpus word list with a word list from an official isiXhosa dictionary. This cross-referencing process would enable misspellings to be filtered out from the corpus, thus reducing the number of spurious neighbours identified, and also facilitate the removal of non-isiXhosa words. At the time of writing, no such dictionary word list could be obtained in a digital format, and so this step has not yet been taken. | Kuna hatua kadhaa za ziada zinazoweza kuchukuliwa ili kuboresha na kupanua hifadhidata iliyowasilishwa katika makala hii. Kwanza, baada ya kukusanya korasi na kabla ya kuichakata ili kupata takwimu za kileksika, ingependeza kuoanisha orodha ya maneno ya korasi na orodha ya maneno kutoka kamusi rasmi ya isiXhosa. Mchakato huu wa kuoanisha mtambuka ungewezesha makosa ya tahajia kuchujwa kutoka kwenye korasi, hivyo kupunguza idadi ya majirani bandia wanaotambuliwa, na pia kurahisisha uondoaji wa maneno yasiyo ya Kixhosa. Wakati wa kuandika, hakuna orodha ya maneno ya kamusi kama hiyo iliyoweza kupatikana katika muundo wa kidijitali, na kwa hiyo hatua hii bado haijachukuliwa. |
| Another notable consideration is that all of the above calculations were based on word forms rather than roots or lemmas. This means that instead of, for example, -lala being treated as a lemma that surfaces in ukulala, uyalala, siyalala, and so forth, each of these word forms is treated as an individual item. This can also lead to inflation of neighbourhood density (however, the words affected by this issue - most notably, verbs - will tend to have their neighbourhood density inflated to the same extent). It remains an empirical question whether it is properties of the word form or the lemma that are better predictors of, for example, word recognition latency in languages such as isiXhosa. In order to address this question, lemmas could be obtained from corpus data using a morphological analyzer (e.g., du Toit & Puttkammer 2021). | Jambo lingine muhimu la kuzingatia ni kwamba hesabu zote zilizo hapo juu ziliegemezwa kwenye maumbo ya maneno badala ya mizizi au lemma. Hii ina maana kwamba badala ya, kwa mfano, -lala kuchukuliwa kama lemma inayojitokeza katika ukulala, uyalala, siyalala, na kadhalika, kila moja ya maumbo haya ya maneno huchukuliwa kama kipengele cha mtu binafsi. Hii inaweza pia kusababisha mfumuko wa msongamano wa ujirani (hata hivyo, maneno yaliyoathiriwa na suala hili - hasa, vitenzi - yataelekea kuwa na msongamano wao wa ujirani uliofumuka kwa kiwango sawa). Inabaki kuwa swali la kiuchunguzi ikiwa ni sifa za umbo la neno au za lemma ambazo ni vitabiri bora vya, kwa mfano, muda wa utambuzi wa neno katika lugha kama vile isiXhosa. Ili kushughulikia swali hili, lemma zinaweza kupatikana kutoka kwenye data ya korasi kwa kutumia kichanganuzi cha kimofolojia (k.m., du Toit & Puttkammer 2021). |
| Lastly, the work presented here could also be expanded by deriving phonological statistics for isiXhosa, such as syllable number and phonological neighbourhood density. Such statistics can be obtained via LexiCAL if each word entry is paired with a phonetic transcription and would enable research on spoken word processing in the language. | Mwisho, kazi iliyowasilishwa hapa inaweza pia kupanuliwa kwa kupata takwimu za kifonolojia za Kixhosa, kama vile idadi ya silabi na msongamano wa ujirani wa kifonolojia. Takwimu kama hizo zinaweza kupatikana kupitia LexiCAL ikiwa kila ingizo la neno limeoanishwa na unukuzi wa kifonetiki na zingewezesha utafiti kuhusu uchakataji wa maneno ya kusemwa katika lugha hiyo. |
| **Conclusion** | **Hitimisho** |
| Psycholinguistic techniques that can capture language processing as it unfolds in real time have yet to be leveraged to examine the processing of isiXhosa and related languages. This paper has discussed one kind of resource - a database of lexical statistics on the language, compiled based on a large-scale corpus - that is necessary to address this research gap and realize the considerable theoretical and practical benefits of doing so. Future collaboration between (computational) linguists and language specialists will allow for the refinement of this resource and the creation of others. | Mbinu za saikolojia-lugha ambazo zinaweza kunasa uchakataji wa lugha unapojitokeza kwa wakati halisi bado hazijatumiwa kuchunguza uchakataji wa isiXhosa na lugha zinazohusiana. Makala hii imejadili aina moja ya rasilimali - hifadhidata ya takwimu za kileksika kuhusu lugha, iliyokusanywa kwa msingi wa korasi ya kiwango kikubwa - ambayo ni muhimu kushughulikia pengo hili la utafiti na kutambua manufaa makubwa ya kinadharia na ya kivitendo ya kufanya hivyo. Ushirikiano wa baadaye kati ya wanaisimu (wa kikompyuta) na wataalamu wa lugha utaruhusu uboreshaji wa rasilimali hii na uundaji wa nyingine. |
