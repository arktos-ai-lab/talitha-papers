| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| This paper describes voicing in Ngamambo, a semi Grassfields Bantu language in the North West Region of Cameroon. The language is classified under the Momo sub-language family (Eberhard, David M., Gray F. Simons and Charles D. Fenning, 2020). Ngamambo is unwritten, and research on the language is scanty. The only available literature on the language is by Asongwed & Hyman (1976)), Achiri-Taboh (2014) and Lem Atanga (2020) However, there has been some recent attempt by the Mbu Language Committee (MLC) to study the language. Interest in the study of Ngamambo stems from the imperative of undertaking a comprehensive description of the language. Preliminary research has revealed the existence of voicing in the language. Voicing is a process whereby the pronunciation of a word is influenced by one of the sounds. Data was obtained from Ngamambo native speakers (informants) over six months. The originality of this study resides in the fact that very little research has been carried out on the language. The authors of this paper discuss one aspect of the language and hope that subsequent studies will determine if voicing is also present in other Grassfields languages, especially the Momo sub-language family. The phonological process of voicing in Ngamambo has been observed when a voiceless sound becomes voiced depending on the environment. It is hoped that understanding this phenomenon would lead to a better understanding of voicing related to language learning. | Makala hii inaelezea ughuna katika Ngamambo, lugha nusu ya Bantu ya Grassfields katika Mkoa wa Kaskazini Magharibi wa Kamerun. | **1.1 The concept of voicing** | **1.1 Dhana ya utoaji sauti** |
| In phonology, voicing (or sonorisation) is a sound change where a voiceless consonant becomes voiced due to the influence of its phonological environment (Consonant voicing and devoicing, 2022). Voicing occurs when the larynx 's adductor muscles close the larynx, enabling the inner edges of the vocal cords to be in light contact. Pressure from the pulmonic airstream builds up, and subglottal pressure rises. As an effect, the vocal cords are blown slightly apart, and the compressed air below the glottis then flows through the narrow gaps in the larynx at a very high speed due to high subglottal pressure. | Katika fonolojia, ughuna (au usonorishaji) ni mabadiliko ya sauti ambapo konsonanti isiyo na ghuna inakuwa na ghuna kutokana na ushawishi wa mazingira yake ya kifonolojia (Consonant voicing and devoicing, 2022). Ughuna hutokea wakati misuli ya kufunga koo ya zoloto inapofunga zoloto, na hivyo kuwezesha kingo za ndani za nyuzi za sauti kuwa katika mgusano mwepesi. Shinikizo kutoka kwa mkondo wa hewa wa mapafu hujenga, na shinikizo la chini ya glottisi hupanda. Kama athari, nyuzi za sauti hupigwa kidogo na kutengana, na hewa iliyobanwa chini ya glottisi kisha hupita kupitia mianya nyembamba katika koo kwa kasi kubwa sana kutokana na shinikizo kubwa la chini ya glottisi. |
| Voicing in sonorant consonants involves the escape of air freely through the oral and the nasal cavities, whereas voicing in obstruent involves the passage of air from the oral cavity partially or fully blocked, leading to a rapid build-up of supraglottal air pressure (Grijzenhout 2000, Dmitrieva 2014). | Ughuna katika konsonanti za sonoranti unahusisha kutoka kwa hewa kwa uhuru kupitia tundu la mdomo na la pua, ambapo ughuna katika obstruenti unahusisha upitaji wa hewa kutoka tundu la mdomo ukiwa umezuiwa kwa sehemu au kabisa, na kusababisha mrundiko wa haraka wa shinikizo la hewa la juu ya glottisi (Grijzenhout 2000, Dmitrieva 2014). |
| Stop consonants are produced by a complete closure in the vocal tract. The complete oral closure in oral stop is combined with a velic closure that prevents the air from escaping through the nasal cavity. As such, there is a rise in intra-oral pressure so that, when the closure is released, the compressed air escapes to the atmosphere with a stop burst, and pressure falls rapidly. According to Warren (1996), voiceless oral plosives have a volume of air of approximately 50ml. Also, voiced stops are always shorter than voiceless stops, and if voiced stops were as long as voiceless ones, then voicing would die out, since the subglottal and oral pressure would equalise. In summary, for voicing to take place, the vocal folds have to be in contact, and there has to be a sufficient pressure drop across the glottis. | Konsonanti za vizuizi hutolewa kwa kufungwa kabisa katika njia ya sauti. Kufungwa kabisa kwa mdomo katika kizuizi cha mdomo kunachanganywa na kufungwa kwa veli ambako kunazuia hewa kutoka kupitia tundu la pua. Kwa hivyo, kuna kupanda kwa shinikizo la ndani ya mdomo ili, wakati kufungwa kunapofunguliwa, hewa iliyobanwa inatoka kwenda angani kwa mlipuko wa kizuizi, na shinikizo hushuka haraka. Kulingana na Warren (1996), vipasuo visivyo na sauti vya mdomo vina kiasi cha hewa cha takriban 50ml. Pia, vizuizi vyenye sauti huwa vifupi kila mara kuliko vizuizi visivyo na sauti, na kama vizuizi vyenye sauti vingekuwa virefu kama vile visivyo na sauti, basi utoaji sauti ungekufa, kwa kuwa shinikizo la chini ya glota na la mdomo lingesawazika. Kwa muhtasari, ili utoaji sauti ufanyike, mikunjo ya sauti lazima iwe katika mgusano, na lazima kuwe na shinikizo la kutosha kushuka kwenye glota. |

Different commands are given to the glottis to produce segments with different phonological specifications for voicing in running speech. In Ngamambo, we observe that a voiceless consonant becomes voiced at the word boundary. This process seems to be part of the native speaker ´ s intuition of the grammar of the language. For instance, in the "infinitive" form of the verb, the final segment is voiceless, and when, for example it is conjugated to mark tense, the segment becomes voiced at the word boundary. | **Devoicing** | **Kupoteza ughuna** |
| While this paper is on voicing in Ngamambo, it is important to point out that there are instances of devoicing in the language. | **1.5 Conclusion** | **1.5 Hitimisho** |
| This paper has attempted to describe the phonological phenomenon of voicing in Ngamambo. It has analysed the manifestation and trigger of the locus of voicing in the language. The study establishes that word-final voiceless consonants become voiced at a word boundary. Furthermore, the effect of the voicing is manifested in the quality of the vowels that precede the consonants. | Kazi hii imejaribu kuelezea hali ya kifonolojia ya ughuna katika Ngamambo. Imechanganua udhihirishaji na kichochezi cha mahali pa ughuna katika lugha hiyo. Utafiti unathibitisha kwamba konsonanti zisizo na ghuna zilizo mwishoni mwa neno huwa ghuna kwenye mpaka wa neno.
