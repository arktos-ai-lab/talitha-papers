| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Whilst lemmatisation in Zulu (and cognate languages) remains to this day a partially unsettled issue, with dictionaries, both bilingual and monolingual, adopting differing strategies in respect of nouns, we wish to focus here on a somewhat minute aspect of the matter: how to lemmatize verbs with optional initial vowels. As the variation occurs at the initial, it is crucial that it receives proper dictionary treatment, lest the user be misdirected or misinformed. | Ingawa ulematishaji katika Kizulu (na lugha jamaa) hadi leo bado ni suala ambalo halijatatuliwa kikamilifu, huku kamusi, za lugha mbili na za lugha moja, zikichukua mikakati tofauti kuhusu nomino, tunataka kukazia hapa kipengele kidogo cha suala hili: jinsi ya kulematisha vitenzi vyenye irabu za mwanzo za hiari. Kwa kuwa tofauti hiyo hutokea mwanzoni, ni muhimu kwamba ipate ushughulikiaji sahihi wa kamusi, isije ikampotosha mtumiaji au kumpa taarifa potofu. |

| **- Zulu monolingual** | **- Kizulu cha lugha moja** |
| Nyembezi and Mbatha provide almost similar descriptions for each variant, each treated as a main entry (see -dlula, -edlula inter alia), occasionally indicating the existence of a variant assimilated to a synonym like in the -hlula and -phula series. Even if variants are somehow synonyms, it would make sense in a linguistic work to discriminate. And that does not preclude inconsistencies: one of Mbatha's examples under dlula is ukwedlula ngendlu yakhiwa not to offer assistance to people working, which should appear under -edlula. | **4 A pragmatic approach** | **4 Mtazamo wa kipragmatiki** |
| Our bidirectional French / Zulu lexicon (Lafon and Mongezi 2022) has no pretention to be extensive but rather a handy support whilst one is engaged in conversation. Space and cost were huge concerns. Still we aimed at covering as much vocabulary as possible whilst attempting to remain consistent throughout. | Kamusi yetu ya Kifaransa / Kizulu ya pande mbili (Lafon na Mongezi 2022) haina dhamira ya kuwa ya kina, bali ni msaada rahisi wakati mtu anashiriki katika mazungumzo. Nafasi na gharama zilikuwa shida kubwa. Bado tulilenga kufunika msamiati mwingi iwezekanavyo huku tukijaribu kubaki thabiti kote. |
| In the French to Zulu part, we opted to place the possible vowel within brackets, so as to offer a maximum of information in a minimum of space, treating i-commencing verbs in a similar fashion: | Katika sehemu ya Kifaransa kwenda Kizulu, tulichagua kuweka irabu inayowezekana ndani ya mabano, ili kutoa kiwango cha juu cha habari katika nafasi ndogo, tukishughulikia vitenzi vinavyoanza na i kwa namna sawa: |
| passer (to pass): -(e)dlula; aller (to come): -(i)za | passer (to pass): -(e)dlula; aller (to come): -(i)za |
| Thus all variants of conjugated forms can be deducted from the lemmas as they stand. | Kwa hivyo vibadala vyote vya maumbo yaliyonyambulishwa vinaweza kutolewa kutoka kwa lemma kama zilivyo. |
| Obviously the same strategy could not be followed in the Zulu to French part. In order to avoid repetition as well as omission, we opted to lemmatize the form which appeared to us the more common (based on native speaker's intuition), the other or possibly other form(s) being however systematically listed and crossreferred to the one chosen as lemma : | Ni wazi kwamba mkakati huo huo haungeweza kufuatwa katika sehemu ya Kizulu kwenda Kifaransa. Ili kuepuka marudio na pia kuachwa kwa maumbo, tuliamua kulematisha umbo ambalo lilionekana kwetu kuwa la kawaida zaidi (kwa kuzingatia hisia za mzungumzaji asilia), umbo lingine au pengine maumbo mengine yakiorodheshwa kwa utaratibu na kurejelewa mtambuka kwa lile lililochaguliwa kuwa lemma: |
| -edlula : voir [see] -dlula | -edlula : angalia [see] -dlula |
| -iza : voir [see] -za | -iza : angalia [see] -za |
| This strategy counterbalanced at least partially any wrong estimate on our side of the relative frequency of variants. The strategy obviously is not ignored by all of the dictionaries reviewed; 17 rather it is not applied systematically. | Mkakati huu ulisawazisha angalau kwa sehemu makadirio yoyote mabaya ya upande wetu ya marudio linganishi ya vibadala. Mkakati huu kwa wazi haupuuzwi na kamusi zote zilizopitiwa; 17 badala yake hautumiwi kwa utaratibu. |
| **5 Conclusion** | **5 Hitimisho** |
| It would seem, from the review of lexicographical treatment of a few items in old as well as recent Zulu dictionaries, both bilingual and monolingual, traditional as well as recent and corpus- and frequencybased (de Schryver's), that the twin issues of "vowel verbs with variant non-vowel forms" and "latent-vowel verbs" have been largely overlooked. Minor as this may seem, it remains a blind spot. Our ad hoc solution, to consistently include all variants in the simplest and less cumbersome manner, might then go some way towards achieving better coverage in that particular instance. This reminds us that there is no Holy Grail to achieving consistency and userfriendliness in the lexicography of Zulu and cognate languages. Lexicographical issues have to be considered taking into account their specifics, this being the only manner to provide adequate, complete and easily accessible information. | Ingeonekana, kutokana na mapitio ya ushughulikiaji wa kileksikografia wa vipengee vichache katika kamusi za Kizulu za zamani na pia za hivi karibuni, za lugha mbili na za lugha moja, za kimapokeo na pia za hivi karibuni na zinazotegemea korasi na marudio (za de Schryver), kwamba masuala pacha ya "vitenzi vya irabu vyenye maumbo vibadala visivyo na irabu" na "vitenzi vya irabu fiche" yamepuuzwa kwa kiasi kikubwa. Japo hili laweza kuonekana dogo, linabaki kuwa eneo lisiloonekana. Suluhisho letu la dharura, la kujumuisha vibadala vyote kwa uthabiti kwa namna rahisi na isiyo na uzito zaidi, huenda likasaidia kufikia ufunikaji bora katika kisa hicho husika. Hii inatukumbusha kwamba hakuna kikombe kitakatifu cha kufikia uthabiti na urahisi wa matumizi katika leksikografia ya Kizulu na lugha zinazofanana. Masuala ya leksikografia yanapaswa kuzingatiwa kwa kutilia maanani mahususi yake, hii ikiwa ndiyo njia pekee ya kutoa taarifa stahiki, kamili na inayopatikana kwa urahisi. |
