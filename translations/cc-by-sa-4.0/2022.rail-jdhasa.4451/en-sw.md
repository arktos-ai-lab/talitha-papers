| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Currently, machine learning is presented as the ultimate solution for language technology regardless of use case and application, however, it requires as a starting point a massive amount of curated linguistic data in electronic form that is expected to be high quality and representative of the kind of language usage that the tools will follow. For minority and indigenous languages, this can be an insurmountable task, as digital materials of the necessary sizes do not exist and can not easily be produced. In this article we present an approach we have successfully used for supporting indigenous languages to survive and grow in digital contexts for years, and describe the potential of our approach for African contexts. Our technological solution is a free and open-source infrastructure that enables language experts and users to cooperate on creating linguistic resources like dictionaries and grammatical descriptions. In addition we provide language-independent frameworks to build these into applications that are needed by the language community. | Kwa sasa, ujifunzaji wa mashine unawasilishwa kama suluhisho kuu la teknolojia ya lugha bila kujali kisa cha matumizi na programu, hata hivyo, unahitaji kama hatua ya kuanzia kiasi kikubwa cha data ya kiisimu iliyoratibiwa katika umbo la kielektroniki ambayo inatarajiwa kuwa ya ubora wa juu na uwakilishi wa aina ya matumizi ya lugha ambayo zana zitafuata. Kwa lugha za wachache na za kiasili, hili linaweza kuwa kazi isiyowezekana, kwani nyenzo za kidijitali za ukubwa unaohitajika hazipo na haziwezi kutayarishwa kwa urahisi. Katika makala haya tunawasilisha mbinu ambayo tumeitumia kwa mafanikio kwa miaka mingi kusaidia lugha za kiasili kuishi na kukua katika mazingira ya kidijitali, na tunaelezea uwezo wa mbinu yetu kwa mazingira ya Kiafrika. | **Introduction** | **Utangulizi** |
| Machine learning (ML) approaches have dominated Natural Language Processing (NLP) during the last two decades. They typically require a big amount of noise-free data, which does not exist for 99% of the 7,000 world's languages, either due to the small number of writers or a young written norm. Even if a small language community like the Inari Sámi one in Finland is exceptionally productive in text writing (10,533 words/speaker as opposed to 1,440 words/speaker for Swedish) the amount of text recommended for regular machine learning approaches ("A 3.4 billion word text corpus was used for the original BERT-Large, so it is worth training with a data set of this size."[1]) cannot be reached. The same counts for language communities with many speakers but with a low degree of literacy in this language or simply missing written language domains or a bilingual context where the majority language only is used as a written language. | Mbinu za ujifunzaji wa mashine (ML) zimetawala uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP) katika miongo miwili iliyopita. Kwa kawaida huhitaji kiasi kikubwa cha data isiyo na kelele, ambayo haipo kwa asilimia 99 ya lugha 7,000 za dunia, ama kwa sababu ya idadi ndogo ya waandishi au kwa sababu ya kanuni za kisasa za kuandika. Hata kama jumuiya ndogo ya lugha kama ile ya Inari Sámi nchini Finland ina tija ya kipekee katika uandishi wa maandishi (maneno 10,533 kwa kila mzungumzaji ikilinganishwa na maneno 1,440 kwa kila mzungumzaji kwa Kiswidi) kiasi cha maandishi kinachopendekezwa kwa mbinu za kawaida za ujifunzaji wa mashine ("Korasi ya maandishi ya maneno bilioni 3.4 ilitumika kwa BERT-Large ya awali, kwa hivyo inafaa kufunza kwa seti ya data ya ukubwa huu."[1]) hakiwezi kufikiwa. Vile vile hutumika kwa jumuiya za lugha zenye wazungumzaji wengi lakini kiwango cha chini cha ujuzi wa kusoma na kuandika katika lugha hii au kukosa tu nyanja za maandishi za lugha au muktadha wa lugha mbili ambapo lugha ya wengi pekee ndiyo inayotumika kama lugha ya maandishi. |
| In this article, we present óur infrastructure - GiellaLT - presently hosting language models for more than 130 languages and building a number of language technology tools that are useful for any language community. GiellaLT is based on an alternative to corpus-based language technology - knowledge-based, also known as rule-based, language technology. The technology is chosen for its ability to support languages with no earlier digital presence. As long as a project has access to a native speaker and a linguist, maybe even in one and the same person, useful tools can be made. Digital resources are always welcome and will help speed up the development work, but they are not a requirement. | Katika makala hii, tunawasilisha miundombinu yetu - GiellaLT - ambayo kwa sasa inashikilia miundo ya lugha kwa zaidi ya lugha 130 na kujenga idadi ya zana za teknolojia ya lugha ambazo ni muhimu kwa jumuiya yoyote ya lugha. GiellaLT inategemea mbadala wa teknolojia ya lugha inayotegemea korasi - teknolojia ya lugha inayotegemea maarifa, inayojulikana pia kama inayotegemea kanuni. Teknolojia hiyo imechaguliwa kwa uwezo wake wa kusaidia lugha zisizo na uwepo wowote wa awali wa kidijitali. Mradi unapokuwa na ufikiaji wa mzungumzaji asilia na mwanaisimu, labda hata katika mtu mmoja yuleyule, zana muhimu zinaweza kutengenezwa. Rasilimali za kidijitali zinakaribishwa daima na zitasaidia kuharakisha kazi ya maendeleo, lakini si hitaji. |

| The GiellaLT infrastructure provides all the building blocks to get started on the language work right away, based on open-source technologies and solutions. There is proven integration with most existing systems and platforms, saving huge amounts of time, money, and resources for newcomers. This is especially important for many indigenous language communities, which would not have the resources to develop underlying technologies and integration solutions on their own. By separating language-independent parts from language-specific ones, the cost of developing the language-independent parts can be shared by everyone, or be carried by communities with enough resources. Being open source we are also maintaining an approach that empowers the language communities in a way that retains their ownership of the language and linguistic data they are working on. In the free open-source model, there is no large danger that someone working on the language models simply acquires the linguistic data from the community for free in order to sell it back to them at a higher price. | Miundombinu ya GiellaLT inatoa vipengele vyote vya msingi vya kuanza kazi ya lugha mara moja, kwa kuzingatia teknolojia na suluhisho za Programu huria. | One final consideration that our rule-based approach has over the machine-learnt models is one of efficiency: a neural language model has to be trained on a system with at least one GPU for over a period of days or weeks and should probably also run on similar hardware, or accessed over high-speed internet. | **GiellaLT - A multilingual infrastructure for everyone** | **GiellaLT - Miundombinu ya lugha nyingi kwa kila mtu** |
| The foundation for the work presented in this article is the multilingual infrastructure GiellaLT, which includes numerous languages that have little or no data, a rare case in the NLP world. Everything produced in the GiellaLT infrastructure is under free and open licences and freely available. The corpora are available with free licensing where possible. The infrastructure is split code-wise into three GitHub organisations: GiellaLT containing the language data for each language, Divvun containing language-independent code for the infrastructure, and Giellatekno for corpus infrastructure. End user tools served by the Divvun group are at divvun.no & divvun.org, and tools served by the Giellatekno group at giellatekno.uit.no, both at UiT-Norway's Arctic University. | Msingi wa kazi iliyowasilishwa katika makala haya ni miundombinu ya lugha nyingi ya GiellaLT, ambayo inajumuisha lugha nyingi ambazo zina data chache au hazina kabisa, hali adimu katika ulimwengu wa NLP. Kila kitu kinachozalishwa katika miundombinu ya GiellaLT kiko chini ya leseni huria na wazi na kinapatikana kwa uhuru. Korasi zinapatikana kwa leseni huria pale inapowezekana. Miundombinu imegawanywa kwa mtazamo wa msimbo katika mashirika matatu ya GitHub: GiellaLT yenye data za lugha kwa kila lugha, Divvun yenye msimbo usiotegemea lugha kwa ajili ya miundombinu, na Giellatekno kwa ajili ya miundombinu ya korasi. Zana za mtumiaji wa mwisho zinazotolewa na kikundi cha Divvun ziko divvun.no & divvun.org, na zana zinazotolewa na kikundi cha Giellatekno ziko giellatekno.uit.no, zote zikiwa katika Chuo Kikuu cha Aktiki cha UiT-Norway. |

An existing grammatical description is also of tremendous help, but not a requirement. | Sharti la msingi la kuunda zana za lugha katika GiellaLT ni tahajia ambayo ama imefafanuliwa mapema au inafafanuliwa wakati wa kutengeneza zana za lugha. The current system for testing is a combination of our long-term investment in testing within the infrastructure locally for developers-combined with modern automatic testing currently supplied by GitHub actions. | Kazi inayotegemea lugha hufanywa ndani ya miundombinu katika hazina mahususi za lugha, vipengele na masasisho yasiyotegemea lugha ambayo yanafaa kwa lugha zote huunganishwa kwa njia ya nusu otomatiki kadri yanavyotengenezwa. | **GiellaLT for African languages** | **GiellaLT kwa Lugha za Afrika** |

| Languages for which Microsoft and Google do not make language technology solutions are vulnerable to technological changes. Closed source programs for such languages run the risk of becoming unusable as word processors or operative system change. Companies behand these programs may then either go bankrupt or change their focus to other areas. Being closed source, the work behind these solutions is then lost. The GiellaTL solution to this is to keep both the linguistic software and the software needed for integrating it in various applications as open source. This means that scarce resources may be reused, without the risk of loosing work. Open access to language independent software also makes it easier to build solutions for lesserused languages. | Lugha ambazo Microsoft na Google hawatengenezei suluhu za teknolojia ya lugha ziko hatarini kwa mabadiliko ya kiteknolojia. This holds most notably for Khoisan and (most) Bantu languages in the south and east as well as Afro-Asiatic languages in the northeast. Lugha nyingi za Afrika pia zina miundo changamano ya kimofolojia. Hii inahusu hasa lugha za Khoisan na Lugha za Kibantu (nyingi) kusini na mashariki pamoja na lugha za Kiafrika-Kiasia kaskazini-mashariki. Lugha kama hizo, zikiwa na mofolojia changamano na rasilimali chache au hakuna za maandishi, zitakuwa tatizo kwa teknolojia ya lugha ya kawaida. |
| Not much lnguage technology has been developed for these languages. The main exceptions are the works by Arvi Hurskainen on Swahili (for an overviow and references, see Hurskainen (2018)) and the works by Laurette Pretorius, Sonja Bosch and others for Zulu, Xhosa and other South African languages (e.g. Pretorius & Bosch (2009a), Pretorius & Bosch (2009b)). | Teknolojia ndogo ya lugha imetengenezwa kwa lugha hizi. Vighairi vikuu ni kazi za Arvi Hurskainen kuhusu Kiswahili (kwa muhtasari na marejeleo, ona Hurskainen (2018)) na kazi za Laurette Pretorius, Sonja Bosch na wengine kwa Kizulu, Kixhosa na Lugha nyingine za Afrika Kusini (k.m. Pretorius & Bosch (2009a), Pretorius & Bosch (2009b)). |
| The GiellaLT infrastructure was developed to deal with this situation. The challenge of making language technology for complex and marginalised circumpolar languages was solved by representing the morphological structure as finite state transducers. Morphological analysers are the core of our language technology tools, they are written in form of Finite State Morphology Beesley & Karttunen (2003), Lindén et al. (2013). This means in practice that language modelling is based on dictionaries and hand-written rules for morphotactics as well as syntactic and semantic processing as needed, e.g. with Constraint Grammar Karlsson (1990), Didriksen (2010). While writing rule-based models for language processing is contemporarily written off as too slow and labour-intensive, in our experience full-time work on the dictionary and morphotactic rules for three months is enough to create high-coverage usable language models. If one compares this to the work it takes to create and curate corpora for machine learning, three months does not get one very far in the creation of gigaword corpora. | Miundombinu ya GiellaLT ilitengenezwa ili kukabiliana na hali hii. Changamoto ya kutengeneza teknolojia ya lugha kwa lugha changamano na zilizotengwa za ncha ya kaskazini ilitatuliwa kwa kuwakilisha muundo wa kimofolojia kama transducers za hali finiti. Vichanganuzi vya kimofolojia ndio kiini cha zana zetu za teknolojia ya lugha, vimeandikwa kwa namna ya Mofolojia ya Hali Finiti Beesley & Karttunen (2003), Lindén et al. (2013). Hii inamaanisha kiutendaji kwamba uundaji wa modeli za lugha unategemea kamusi na sheria zilizoandikwa kwa mkono za mofotaktiki pamoja na uchakataji wa kisintaksia na kisemantiki inapohitajika, k.mf. kwa Sarufi ya Vikwazo Karlsson (1990), Didriksen (2010). Ingawa uandikaji wa modeli zinazotegemea sheria kwa ajili ya uchakataji wa lugha kwa sasa unapuuzwa kuwa wa polepole mno na unaohitaji nguvu kazi kubwa, kwa uzoefu wetu kazi ya muda wote kwenye kamusi na sheria za mofotaktiki kwa miezi mitatu inatosha kuunda modeli za lugha zinazoweza kutumika zenye ufunikaji wa juu. Ikiwa mtu analinganisha hili na kazi inayohitajika kuunda na kusimamia korasi za ujifunzaji wa mashine, miezi mitatu haimfikishi mtu mbali sana katika uundaji wa korasi za gigaword. |
| The main language families treated in the GiellaLT infrastructure are Uralic, Algonquin, EskimoAleut and various Siberian languages. As for African languages, we have so far only experimented with eight of them[2]. The Somali language model is far beyond alpha level, it contains almost 16000 stems and the core morphology. The other language models are all relatively small, but in some cases, they still give an impression of how central morphophonological challenges may be solved. | Familia kuu za lugha zinazoshughulikiwa katika miundombinu ya GiellaLT ni Uralic, Algonquin, EskimoAleut na lugha mbalimbali za Siberia. Modeli nyingine za lugha zote ni ndogo kiasi, lakini katika baadhi ya matukio, bado zinatoa taswira ya jinsi changamoto kuu za kimofofonolojia zinavyoweza kutatuliwa. |

| Proofing tools are a crucial piece of software to support normative language writing, i.e. spelling and grammar checkers and correctors. | **Spellers** | **Vikagua tahajia** |
| A speller consists of essentially two parts: a morphological dictionary that contains the information as to whether a given word is part of the language or not. It is assumed by spell-checking that words not in the collected dictionary are misspellings of real words. The second part is an error model that takes the unknown word user inputted and tries to find similar words that are found in the language. | Kikagua tahajia kinajumuisha sehemu mbili kimsingi: kamusi ya kimofolojia iliyo na taarifa kuhusu kama neno fulani ni sehemu ya lugha au la. Inachukuliwa na kikagua tahajia kwamba maneno yasiyomo katika kamusi iliyokusanywa ni makosa ya tahajia ya maneno halisi. Sehemu ya pili ni kielelezo cha makosa kinachochukua neno lisilojulikana lililoingizwa na mtumiaji na kujaribu kutafuta maneno yanayofanana yanayopatikana katika lugha. |
| Technologies similar to the ones presented hare have been applied for African languages as well. One example is Bosch & Eiselen (2012), for Zulu, another is the Swahili speller distributed by the Finnish company Lingsoft. | Teknolojia zinazofanana na zile zilizowasilishwa hapa zimetumika kwa Lugha za Afrika pia. Mfano mmoja ni Bosch & Eiselen (2012), kwa Kizulu, mwingine ni kikagua tahajia cha Kiswahili kinachosambazwa na kampuni ya Kifini ya Lingsoft. |
| In the GiellaLT infrastructure, both parts are modelled as finite state transducers (FSTs). The morphologically aware dictionary is built directly on the morphological analyser mentioned above after removing unwanted content such as punctuation and non-standard language. The error model is built as a Levenshtein edit distance model Levenshtein (1965) plus language-specific weighted replace rules Pirinen & Lindén (2010). | Katika miundombinu ya GiellaLT, sehemu zote mbili zinaundwa kama transducers za hali finiti (FSTs). Kamusi inayofahamu mofolojia inajengwa moja kwa moja juu ya kichanganuzi cha mofolojia kilichotajwa hapo juu baada ya kuondoa maudhui yasiyotakikana kama vile alama ya uakifishaji na lugha isiyo sanifu. Mfano wa makosa umejengwa kama mfano wa umbali wa uhariri wa Levenshtein Levenshtein (1965) pamoja na sheria za ubadilishaji zenye uzani mahususi kwa lugha Pirinen & Lindén (2010). |
| The speller package is distributed through our own desktop installation and updates system Páhkat, and a pahkat client is also part of the mobile keyboard apps. This means that spellers on all supported systems, both mobile and desktop, are automatically kept up to date. | Paketi ya speller inasambazwa kupitia mfumo wetu wa kipekee wa usakinishaji na masasisho ya desktop unaoitwa Páhkat, na mteja wa pahkat pia ni sehemu ya programu za kibodi za simu. Hii inamaanisha kwamba vikagua tahajia kwenye mifumo yote inayotumika, ya simu ya mkononi na ya eneokazi, vinasasishwa kiotomatiki. |
| As part of the mobile keyboard app, spellers help people in the writing process when using their native keyboard. We are currently experimenting with various forms of word completion and prediction models, but nothing has been released yet. | Kama sehemu ya programu ya kibodi ya simu ya mkononi, vikagua tahajia huwasaidia watu katika mchakato wa uandishi wanapotumia kibodi yao ya asili. Kwa sasa tunajaribu aina mbalimbali za ukamilishaji wa neno na mifano ya utabiri, lakini hakuna kitu ambacho kimetolewa bado. |
| **Grammar checking** | **Ukaguzi wa sarufi** |
| The GiellaLT infrastructure includes an advanced grammar checker framework. It uses a combination of morphological analysers and Constraint Grammar Didriksen (2010) disambiguation and error detection components. Furthermore, the constraint grammar logic is used to determine where the grammar errors are; the logic is similar to grammar-based syntax parsing Wiechetek (2012). It is all rule-based, which means that it is possible to develop with essentially no pre-existing electronic corpora. The grammar checker features are quite new but are already used for four different languages. | Miundombinu ya GiellaLT inajumuisha mfumo wa hali ya juu wa kukagua sarufi. Inatumia mchanganyiko wa vichanganuzi vya kimofolojia na vipengele vya Constraint Grammar Didriksen (2010) vya kuondoa utata na kugundua makosa. Zaidi ya hayo, mantiki ya sarufi ya vizuizi inatumiwa kubainisha mahali ambapo makosa ya sarufi yapo; mantiki hiyo inafanana na uchanganuzi wa sintaksi unaotegemea sarufi Wiechetek (2012). Yote inategemea kanuni, jambo ambalo linamaanisha kwamba inawezekana kuendeleza bila kimsingi kuwepo kwa korasi za kielektroniki za awali. Vipengele vya kikagua sarufi ni vipya kabisa lakini tayari vinatumika kwa lugha nne tofauti. |
| **Machine Translation** | **Utafsiri wa Mashine** |
| The GiellaLT infrastructure supports developing machine translation systems in cooperation with Apertium Khanna et al. (2021). The monolingual models developed in the GiellaLT infra are then combined with the transfer rules and lexicons in Apertium to provide an end-to-end MT system. The Apertium system at its core is also a rule-based machine translation toolkit. This means that one can build MT systems for languages with next to no existing resources in bilingual corpora as well, extending the work put in the monolingual dictionaries. The components needed for a rule-based machine translation on top of the rule-based morphological analysis in GiellaLT infra are a bilingual dictionary, i.e. a regular word-to-word translation dictionary and a set of grammatical rules concerning the translation of linguistic differences between languages. | Miundombinu ya GiellaLT inasaidia kuendeleza mifumo ya tafsiri ya mashine kwa ushirikiano na Apertium Khanna et al. (2021). Mifano ya lugha moja iliyoendelezwa katika miundombinu ya GiellaLT kisha inaunganishwa na kanuni za uhamishaji na leksika katika Apertium ili kutoa mfumo wa MT wa mwanzo hadi mwisho. Mfumo wa Apertium katika msingi wake pia ni zana ya tafsiri ya mashine inayotegemea kanuni. Hii ina maana kwamba mtu anaweza kujenga mifumo ya MT kwa lugha zisizo na rasilimali zilizopo katika korasi za lugha mbili pia, akipanua kazi iliyowekwa katika kamusi za lugha moja. Vipengele vinavyohitajika kwa tafsiri ya mashine inayotegemea kanuni juu ya uchanganuzi wa kimofolojia unaotegemea kanuni katika miundombinu ya GiellaLT ni kamusi ya lugha mbili, yaani kamusi ya kawaida ya tafsiri ya neno kwa neno na seti ya kanuni za kisarufi zinazohusu tafsiri ya tofauti za kiisimu kati ya lugha. |

| We have presented the GiellaLT infrastructure and its philosophy and goal of supporting indigenous languages around the world, especially languages with complex morphology or phonology - or both.
