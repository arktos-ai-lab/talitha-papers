| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| This paper deals with an electronic resource under construction. The objective is to construct a lexical database of Malagasy adjectives. Malagasy is an agglutinative language of Austronesian origin, spoken in the African island of Madagascar. The method used to construct the resource is adapted from the approach of Gross (1989) to electronic dictionaries. The content of the resource is based on linguistic analysis and encoded so as to be used by language-processing software. However, care has been taken that all linguistic information is easily readable and updatable. The resource allows for morphological analysis and generation of adjectives, removing obstacles to the construction of computer applications to process the Malagasy language. The originality of this paper also comes from our proposal of a distinction between adjectives in the usual sense and adjectival forms of other parts of speech. | Makala haya yanahusu rasilimali ya kielektroniki inayojengwa. | **2 Adjectives in Malagasy** | **2 Vivumishi katika Kimalagasi** |
| Language-independent definitions of adjectives, e.g. "a term used in grammatical classification of words to refer to the main set of items which specify the attributes of nouns" (Crystal, 2008: 11), are suitable for Malagasy, but not precise enough, for example, to distinguish adjectives from verbs with the m- prefix. For that matter, we follow Rajaona (1972: 404) and Ralalaoherivony (1995) who draw the line by using the following criterion: verbs have forms in the circumstantial voice, while adjectives do not. Refer to Rajaona (1972: 521), Keenan & Polinski (1998: 609), Dalrymple et al. (2005) about circumstantial voice. So, mandèha " walk" , mandrày "take" are verbs because the circumstantial forms andehánana, andráisana are in use; and elements as m-a-kòtroka, màtotra are adjectives because the circumstantial forms *akotróhana, *atórana are not attested. (Graphical accents in Malagasy are optional and indicate stress.) | Fasili zisizotegemea lugha za vivumishi, k.m. "neno linalotumika katika uainishaji wa kisarufi wa maneno kurejelea seti kuu ya vipashio vinavyobainisha sifa za nomino" (Crystal, 2008: 11), zinafaa kwa Kimalagasi, lakini si sahihi vya kutosha, kwa mfano, kutofautisha vivumishi na vitenzi vyenye kiambishi awali m-. | Like verbs, some adjectives receive inflectional markers of grammatical tense in Malagasy (cf. Rajaonarimanana, 1995: 64). | Sawa na vitenzi, vivumishi fulani hupokea viambishi vya unyambulishaji vya wakati wa kisarufi katika Kimalagasi (cf. Rajaonarimanana, 1995: 64). |

| Finally, modes of inflection are defined explicitly, so that the inflected forms of a stem can be generated automatically. A mode of inflection is the set of morphological changes underwent by a stem to generate its inflected forms. Several lexical entries can share the same inflectional mode, as ring and sing in English. The method requires that inflectional modes are defined independently of one another, in order to avoid constructing a hierarchy of general rules and exceptional rules, since such hierarchies are usually complex to maintain later, as the database undergoes updates. As each inflectional mode is independent, updating one does not require updating others. In consequence, we assign an identifier to each inflectional mode and we mark in each entry of the dictionary the identifier of the type of inflection applicable. Thus, knowing if a rule is applicable to a lexical entry does not require applying it. This contrasts with Two-level morphology (Koskenniemi, 1983), where rule scope is encoded in rules, so that knowing if a lexical entry is affected by a change in a rule requires applying both versions of the rule. In addition, two-level rules are ordered, so that knowing if a lexical entry is affected by a change in rule order requires applying both versions of all rules. These features are practical obstacles to updates, corrections and extensions of two-level databases. | Hatimaye, njia za unyambulishaji zinafafanuliwa kwa uwazi, ili maumbo yaliyonyambulishwa ya shina yaweze kuzalishwa kiotomatiki. Kwa hiyo, tunakabidhi kitambulishi kwa kila modi ya unyambulishaji na tunaweka alama katika kila ingizo la kamusi kitambulishi cha aina ya unyambulishaji unaotumika. Hivyo, kujua ikiwa kanuni fulani inafaa katika ingizo la kileksika hakumaanishi kuitumia. Hii inatofautiana na mofolojia ya ngazi mbili (Koskenniemi, 1983), ambapo wigo wa kanuni umesimbwa katika kanuni, hivyo kujua kama ingizo la kileksika linaathiriwa na badiliko katika kanuni kunahitaji kutumia matoleo yote mawili ya kanuni hiyo. Zaidi ya hayo, kanuni za ngazi mbili zimepangwa kwa mpangilio, hivyo kujua kama ingizo la kileksika linaathiriwa na badiliko katika mpangilio wa kanuni kunahitaji kutumia matoleo yote mawili ya kanuni zote. Sifa hizi ni vizuizi vya kiutendaji kwa usasishaji, marekebisho na upanuzi wa hifadhidata za ngazi mbili. |
| Thus, our approach encodes the generation of inflected forms from their stems, providing a formal link between them, e.g. between sing and singing in English. However, the approach does not do the same between derivatives and their bases, as speak and speech, since semantic and syntactic irregularities reduce the potential applications of such generation. Thus, derived words such as m-èndrika "fit, proper, worthy " , m-i-kodiadìa "big and fat (of a child)" are encoded as stems. | Hivyo, mbinu yetu inasimba uzalishaji wa maumbo yaliyonyambulika kutoka kwenye mashina yake, ikitoa kiungo rasmi kati yake, kwa mfano, kati ya sing na singing katika Kiingereza. Hata hivyo, mkabala huo haufanyi vivyo hivyo kati ya viambajengo na misingi yake, kama speak na speech, kwa kuwa hitilafu za semantiki na kisintaksia hupunguza matumizi yanayowezekana ya uzalishaji huo. | Gross' approach, devised for inflectional languages, has been extended to agglutinative languages (Berlocher et al., 2006; Ranaivoarison et al., 2013) by distinguishing two levels of morphological changes: | Mbinu ya Gross, iliyobuniwa kwa ajili ya lugha za unyambulishaji, imepanuliwa kwa lugha za uambishaji (Berlocher et al., 2006; Ranaivoarison et al., 2013) kwa kutofautisha viwango viwili vya mabadiliko ya kimofolojia: |
| morpheme-internal allomorphy, e.g., the noun sómotra "beard" becomes somór immediately before some suffixes, | mabadiliko ya alomofia ndani ya mofimu, kwa mfano, nomino sómotra "ndevu" inakuwa somór mara moja kabla ya viambishi tamati fulani, |
| affixations, e.g., -ina, a morpheme of formation of adjectival forms, can be suffixed to somór, giving somórina "bearded" . | viambishi, k.m., -ina, mofimu ya uundaji wa maumbo ya vivumishi, inaweza kuongezwa kama kiambishi tamati kwa somór, ikitoa somórina "mwenye ndevu". |
| Through the two steps corresponding to these two levels, the inflected form somòrina can be generated from the lemma sómotra, or conversely, the lemma can be recognised from the inflected form. | Kupitia hatua mbili zinazolingana na viwango hivi viwili, umbo lililonyambulishwa somòrina linaweza kuzalishwa kutoka kwa lemma sómotra, au kinyume chake, lemma inaweza kutambuliwa kutoka kwa umbo lililonyambulishwa. |
| The Unitex open-source platform of language processing (Paumier, 2003) is compatible with resources devised according to this approach and encoded in the DELA format (Gross 1989: 8). In contrast, the Text encoding initiative's dictionary encoding formats are mainly designed for human-oriented dictionaries (TEI Consortium, 2022) and do not address the processing of lexical databases. | Jukwaa huria la Unitex la usindikaji wa lugha (Paumier, 2003) linaendana na rasilimali zilizobuniwa kulingana na mbinu hii na zilizoandikwa katika muundo wa DELA (Gross 1989: 8). Unitex hufanya unyambulishaji, mgandamizo na utafutaji wa hali ya juu (Neme, Paumier, 2019) wa hifadhidata za kileksika zilizosimbwa katika umbizo hili. Kinyume chake, miundo ya usimbaji ya kamusi ya mpango wa Text encoding initiative imeundwa hasa kwa ajili ya kamusi zinazolenga binadamu (TEI Consortium, 2022) na haishughulikii uchakataji wa hifadhidata za kileksika. |
| In the electronic-dictionary approach, lexical databases mainly cover parts-of-speech and inflection. Due to this difference, sense distinctions are more finegrained in the latter approach (Laporte, 1991). | Katika mkabala wa kamusi-elektroniki, hifadhidata za kileksika hushughulikia hasa aina za maneno na unyambulishaji. | **4.1 Phenomena of allomorphy** | **4.1 Matukio ya alomofi** |
| Immediately before or after affixes, some stems do not vary, as in èrika "drizzling rain"/ m-èrika "drizzly, misty " , dìo "cleanliness, purity " /m-a-dìo "clean, clear, pure" , but most do. | prosodic alternations as in sòmotra/somór (the accent shifts from the first ò to the second ó) | mabadiliko ya kiimbo kama katika sòmotra/somór (mkazo huhama kutoka ò ya kwanza hadi ó ya pili) |
| insertion of a letter as in safòfoka/tsafòfoka (insertion of t before the stem in the inflected form mantsafòfoka) | uingizaji wa herufi kama katika safòfoka/tsafòfoka (uingizaji wa t kabla ya shina katika umbo lililonyambulishwa mantsafòfoka) |
| substitution of a letter hàtsiaka/gàtsiaka (substitution of g in the inflected form mangàtsiaka for h in the stem) | ubadilishaji wa herufi hàtsiaka/gàtsiaka (ubadilishaji wa g katika umbo lililonyambulishwa mangàtsiaka badala ya h katika shina) |
| extension as in nòfo/nofós (insertion of s in the inflected form nofósana) | upanuzi kama katika nòfo/nofós (uingizaji wa s katika umbo lililonyambulishwa nofósana) |
| deletion of a letter as in vorètra/orètra (deletion of the letter v of the stem in the inflected form mamorètra) or in sòmotra / somór (deletion of the t and a of sòmotra in the inflected form somórina) | ufutaji wa herufi kama katika vorètra/orètra (ufutaji wa herufi v ya shina katika umbo lililonyambulishwa mamorètra) au katika sòmotra / somór (ufutaji wa t na a za sòmotra katika umbo lililonyambulishwa somórina) |

| **8 Conclusion** | **8 Hitimisho** |
| We described a lexical database of Malagasy adjectives under construction. The content of the resource is based on linguistic analysis, bearing in mind relevant methodological safeguards. We make a distinction between adjectives in the usual sense and adjectival forms of nouns. The database can be used by language-processing software. The resource allows for morphological analysis and generation of adjectives. It is not limited to the adjectival forms occurring in a corpus of texts, but takes into account our competence as a native speaker and grammatical tradition. | Tulielezea hifadhidata ya kileksika ya vivumishi vya Kimalagasi inayojengwa. Maudhui ya rasilimali yanategemea uchambuzi wa lugha, huku tukizingatia tahadhari za kimbinu zinazohusika. Tunatofautisha kati ya vivumishi katika maana ya kawaida na maumbo ya vivumishi ya nomino. Hifadhidata inaweza kutumika na programu za usindikaji wa lugha. Rasilimali inaruhusu uchambuzi wa kimorfolojia na uundaji wa sifa. Haikomei kwenye maumbo ya vivumishi yanayotokea katika mkusanyiko wa matini, bali inazingatia umahiri wetu kama mzungumzaji asilia na mapokeo ya kisarufi. |
| This research has several potential applications. Its results can be used in grammar textbooks to describe the inflection of Malagasy adjectives. Linguists can use them to launch queries for grammatical configurations containing adjectives, e.g. noun phrases. Finally, lexical databases remove obstacles to the construction of computer applications to process languages. | Utafiti huu una matumizi kadhaa yanayowezekana. Matokeo yake yanaweza kutumiwa katika vitabu vya kiada vya sarufi kuelezea unyambulishaji wa vivumishi vya Kimalagasi. Wanaisimu wanaweza kuzitumia kuanzisha maswali kwa ajili ya mipangilio ya kisarufi iliyo na vivumishi, kwa mfano, virai nomino. Hatimaye, hifadhidata za kileksika huondoa vizuizi kwa ujenzi wa programu za kompyuta za kuchakata lugha. |
