| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| This article overviews digital language resources available for Sesotho, an official language of South Africa. The South African Center for Digital Language Resources (SADiLaR) repository is used as a reference as it is the official host of various language resources for South African languages. A total of 18 written resources are identified from the repository, and a further 16 spoken resources are identified. Finally, a total of 45 applications and modules were identified. Findings indicate that the majority of applications and modules available for Sesotho are in fact general resources aimed at all eleven official South African languages. Furthermore, the available resources indicate an inclination to the development of entry level, basic language resources and an absence of middle and higher resources with functionalities such as semantic analyses for written resources and prosody prediction for spoken resources. The study is hindered by the dearth of resource specific evaluations and related research and exacerbated by the absence of some of the resources on the repository. | Makala haya yanatoa muhtasari wa rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali zinazopatikana kwa Sesotho, lugha rasmi ya Afrika Kusini. Hazina ya Kituo cha Afrika Kusini cha Rasilimali za Lugha za Kidijitali (SADiLaR) inatumika kama rejeleo kwa kuwa ndiyo mwenyeji rasmi wa rasilimali mbalimbali za lugha kwa Lugha za Afrika Kusini. Jumla ya rasilimali 18 zilizoandikwa zinatambuliwa kutoka kwenye hazina hiyo, na rasilimali nyingine 16 za mazungumzo zinatambuliwa. Hatimaye, jumla ya programu tumizi na moduli 45 zilitambuliwa. Matokeo yanaonyesha kwamba programu tumizi na moduli nyingi zinazopatikana kwa Sesotho kwa kweli ni rasilimali za jumla zinazolenga Lugha zote kumi na moja rasmi za Afrika Kusini. Zaidi ya hayo, rasilimali zilizopo zinaonyesha mwelekeo wa kuendeleza rasilimali za lugha za kiwango cha kuingia, za msingi na ukosefu wa rasilimali za kati na za juu zenye utendaji kama vile uchanganuzi wa semantiki kwa rasilimali zilizoandikwa na utabiri wa prosodi kwa rasilimali za mazungumzo. | **1 Introduction** | **1 Utangulizi** |
| There is a growing interest in Human Language Technologies (HLTs) for low-resourced languages (LRLs) (Strassel & Tracey 2016). Accordingly, a number of HLT audits have been conducted on South African official languages (Grover et al. 2010, 2011, Moors, Wilken, Calteaux & Gumede 2018, Moors, Wilken, Gumede & Calteaux 2018). The language audits are aimed at two objectives that are (i) determining resources that need to be developed, and (ii) opportunities for multidisciplinary research. South Africa currently recognizes eleven official languages, namely, Afrikaans, English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Siswati, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, isiNdebele, Setswana, Sepedi and Sesotho. In this article, we pay special attention to digital language resources available for Sesotho, a Bantu language that forms part of the bigger SothoTswana group with Sepedi and Setswana (Riep 2013, Van Heerden et al. 2010, Nkolola-Wakumelol et al. 2012, Mojela 2016). Additionally, Sesotho is one of the official languages in Lesotho, and an officially recognised language in Zimbabwe (Ndlovu 2011, 2013, Kadenge & Mugari 2015, Wissing & Roux 2017). Sesotho has developed as both a spoken and written language (Moeketsi 2014, Koai & Fredericks 2019), and is used in a variety of domains. | Kuna hamu inayoongezeka katika Teknolojia za Lugha za Kibinadamu (HLTs) kwa lugha zenye rasilimali chache (LRLs) (Strassel & Tracey 2016). Kwa mujibu huo, ukaguzi kadhaa wa HLT umefanywa kwa lugha rasmi za Afrika Kusini (Grover et al. 2010, 2011, Moors, Wilken, Calteaux & Gumede 2018, Moors, Wilken, Gumede & Calteaux 2018). Ukaguzi wa lugha unalenga malengo mawili ambayo ni (i) kubainisha rasilimali zinazohitaji kuendelezwa, na (ii) fursa za utafiti wa fani mbalimbali. Afrika Kusini kwa sasa inatambua lugha rasmi kumi na moja, nazo ni, Afrikaans, English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Siswati, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, isiNdebele, Setswana, Sepedi na Sesotho. Katika makala hii, tunatoa kipaumbele maalum kwa rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali zinazopatikana kwa Sesotho, lugha ya Kibantu ambayo ni sehemu ya kundi kubwa la SothoTswana pamoja na Sepedi na Setswana (Riep 2013, Van Heerden et al. 2010, Nkolola-Wakumelol et al. 2012, Mojela 2016). Zaidi ya hayo, Kisotho ni mojawapo ya lugha rasmi nchini Lesotho, na ni lugha inayotambuliwa rasmi nchini Zimbabwe (Ndlovu 2011, 2013, Kadenge & Mugari 2015, Wissing & Roux 2017). Kisotho kimeendelezwa kama lugha ya mazungumzo na ya maandishi (Moeketsi 2014, Koai & Fredericks 2019), na kinatumika katika nyanja mbalimbali. |
| Of the eleven official languages of South Africa, two languages, namely: Afrikaans and South African English, have the most digital language resources followed by Setswana, Sepedi, isiZulu, isiXhosa and Sesotho in no particular order (Moors, Wilken, Calteaux & Gumede 2018, Moors, Wilken, Gumede & Calteaux 2018). This reality is propelled by the lack of investment into the remaining nine indigenous languages. This image was illustrated at the Workshop on Collaboration and Computing for Under-Resourced Languages in the Linked Open Data Era (Pretorius et al. 2014). Nonetheless, although there are digital language resources in Sesotho, the language remains marginalised and under-resourced as it is yet to have enough high quality supervised data (Koai & Fredericks 2019, Magueresse et al. 2020). As a result, multiple studies identify Sesotho as a low-resourced language (Mahloane & Trausan-Matu 2015, Chiguvare & Cleghorn 2021, Hanslo 2021, Sibeko & Van Zaanen 2022a). Moors, Wilken, Gumede & Calteaux (2018) go as far as identifying it as a severely under-resourced language. That is, although it is taught as a home language and an additional language in both South Africa and Lesotho, it persists to be less studied for computerization, and thus remains less privileged and of low density (Cieri et al. 2016, Magueresse et al. 2020). | Kati ya lugha rasmi kumi na moja za Afrika Kusini, lugha mbili, nazo: Kiafrikana na Kiingereza cha Afrika Kusini, ndizo zenye rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali nyingi zaidi zikifuatiwa na Kisetswana, Kipedi, Kizulu, Kixhosa na Kisotho bila mpangilio maalum (Moors, Wilken, Calteaux & Gumede 2018, Moors, Wilken, Gumede & Calteaux 2018). Ukweli huu unachochewa na ukosefu wa uwekezaji katika lugha tisa za asili zilizobaki. Picha hii ilionyeshwa katika Warsha ya Ushirikiano na Kompyuta kwa Lugha zenye Rasilimali Chache katika Enzi ya Data Huria Iliyounganishwa (Pretorius et al. 2014). Hata hivyo, ingawa kuna rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali katika Kisotho, lugha hiyo inabakia kutengwa na kuwa na rasilimali chache kwani bado haina data za kutosha za ubora wa juu zilizosimamiwa (Koai & Fredericks 2019, Magueresse et al. 2020). Kwa sababu hiyo, tafiti nyingi zinatambua Kisotho kama lugha yenye rasilimali chache (Mahloane & Trausan-Matu 2015, Chiguvare & Cleghorn 2021, Hanslo 2021, Sibeko & Van Zaanen 2022a). Moors, Wilken, Gumede & Calteaux (2018) wanafikia hatua ya kukitambua kama lugha yenye upungufu mkubwa wa rasilimali. Yaani, ingawa inafundishwa kama lugha ya nyumbani na lugha ya ziada nchini Afrika Kusini na Lesotho, inaendelea kuchunguzwa kidogo kwa ajili ya ukompyutishaji, na hivyo inabaki ikiwa na upendeleo mdogo na msongamano wa chini (Cieri et al. 2016, Magueresse et al. 2020). |
| Although there have been language audits of all eleven official languages of South Africa, as far as we could ascertain for this article, no study has paid in depth attention to Sesotho digital language resources. Ensuingly, this article seeks to describe basic digital language resources, modules, and applications used in written and speech applications as a way of inventorising Sesotho digital language resources. | Ingawa kumekuwa na ukaguzi wa lugha wa lugha zote kumi na moja rasmi za Afrika Kusini, kadiri tulivyoweza kubaini kwa makala hii, hakuna utafiti ambao umezingatia kwa kina rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali za Kisotho. Kufuatia hayo, makala hii inalenga kueleza rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali za msingi, moduli, na programu-tumizi zinazotumika katika programu-tumizi za maandishi na za usemi kama njia ya kuorodhesha rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali za Kisotho. |
| **2 Background** | **2 Usuli** |
| Natural Language Processing (NLP) has been a very active area of research (Drake 2003). It is aimed at both automatically analysing and representing human language using computational technologies (Cambria & White 2014, Kang et al. 2020). These computational techniques are based on both theory and available technologies that enable them to learn, from existing human language content, understand how the human language is produced, and produce human language content independent of humans (Liddy 2001, Hirschberg & Manning 2015). NLP research gathers knowledge on how humans use language and in turn develop applications to enable computers to simulate and handle natural languages (Chowdhury 2003). As such, NLP is a discipline within Artificial Intelligence (AI) and linguistics since it is characterized by human-like language processing capabilities (Drake 2003, Nadkarni et al. 2011). Frequent applications of NLP include (i) information retrieval, (ii) information extraction, (iii) question answering, (iv) summarization, (v) machine translation, and (vi) dialogue systems (Drake 2003). Some models cater for more than one language (Castellucci et al. 2021). | Uchakataji wa Lugha Asilia (NLP) umekuwa eneo amilifu sana la utafiti (Drake 2003). Inalenga kuchanganua kiotomatiki na kuwakilisha lugha ya binadamu kwa kutumia teknolojia za kikompyuta (Cambria & White 2014, Kang et al. 2020). Mbinu hizi za kikompyuta zinategemea nadharia na teknolojia zilizopo zinazowawezesha kujifunza, kutoka kwa maudhui yaliyopo ya lugha ya binadamu, kuelewa jinsi lugha ya binadamu inavyotokezwa, na kutokeza maudhui ya lugha ya binadamu bila kutegemea binadamu (Liddy 2001, Hirschberg & Manning 2015). Utafiti wa NLP hukusanya maarifa kuhusu jinsi binadamu wanavyotumia lugha na kwa upande mwingine kuendeleza programu-tumizi ili kuwezesha kompyuta kuiga na kushughulikia lugha asilia (Chowdhury 2003). Kwa hivyo, NLP ni taaluma ndani ya Ujasusi Bandia (AI) na isimu kwa kuwa ina sifa ya uwezo wa kuchakata lugha unaofanana na wa binadamu (Drake 2003, Nadkarni et al. 2011). Matumizi ya mara kwa mara ya NLP yanajumuisha (i) urejeshaji wa taarifa, (ii) uchimbaji wa taarifa, (iii) kujibu maswali, (iv) ufupisho, (v) tafsiri ya mashine, na (vi) mifumo ya mazungumzo (Drake 2003). Baadhi ya modeli huhudumia zaidi ya lugha moja (Castellucci et al. 2021). |
| Carrying out NLP tasks relies on the availability of HLT resources which are developed using digital language resources. Unfortunately, none of the South African official languages have official Basic Language Resource Kits (BLARKs) as conceptualised in Krauwer (2003). Generally, a BLARK defines the minimal required resources for performing pre-competitive research in both spoken and written language for a specific language (Arppe et al. 2010). BLARKs are categorised into three, namely, definition, specification, and content (Maegaard et al. 2006). | Utekelezaji wa kazi za NLP unategemea upatikanaji wa rasilimali za HLT ambazo hutengenezwa kwa kutumia rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali. Kwa bahati mbaya, hakuna lugha rasmi za Afrika Kusini zilizo na Vifaa vya Msingi vya Rasilimali za Lugha (BLARKs) rasmi kama ilivyodhaniwa katika Krauwer (2003). Kwa ujumla, BLARK inafafanua rasilimali za chini zinazohitajika kwa ajili ya kufanya utafiti wa kabla ya ushindani katika lugha ya kusemwa na kuandikwa kwa lugha maalum (Arppe et al. 2010). BLARKs zimegawanywa katika makundi matatu, yaani, ufafanuzi, vipimo, na maudhui (Maegaard et al. 2006). |
| First, the definition category indicates what should be regarded as basic in the given language. For instance, Arppe et al. (2016) present a matrix that indicates the resources needed for Plains Cree (an indigenous language of central Canada) together with their levels of importance. | Kwanza, kundi la ufafanuzi linaonyesha kile kinachopaswa kuchukuliwa kuwa msingi katika lugha husika. Kwa mfano, Arppe et al. (2016) wanawasilisha matrix inayoonyesha rasilimali zinazohitajika kwa Plains Cree (lugha ya kiasili ya Kanada ya kati) pamoja na viwango vyao vya umuhimu. |
| Second, the specification category prescribes the quantities of the resources defined such as the Arabic basic resource specification by Maegaard et al. (2006) that lists the quantities of resources needed for Arabic. Finally, the content category describes what already exists. One example of this is a survey of applications available for Swedish (Elenius et al. 2008). | Pili, kundi la vipimo linaagiza kiasi cha rasilimali zilizofafanuliwa kama vile vipimo vya msingi vya rasilimali za Kiarabu na Maegaard et al. (2006) vinavyoorodhesha kiasi cha rasilimali zinazohitajika kwa Kiarabu. Hatimaye, kategoria ya maudhui inaelezea kile ambacho tayari kipo. Mfano mmoja wa hili ni uchunguzi wa programu zinazopatikana kwa Kiswidi (Elenius et al. 2008). |
| In this article, we limit our discussion of basic digital language resources to BLARK content. That is, we inventorize written and spoken digital language resources available in and for Sesotho. Although the definition of BLARK content is language-independent, the resources identified and the level of importance allotted to the resources are specific to Sesotho (Krauwer 2003, Arppe et al. 2010). | Katika makala hii, tunapunguza mjadala wetu wa rasilimali za msingi za kidijitali za lugha kwa maudhui ya BLARK. Yaani, tunaorodhesha rasilimali za kidijitali za lugha zilizoandikwa na zinazozungumzwa zinazopatikana ndani na kwa ajili ya Kisotho. Ingawa ufafanuzi wa maudhui ya BLARK haujategemei lugha, rasilimali zilizotambuliwa na kiwango cha umuhimu kilichotolewa kwa rasilimali hizo ni mahususi kwa Kisotho (Krauwer 2003, Arppe et al. 2010). |
| Amongst others, industry and education institutions benefit from the availability of BLARKs (Maegaard et al. 2005). In other words, since the BLARK can identify both what already exists and what should be developed, industrial and academic developers can earmark needed resources. Furthermore, BLARK content makes it easier for researchers and educators to unearth paid and freely accessible resources at their disposal. Even so, there are two main issues with BLARKS. First, availability is not a binary distinction in that some existing resources may still be inaccessible due to financial or copyright issues (Krauwer 2003). Second, the multiplicity of resources may not be equated to usability, quality output, or user-friendliness. | Miongoni mwa mengine, tasnia na taasisi za elimu hunufaika kutokana na upatikanaji wa BLARK (Maegaard et al. 2005). Kwa maneno mengine, kwa kuwa BLARK inaweza kutambua kile kilichopo tayari na kile kinachopaswa kuendelezwa, watengenezaji wa viwandani na wa kitaaluma wanaweza kutenga rasilimali zinazohitajika. Zaidi ya hayo, maudhui ya BLARK hurahisisha watafiti na waelimishaji kugundua rasilimali za kulipia na zinazopatikana bure walizo nazo. Hata hivyo, kuna masuala makuu mawili kuhusu BLARK. Kwanza, upatikanaji si tofauti ya namna mbili kwa kuwa baadhi ya rasilimali zilizopo bado zinaweza kuwa zisizoweza kufikiwa kutokana na masuala ya kifedha au hakimiliki (Krauwer 2003). Pili, wingi wa rasilimali hauwezi kulinganishwa na utumikaji, matokeo bora, au urafiki wa mtumiaji. |
| When carrying out BLARK content, three BLARK aspects are evaluated, namely, (i) data, (ii) modules, and (iii) applications (Arppe et al. 2010, Krauwer 2003). For instance, in their discussion, Sibeko & Van Zaanen (2022a) identify their target application, that is, a readability analysis application. However, they indicated that some modules such as a syllabification system needed to be developed. For the development of the syllabification system, they created a corpus of syllabification annotated corpora. In this way, the data enabled the development of the module which will then be incorporated into their application. | Wakati wa kutekeleza yaliyomo ya BLARK, vipengele vitatu vya BLARK vinatathminiwa, yaani, (i) data, (ii) moduli, na (iii) matumizi (Arppe et al. 2010, Krauwer 2003). Kwa mfano, katika mjadala wao, Sibeko & Van Zaanen (2022a) wanabainisha programu tumizi yao lengwa, yaani, programu tumizi ya uchanganuzi wa usomaji. Hata hivyo, walionyesha kwamba baadhi ya moduli kama vile mfumo wa ugawaji wa silabi zilihitaji kutengenezwa. Kwa ajili ya utengenezaji wa mfumo wa ugawaji wa silabi, waliunda korasi ya korasi zilizofafanuliwa kwa ugawaji wa silabi. Kwa njia hii, data iliwezesha utengenezaji wa moduli ambayo baadaye itajumuishwa katika programu yao. |

| **4.1.1 Monolingual lexicon** | **4.1.1 Leksimu ya lugha moja** |
| Few written monolingual corpora were identified. One, the National Centre for Human Language Technology (NCHLT) produced four data sets focused on plain corpora, annotated corpora (Eiselen & Puttkamer 2014), phrase chunks (Eiselen 2016b), and named entity (Eiselen 2016a). Two, a customised government domain specific dictionary was identified (Bosch & Griesel 2017). Finally, a syllable annotated word list was identified. It contains one word and its corresponding syllabified versions on each line (Sibeko & Van Zaanen 2022b). | **4.1.2 Translated word lists** | **4.1.2 Orodha za maneno zilizotafsiriwa** |
| Many language pairs lack enough parallel texts (Koehn & Knight 2002). This is seemingly the same case in South African indigenous languages. | **4.3.4 Sesotho-only** | **4.3.4 Sesotho-pekee** |
| We identified seven applications that are specifically for Sesotho. First, we identify two NCHLT products. One, the lemmatiser was developed using a rules-based approach (Eiselen & Puttkamer 2014). Two, the Morphological Decomposer that splits tokens to morphemes was developed after the POS tagger (Eiselen & Puttkamer 2014). Although Eiselen & Puttkamer (2014) provide examples of decomposition for isiZulu and Afrikaans, there are no examples for decomposition in other languages such as Sesotho. Second, the Lwazi Sesotho Pronunciation Dictionary includes audios for phonemes and letter-to-sound rule set based on generic words (Davel & Martirosian 2009). Although accuracy is not guaranteed, practical usability is assured. Third, the Lwazi II Sotho Pronunciation Dictionaries (Du Plessis et al. 1974), are based on the Lwazi dictionaries. Fourth, the Spelling Checker 1.0 is an application that checks spelling and provides hyphenations. It is compatible with some versions of Ms Office. | **5 Discussion and conclusion** | **5 Mjadala na Hitimisho** |
| Many LRL's are underrepresented in NLP tasks because of insufficient corpora needed to complete NLP tasks (Mahloane & Trausan-Matu 2015). The limited availability of corpora as indicated in this article shows a great need for curating more corpora for Sesotho. This article considered basic digital language resources available to Sesotho from a BLARKs content perspective. | LRL nyingi hazijawakilishwa vya kutosha katika kazi za NLP kwa sababu ya ukosefu wa korasi za kutosha zinazohitajika kukamilisha kazi za NLP (Mahloane & Trausan-Matu 2015). Upatikanaji mdogo wa korpus kama ilivyoonyeshwa katika makala hii unaonyesha haja kubwa ya kuratibu korpus zaidi kwa lugha ya Sesotho. Makala hii ilizingatia rasilimali za lugha za kimsingi za kidijitali zinazopatikana kwa Kisotho kutoka mtazamo wa maudhui ya BLARKs. |
| The listed resources were listed without in depth analysis of each resource such as considering how each resource works or the levels of accuracy and practical issues such as user-friendliness. We consulted literature relevant to the resources presented in the repository. To this end, some of these resources have been judged on issues such as accuracy and user-friendliness. However, this was not the aim of this paper. This is typical of BLARK content studies. Although this is basic, it functions as a starting point for investigations of BLARKs specification and definition. That is, determining what should be regarded as basic in the language in Sesotho and the quantities that should be developed. Nonetheless, it is clear from this article that written resources are focused only on very basic functions. For instance, there are no resources for higher level basic functionalities such as semantic analysis and term extractors. Even so, only the handwritten OCR and ontologies are missing from the written resources as identified in the BLARKs (Maegaard et al. 2005, 2006, Krauwer 2003). There have been even fewer attempts at speech technologies, especially those that are specifically aimed at Sesotho or the Sesotho language group. Even so, it is interesting how much work has been achieved. | Rasilimali zilizoorodheshwa ziliorodheshwa bila uchambuzi wa kina wa kila rasilimali kama vile kuzingatia jinsi kila rasilimali inavyofanya kazi au viwango vya Usahihi na masuala ya kivitendo kama vile urahisi wa matumizi. Tulishauriana na fasihi inayohusiana na rasilimali zilizowasilishwa kwenye hazina. Kwa kusudi hili, baadhi ya rasilimali hizi zimehukumiwa kuhusu masuala kama vile Usahihi na urahisi wa matumizi. Hata hivyo, hili halikuwa lengo la makala hii. Hii ni kawaida ya tafiti za maudhui ya BLARK. Ingawa hili ni la msingi, linafanya kazi kama kianzio cha uchunguzi wa vipimo na ufafanuzi wa BLARK. Yaani, kuamua kile kinachopaswa kuchukuliwa kama msingi katika lugha ya Sesotho na kiasi kinachopaswa kuendelezwa. Hata hivyo, ni wazi kutokana na makala hii kwamba rasilimali zilizoandikwa zinalenga tu kazi za msingi sana. Kwa mfano, hakuna rasilimali za utendakazi wa kiwango cha juu zaidi kama vile uchanganuzi wa semantiki na vitoa istilahi. Hata hivyo, ni OCR iliyoandikwa kwa mkono na ontolojia pekee ndizo zinazokosekana kutoka kwa rasilimali zilizoandikwa kama zilivyotambuliwa katika BLARKs (Maegaard et al. 2005, 2006, Krauwer 2003). Kumekuwa na majaribio machache hata zaidi katika teknolojia za usemi, hasa zile zinazolenga Sesotho au kikundi cha lugha cha Sesotho. Hata hivyo, inavutia ni kiasi gani cha kazi kimefikiwa. |
| A narrowed investigation into the current resources and an evaluation of each technology should be considered for future studies. SADiLaR is currently conducting their routine language resource audit, more resources might be added to their repository after their investigation. Perhaps we may gain more insight into digital language resources available to Sesotho. | Uchunguzi uliofinywa katika rasilimali za sasa na tathmini ya kila teknolojia inapaswa kuzingatiwa kwa tafiti za baadaye. SADiLaR kwa sasa inafanya ukaguzi wao wa kawaida wa rasilimali za lugha, rasilimali zaidi zinaweza kuongezwa kwenye hazina yao baada ya uchunguzi wao. Pengine tunaweza kupata ufahamu zaidi kuhusu rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali zinazopatikana kwa Sesotho. |
| This article was limited in three aspects. First, the resources surveyed in this review are not evaluated for their usability, accuracy, and precision. Second, some of the resources were only listed and indexed on the repository, however, the actual resources were not accessible. Third, the study only reviews resources that are indexed by SADiLaR. We recommend that future developments of digital language resources for Sesotho consider this inventory in their decisions on what resources to develop so that focus is paid to new and currently unavailable resources. Even so, we acknowledge that the current index indicates variation in the current collection. | Makala haya yalikuwa na mipaka katika vipengele vitatu. Kwanza, rasilimali zilizochunguzwa katika mapitio haya hazijatathminiwa kwa ajili ya utumiaji wake, Usahihi, na Ushabaha. Pili, baadhi ya rasilimali ziliorodheshwa tu na kuwekwa kwenye faharasa kwenye hazina, hata hivyo, rasilimali halisi hazikuweza kufikiwa. Tatu, utafiti unapitia tu rasilimali ambazo zimeorodheshwa na SADiLaR. Tunapendekeza kwamba maendeleo ya baadaye ya rasilimali za lugha za kidijitali kwa Sesotho yazingatie orodha hii katika maamuzi yao kuhusu rasilimali zipi za kuendeleza ili mkazo uelekezwe kwenye rasilimali mpya na zisizopatikana kwa sasa. Hata hivyo, tunakiri kwamba faharasa ya sasa inaonyesha tofauti katika mkusanyiko wa sasa. |
