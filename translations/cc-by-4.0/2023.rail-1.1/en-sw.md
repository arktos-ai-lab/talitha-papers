| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| This paper presents a spell checker and correction tool specifically designed for Wolof, an under-represented spoken language in Africa. The proposed spell checker leverages a combination of a trie data structure, dynamic programming, and the weighted Levenshtein distance to generate suggestions for misspelled words. We created novel linguistic resources for Wolof, such as a lexicon and a corpus of misspelled words, using a semi-automatic approach that combines manual and automatic annotation methods. Despite the limited data available for the Wolof language, the spell checker's performance showed a predictive accuracy of 98.31% and a suggestion accuracy of 93.33%. | Makala hii inawasilisha kikagua tahajia na zana ya usahihishaji iliyoundwa mahsusi kwa Wolof, lugha inayozungumzwa isiyowakilishwa vya kutosha barani Afrika. | The causes of spelling errors are diverse and can stem from both cognitive and typographical sources (Peterson, 1980). Cognitive errors arise when an individual lacks the proper understanding of the correct spelling of a word while typographical errors take place when incorrect keystrokes are made when typing. Literature in the field of spelling correction has typically approached these error types separately, with various techniques developed specifically to address each type (Kukich, 1992). Despite the significance of language processing, there has been a shortfall of focus on the creation of automatic spelling correction tools for low resource languages especially. While some attempts have been made to apply standard automatic spelling correction techniques to a few African indigenous languages (Boago Okgetheng et al., 2022; M'eric, 2014; Salifou and Naroua, 2014), no such efforts have been made for the Wolof language. As far as we are aware, the only research solely dedicated to the correction of Wolof is (Lo et al., 2016), which provides an overview of the state of the art and outlines potential solutions for developing a tailored orthographic corrector for Wolof. This research adopts commonly used approaches in the field and assesses the performance of our system using various known evaluation metrics. | Visababishi vya makosa ya tahajia ni vingi na vinaweza kutokana na vyanzo vya kiakili na vya uchapaji (Peterson, 1980). Kadiri tujuavyo, utafiti pekee uliojitolea kikamilifu kwa usahihishaji wa Wolof ni (Lo et al., 2016), ambao unatoa muhtasari wa hali ya kisasa na kuelezea suluhu zinazowezekana za kuunda kirekebishaji cha tahajia kilicholengwa mahususi kwa Wolof. However, word tokenization is the most commonly used form of segmentation in spell checking systems, as it separates the text into individual words and provides a solid foundation for the identification of spelling errors (Mosavi Miangah, 2013; Rahman et al., 2021; Abdulrahman and Hassani, 2022). | Mchakato wa ugawaji wa tokeni husababisha vipashio vinavyoweza kuanzia herufi binafsi au maneno hadi vishazi au hata sentensi kamili. | Word tokenization not only enhances the accuracy and efficiency of the spell checking process but also allows for an analysis of the context in which each word appears. | In the current investigation, we are implementing the process of tokenization with a focus on word-level segmentation. | Katika uchunguzi wa sasa, tunatekeleza mchakato wa ugawaji wa tokeni kwa kuzingatia ugawaji katika kiwango cha neno. |
| **3.3 Error Detection** | **3.3 Kugundua makosa** |
| The error detection stage in our spell checking system is designed to identify non-word errors in the text. This is achieved through a two-step process, consisting of validation against Wolof writing rules and comparison with a constructed lexicon. | Hatua ya utambuzi wa makosa katika mfumo wetu wa kikagua tahajia imeundwa kutambua makosa ya maneno yasiyo sahihi katika maandishi. Hili linafikiwa kupitia mchakato wa hatua mbili, unaojumuisha uthibitishaji dhidi ya kanuni za uandishi za Wolof na ulinganisho na leksimu iliyoundwa. |
| **3.3.1 Rules Validator** | **3.3.1 Kithibitishaji cha Kanuni** |
| The spelling of words in the Wolof language follows certain conventions, as described by the CVC and CVCV(C) forms for monosyllabic and disyllabic words, respectively (Merrill, 2021). These conventions specify that the final consonant and vowel of a syllable cannot both be long, and strong consonants cannot appear after a long vowel or at the beginning of a word, except for prenasalized consonants. | Tahajia ya maneno katika lugha ya Wolof hufuata kanuni fulani, kama inavyoelezwa na maumbo ya CVC na CVCV(C) kwa maneno ya silabi moja na mawili, mtawalia (Merrill, 2021). Kanuni hizi zinabainisha kwamba konsonanti ya mwisho na irabu ya silabi haziwezi zote kuwa ndefu, na konsonanti kali haziwezi kutokea baada ya irabu ndefu au mwanzoni mwa neno, isipokuwa kwa konsonanti zilizotangulizwa nazali. |
| Our error detection stage includes a validation step that rigorously checks each word in the input text against these writing conventions. If a word is found to be in compliance with these rules, it will move on to the next stage of validation. Conversely, if the word is determined to be non-compliant, it will be flagged as invalid and require correction. | Hatua yetu ya utambuzi wa makosa inajumuisha hatua ya uthibitishaji inayokagua kwa ukali kila neno katika maandishi ya pembejeo dhidi ya kanuni hizi za uandishi. Iwapo neno litapatikana kuwa linafuata kanuni hizi, litaendelea hadi hatua inayofuata ya uthibitishaji. Kinyume chake, iwapo neno litabainika kuwa halifuati, litawekewa alama kama batili na kuhitaji marekebisho. |
| **3.3.2 Lexicon Check** | **3.3.2 Ukaguzi wa Leksikoni** |
| In the lexicon verification phase, the spell checking system assesses each word in the input text against the Wolof lexicon to determine its validity. The lexicon, being a large repository of words, can pose challenges for quick and efficient searches. To address this, various techniques such as hash tables (Kukich, 1992), binary search (Knuth, 1998), tries data structure (Bentley and Sedgewick, 1997), and bloom filter (Bloom, 1970) have been developed to enable fast dictionary lookups. | Katika awamu ya uthibitishaji wa leksikoni, mfumo wa kikagua tahajia hutathmini kila neno katika maandishi ya pembejeo dhidi ya leksikoni ya Wolof ili kubaini uhalali wake. Leksikoni, ikiwa ni hifadhi kubwa ya maneno, inaweza kuleta changamoto kwa utafutaji wa haraka na wenye ufanisi. Ili kukabiliana na hili, mbinu mbalimbali kama vile majedwali ya heshi (Kukich, 1992), utafutaji wa mfuatano wa jozi (Knuth, 1998), muundo wa data wa tries (Bentley and Sedgewick, 1997), na kichujio cha bloom (Bloom, 1970) zimetengenezwa ili kuwezesha upekuzi wa haraka wa kamusi. |
| In the present spell checker, the system uses the trie data structure, which organizes the lexicon into nodes that represent individual characters and the root node that represents the empty string. In this structure, searching for a word in the lexicon involves following the path through the trie that corresponds to the characters of the target word (Feng et al., 2012). If the end of the path is a terminal node, the word is considered to be in the lexicon and deemed valid. Conversely, if the path ends before reaching a terminal node, the word is considered incorrect and corrections are initiated. | Katika kikagua tahajia cha sasa, mfumo hutumia muundo wa data wa trie, ambao hupanga leksikoni katika nodi zinazowakilisha herufi binafsi na nodi ya mzizi inayowakilisha tungo tupu. Katika muundo huu, kutafuta neno katika leksikoni kunahusisha kufuata njia kupitia trie inayolingana na herufi za neno lengwa (Feng et al., 2012). Ikiwa mwisho wa njia ni nodi ya mwisho, neno linachukuliwa kuwa katika leksikoni na kuhesabiwa kuwa halali. Kinyume chake, ikiwa njia inaisha kabla ya kufikia nodi ya mwisho, neno linachukuliwa kuwa si sahihi na masahihisho huanzishwa. |

| **3.4.2 Generation of Candidate Suggestions** | **3.4.2 Uzalishaji wa Mapendekezo ya Wagombea** |
| In the current system, to generate potential alternatives for misspelled words, we have implemented a lexicographical distance comparison method. This process involves determining the minimum number of edit operations, such as insertion, deletion, transposition, and substitution, necessary to change one word into another (Vienney, 2004). The more significant the disparities between two words, the greater the lexicographical distance between them. Out of various lexicographical distance metrics, the Levenshtein Distance (Levenshtein, 1965) is the most commonly utilized. It quantifies the difference between two strings based on the three fundamental string operations: substitution, insertion, and deletion. | Katika mfumo wa sasa, ili kuzalisha mbadala zinazowezekana kwa maneno yaliyoandikwa vibaya, tumetekeleza mbinu ya kulinganisha umbali wa kileksikografia. | **5 Limitations** | **5 Mapungufu** |
| Despite the impressive performance and minimal processing time of our spell checker, it is important to acknowledge its limitations. | Licha ya utendaji wa kuvutia na muda mdogo wa usindikaji wa mkaguzi wetu wa tahajia, ni muhimu kutambua vikwazo vyake. |
| Firstly, the spell checker is restricted to the words included in the created Wolof lexicon and cannot recognize words outside of it. Secondly, the weighted Levenshtein distance algorithm used may not always accurately reflect the likelihood of different types of errors, leading to potential inaccuracies in the suggestions. | Kwanza, kikagua tahajia kinahusisha maneno yaliyojumuishwa katika leksimu ya Kiolofu iliyoundwa pekee na hakiwezi kutambua maneno nje yake. Pili, algoriti ya umbali wa Levenshtein yenye uzani inayotumika huenda isiakisi kwa usahihi kila mara uwezekano wa aina tofauti za makosa, na kusababisha uwezekano wa kutokuwa sahihi katika mapendekezo. |
| Thirdly, the dynamic programming and trie data structures utilized may result in false positive suggestions due to a lack of consideration for the semantic meaning of words. Additionally, the computational cost of our approach can be substantial, particularly for larger lexicons or words with numerous possible corrections. Finally, the lack of context awareness may result in missed errors or incorrect suggestions. | Tatu, miundo ya data ya upangaji programu nguvu na trie inayotumika inaweza kusababisha mapendekezo ya chanya ya uwongo kutokana na kutozingatiwa kwa maana ya semantiki ya maneno. Zaidi ya hayo, gharama ya kikokotozi ya mbinu yetu inaweza kuwa kubwa, hasa kwa leksika kubwa au maneno yenye marekebisho mengi yanayowezekana. Mwishowe, ukosefu wa ufahamu wa muktadha unaweza kusababisha makosa yaliyokosekana au mapendekezo yasiyo sahihi. |
| **6 Conclusion** | **6 Hitimisho** |
| This paper presented a novel spell checker for the Wolof language, that has demonstrated its potential, owing to its effective combination of the trie data structure, dynamic programming, and weighted Levenshtein distance algorithms. The hybrid approach of manual and automatic annotation enabled the construction of a comprehensive lexicon and a robust Misspelled Word Corpus, allowing for a robust evaluation of the spell checker's potential despite the limited data available for the language. Through these efforts, we hope to advance the state of NLP research for the Wolof language and contribute to preserving the linguistic heritage of African nations, ensuring that their distinct cultural expressions are protected for future generations. | Makala hii iliwasilisha kikagua tahajia kipya kwa lugha ya Wolof, ambacho kimeonyesha uwezo wake, kutokana na mchanganyiko wake mzuri wa muundo wa data wa trie, upangaji programu nguvu, na algoriti za umbali wa Levenshtein zenye uzani. Mbinu mseto ya ufafanuzi kwa mkono na otomatiki iliwezesha ujenzi wa leksika pana na Korasi Imara ya Maneno yenye Makosa ya Tahajia, ikiruhusu tathmini imara ya uwezo wa kikagua tahajia licha ya data chache inayopatikana kwa lugha hiyo. Kupitia juhudi hizi, tunatumai kuendeleza hali ya utafiti wa NLP kwa lugha ya Wolof na kuchangia katika kuhifadhi urithi wa kiisimu wa mataifa ya Kiafrika, kuhakikisha kwamba maonyesho yao tofauti ya kitamaduni yanalindwa kwa vizazi vijavyo. |
| The findings of this research provide compelling evidence of the viability of the spell checker for the Wolof language, opening avenues for further improvement and exploration. | Matokeo ya utafiti huu yanatoa ushahidi thabiti wa uwezo wa kifaa cha kusahihisha herufi kwa lugha ya Wolof, na kufungua njia za uboreshaji zaidi na uchunguzi. |
| For future research, it would be of interest to study the effect of increasing the lexicon and Misspelled Word Corpus on the spell checker's performance. Furthermore, a comparison of the spell checker's performance with other spell-checking methods used in low-resource languages, such as the Indigenous African languages, could provide valuable insights into the strengths and weaknesses of the current approach. The integration of state-of-the-art techniques, taking into consideration the context, such as those based on machine learning and Deep Neural Networks, into the spell checker could also be explored to further enhance its capabilities. | Kwa utafiti wa baadaye, itakuwa ya manufaa kuchunguza athari za kuongeza leksika na korasi ya maneno yaliyoandikwa vibaya kwenye utendaji wa kikagua tahajia. Zaidi ya hayo, ulinganisho wa utendaji wa kikagua tahajia na mbinu nyingine za kukagua tahajia zinazotumiwa katika lugha zenye rasilimali chache, kama vile Lugha za Afrika za Asili, unaweza kutoa maarifa muhimu kuhusu nguvu na udhaifu wa mkabala wa sasa. Ujumuishaji wa mbinu za kisasa, ukizingatia muktadha, kama zile zinazotegemea ujifunzaji wa mashine na Mitandao ya Neva ya Kina, ndani ya kikagua tahajia unaweza pia kuchunguzwa ili kuongeza zaidi uwezo wake. |
