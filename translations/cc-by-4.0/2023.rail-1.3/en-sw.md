| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| This paper introduces two multilingual government themed corpora in various South African languages. The corpora were collected by gathering the South African Government newspaper (Vuk'uzenzele), as well as South African government speeches (ZA-gov-multilingual), that are translated into all 11 South African official languages. The corpora can be used for a myriad of downstream NLP tasks. The corpora were created to allow researchers to study the language used in South African government publications, with a focus on understanding how South African government officials communicate with their constituents. | Makala hii inatambulisha korasi mbili zenye mada ya serikali katika lugha mbalimbali za Afrika Kusini. Korasi zilikusanywa kwa kukusanya gazeti la Serikali ya Afrika Kusini (Vuk'uzenzele), pamoja na hotuba za serikali ya Afrika Kusini (ZA-gov-multilingual), ambazo zinatafsiriwa katika lugha rasmi zote 11 za Afrika Kusini. Korasi hizo zinaweza kutumika kwa wingi wa kazi za NLP zinazofuata. Korasi ziliundwa ili kuruhusu watafiti kuchunguza lugha inayotumika katika machapisho ya serikali ya Afrika Kusini, kwa lengo la kuelewa jinsi maafisa wa serikali ya Afrika Kusini wanavyowasiliana na wapiga kura wao. |

With these aligned sentences we then provide NMT benchmarks for 9 indigenous languages by fine-tuning a massively multilingual pre-trained language model. | Katika makala hii tunaangazia mchakato wa kukusanya, kusafisha na kufanya korasi zipatikane. | **1 Introduction** | **1 Utangulizi** |
| The advancement of Natural Language Processing (NLP) research in Africa is impeded due to the scarcity of data for training models for various NLP tasks (Nekoto et al., 2020) as well as availability of benchmarks and ways to reproduce them (Martinus and Abbott, 2019). For many South African languages there are still challenges finding easily available textual datasets (Marivate et al., 2020) even if there are many speakers for those languages (Ranathunga and de Silva, 2022). There is a need to focus on development of local language (Joshi et al., 2020) NLP resources. | Maendeleo ya utafiti wa Uchakataji wa Lugha Asilia (NLP) barani Afrika yanakwamishwa kutokana na uhaba wa data kwa ajili ya kufunza modeli kwa kazi mbalimbali za NLP (Nekoto et al., 2020) pamoja na upatikanaji wa vigezo na njia za kuzizalisha tena (Martinus na Abbott, 2019). Kwa Lugha nyingi za Afrika Kusini bado kuna changamoto kupata seti za data za kimaandishi zinazopatikana kwa urahisi (Marivate et al., 2020) hata kama kuna wazungumzaji wengi wa lugha hizo (Ranathunga na de Silva, 2022). Kuna haja ya kulenga katika uundaji wa rasilimali za NLP za lugha za kienyeji (Joshi et al., 2020). |
| This paper builds upon the work of Autshumato (Groenewald and Fourie, 2009; Groenewald and du Plooy, 2010) by creating automatically aligned parallel corpora from government textual data in the 11 official languages of South Africa. While the Autshumato project focused on creating Machine Translation tools for five indigenous languages, the resulting corpora lacked information about its origin or context, limiting its usefulness for other NLP tasks such as categorisation, topic modelling over time and other tasks that require contextual information of the content. Our approach provides more comprehensive data that can support a wider range of NLP applications. | Makala haya yanajenga juu ya kazi ya Autshumato (Groenewald na Fourie, 2009; Groenewald na du Plooy, 2010) kwa kuunda korasi sambamba zilizopangiliwa kiotomatiki kutoka kwa data za kimaandishi za serikali katika lugha rasmi 11 za Afrika Kusini. Ingawa mradi wa Autshumato ulilenga kuunda zana za tafsiri ya mashine kwa lugha tano za kiasili, korasi zilizotokana nazo zilikosa taarifa kuhusu asili au muktadha wake, hivyo kupunguza manufaa yake kwa kazi nyingine za NLP kama vile uainishaji, uundaji wa mada kwa muda na kazi nyingine zinazohitaji taarifa za muktadha wa maudhui. Mbinu yetu inatoa data pana zaidi inayoweza kusaidia wigo mpana wa matumizi ya NLP. |

| **4.2.1 LASER Encodings** | **4.2.1 Usimbaji wa LASER** |

LASER generates sentence representations by encoding sentences into a vector. The vectors serve as a machine representation of the sentence. The vectors can be compared using cosine similarity which outputs a score between zero and one. Cosine scores closer to one indicate high similarity. This score is recorded in the LASER datasets (available in the dataset repository). Vekta hizo hutumika kama uwakilishi wa kimashine wa sentensi. Vekta hizo zinaweza kulinganishwa kwa kutumia ufanano wa kosini ambao hutoa alama kati ya sifuri na moja. | **5 NMT Benchmarks** | **5 Vigezo vya NMT** |
| Minimal aligned sentence corpora, for low-resourced African languages, hinder the quality of NMT models trained from scratch (Martinus and Abbott, 2019; Nekoto et al., 2020; Adelani et al., 2022). Recently Adelani et al. (2022) approached this problem by fine-tuning massively multilingual models, including the M2M100 model (Fan et al., 2021), on a small number of aligned sentences. The M2M100 model is a Many-toMany non-English centric language model trained to translate directly between 100 languages, including five South African official languages (Fan et al., 2021). Adelani et al. (2022) demonstrated how to effectively leverage this model for small quantities of data, to create NMT systems for languages and domains not included in pre-training. | Korasi ndogo za sentensi zilizolinganishwa, kwa Lugha za Afrika zenye rasilimali chache, zinakwamisha ubora wa modeli za NMT zinazofunzwa kutoka mwanzo (Martinus and Abbott, 2019; Nekoto et al., 2020; Adelani et al., 2022). Hivi karibuni Adelani et al. (2022) walishughulikia tatizo hili kwa kurekebisha vizuri modeli za lugha nyingi kwa kiwango kikubwa, ikiwemo modeli ya M2M100 (Fan et al., 2021), kwenye idadi ndogo ya sentensi zilizolinganishwa. Modeli ya M2M100 ni modeli ya lugha ya Nyingi-kwa-Nyingi isiyozingatia Kiingereza iliyofunzwa kutafsiri moja kwa moja kati ya lugha 100, ikiwemo lugha rasmi tano za Afrika Kusini (Fan et al., 2021). Adelani et al. (2022) walionyesha jinsi ya kutumia modeli hii kwa ufanisi kwa kiasi kidogo cha data, ili kuunda mifumo ya NMT kwa lugha na vikoa ambavyo havijajumuishwa katika mafunzo ya awali. |
| Building on their work, we create baseline translation benchmarks for the Vuk'uzenzele and ZA-gov-multilingual datasets, in the government publication domain, by fine-tuning the M2M100 model. To provide our results in context and for comparison purposes we also fine-tune the M2M100 model on subsets of the existing Autshumato parallel corpora obtained from the South African Centre for Digital Language Resources (McKellar, (2021,2,2,2,0,2) (https://sadilar.org). We focus our efforts on providing NMT benchmarks for the low resource African languages in the datasets, as such Afrikaans translations are not included due to the relatively high availability of digital datasets in this language, and we leave this for future work. | Kwa kujenga juu ya kazi yao, tunaunda vigezo vya msingi vya tafsiri kwa seti za data za Vuk'uzenzele na ZA-gov-multilingual, katika kikoa cha machapisho ya serikali, kwa kurekebisha vizuri modeli ya M2M100. Ili kutoa matokeo yetu katika muktadha na kwa madhumuni ya kulinganisha, pia tunarekebisha vizuri modeli ya M2M100 kwenye vikundi vidogo vya korasi sambamba zilizopo za Autshumato zilizopatikana kutoka Kituo cha Afrika Kusini cha Rasilimali za Lugha za Kidijitali (McKellar, (2021,2,2,2,0,2) (https://sadilar.org). Tunaelekeza juhudi zetu katika kutoa vigezo vya NMT kwa Lugha za Afrika zenye rasilimali chache katika seti za data, kwa hivyo tafsiri za Kiafrikana hazijajumuishwa kwa sababu ya upatikanaji wa juu kiasi wa seti za data za kidijitali katika lugha hii, na tunaacha hili kwa kazi ya baadaye. |

| **6 Conclusion** | **6 Hitimisho** |
| Finally, this paper presented two multilingual corpora that are automatically aligned to facilitate the translation of texts between languages. These datasets contribute to an expanding collection of corpora for training African language NMT models that are left out or under-resourced in contemporary NLP research. It is the hope of the authors that these datasets will aid in creating NMT models for low-resource African languages and that the models can be used to facilitate access to translation services, empowering African speakers and writers to communicate more effectively in their native languages. It is also hoped that the datasets will further NLP research into the multilingualism of African languages and contribute to an understanding of the various dialects present in Africa. | Hatimaye, makala hii iliwasilisha korasi mbili za lugha nyingi ambazo zimelinganishwa kiotomatiki ili kuwezesha tafsiri ya maandishi kati ya lugha. Seti hizi za data zinachangia mkusanyiko unaopanuka wa korasi kwa ajili ya kufunza miundo ya NMT ya lugha za Kiafrika ambazo zimeachwa nje au zenye rasilimali pungufu katika utafiti wa kisasa wa NLP. Ni matumaini ya waandishi kwamba seti hizi za data zitasaidia katika kuunda modeli za NMT kwa Lugha za Afrika zenye rasilimali chache na kwamba modeli hizo zinaweza kutumika kuwezesha upatikanaji wa huduma za tafsiri, zikiwawezesha wazungumzaji na waandishi wa Kiafrika kuwasiliana kwa ufanisi zaidi katika lugha zao za asili. Pia inatarajiwa kwamba seti za data zitaimarisha utafiti wa NLP kuhusu wingilugha wa Lugha za Afrika na kuchangia uelewa wa lahaja mbalimbali zilizopo Afrika. |
| **7 Limitations** | **7 Mapungufu** |
| The NMT models discussed have been created for benchmarking the described datatsets and have not been exhaustively quality tested for production purposes. We also only tested the effectiveness of fine-tuning the M2M100 model with English as a target language and have have not extended the NMT systems to translations between indigenous South African languages, therefore further benchmarking still needs to be implemented. We would like to extend testing to include the evaluation set created by (McKellar and Puttkammer, 2020), which contains data (excluded from the Autshumato corpora) for all 11 official South African languages and could provide a more accurate comparison of the NMT models. It is noted that while the BLEU score results are promising, a qualitative linguistic analysis still needs to done on the translation models to determine if the BLEU scores for certain language translations (i.e. 'tso-eng') correlate to accurate translations within our domain. As future work we hope to collaborate with respective linguists to improve the quality and effectiveness of such NMT systems for South African Languages (Skosana and Mlambo, 2021). | Modeli za NMT zilizojadiliwa zimeundwa kwa ajili ya kuweka alama za kulinganisha kwenye seti za data zilizoelezwa na hazijajaribiwa kwa kina kwa ubora kwa madhumuni ya uzalishaji. Pia tulijaribu tu ufanisi wa kurekebisha vizuri modeli ya M2M100 kwa Kiingereza kama lugha lengwa na hatujapanua mifumo ya NMT kwa tafsiri kati ya Lugha za Afrika za asili za Afrika Kusini, kwa hiyo uwekaji alama zaidi wa kulinganisha bado unahitaji kutekelezwa. Tungependa kupanua upimaji ili kujumuisha seti ya tathmini iliyoundwa na (McKellar na Puttkammer, 2020), ambayo ina data (iliyotengwa kutoka kwa korasi za Autshumato) kwa lugha zote 11 rasmi za Afrika Kusini na inaweza kutoa ulinganisho sahihi zaidi wa modeli za NMT. Inabainishwa kwamba ingawa matokeo ya alama za BLEU yanaahidi, uchambuzi wa kiisimu wa kimaelezo bado unahitaji kufanywa kwenye modeli za tafsiri ili kubaini kama alama za BLEU kwa tafsiri za lugha fulani (yaani 'tso-eng') zinahusiana na tafsiri sahihi ndani ya kikoa chetu. Kama kazi ya baadaye tunatumaini kushirikiana na wanaisimu husika ili kuboresha ubora na ufanisi wa mifumo hiyo ya NMT kwa Lugha za Afrika Kusini (Skosana na Mlambo, 2021). |
| **8 Ethics Statement** | **8 Taarifa ya Maadili** |
| The datasets created and used for the translation model benchmarks are taken solely from South African government resources. Therefore it is highlighted that if these models are used in production, they might ignore certain social/societal structures and will be representative of the dominant political party at the time the datasets were sourced (Bender et al., 2021). We also note that the benchmark models and datasets have not been curated to determine any biases that are present. As such, any existing biases in the system might have the potential to harm specific groups, when used in NLP downstream production tasks (Bender et al., 2021). | Seti za data zilizoundwa na kutumika kwa alama za kulinganisha za modeli za tafsiri zimechukuliwa pekee kutoka kwa rasilimali za serikali ya Afrika Kusini. Kwa hiyo inasisitizwa kwamba ikiwa modeli hizi zitatumika katika uzalishaji, zinaweza kupuuza baadhi ya miundo ya kijamii/ya kijamii na zitakuwa kiwakilishi cha chama kikuu cha kisiasa wakati seti za data zilipokusanywa (Bender et al., 2021). Pia tunabainisha kwamba modeli za benchmark na seti za data hazijapangwa ili kubaini upendeleo wowote uliopo. Kwa hivyo, upendeleo wowote uliopo katika mfumo unaweza kuwa na uwezo wa kudhuru makundi maalum, unapotumika katika kazi za uzalishaji za NLP za mkondo wa chini (Bender et al., 2021). |
