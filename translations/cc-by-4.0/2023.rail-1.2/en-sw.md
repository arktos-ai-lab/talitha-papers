| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| In this study, we investigate the effectiveness of using cross-lingual word embeddings for zero-shot transfer learning between a language with an abundant resource, English, and a language with limited resource, isiZulu. IsiZulu is a part of the South African Nguni language family, which is characterised by complex agglutinating morphology. We use VecMap, an open source tool, to obtain cross-lingual word embeddings. To perform an extrinsic evaluation of the effectiveness of the embeddings, we train a news classifier on labelled English data in order to categorise unlabelled isiZulu data using zero-shot transfer learning. In our study, we found our model to have a weighted average F1-score of 0.34. Our findings demonstrate that VecMap generates modular word embeddings in the cross-lingual space that have an impact on the downstream classifier used for zero-shot transfer learning. | Katika utafiti huu, tunachunguza ufanisi wa kutumia upachikaji wa neno kati ya lugha kwa ajili ya Ujifunzaji hamishi wa zero-shot kati ya lugha yenye rasilimali nyingi, Kiingereza, na lugha yenye rasilimali chache, isiZulu. IsiZulu ni sehemu ya familia ya lugha ya Nguni ya Afrika Kusini, ambayo ina sifa ya mofolojia changamano ya kuambatanisha. Tunatumia VecMap, zana ya Programu huria, kupata upachikaji wa neno kati ya lugha. Ili kufanya tathmini ya nje ya ufanisi wa upachikaji, tunafunza kiainishi cha habari kwenye data ya Kiingereza iliyo na lebo ili kuainisha data ya isiZulu isiyo na lebo kwa kutumia Ujifunzaji hamishi wa zero-shot. Katika utafiti wetu, tuligundua kuwa modeli yetu ina wastani wa uzani wa alama ya F1 ya 0.34. Matokeo yetu yanaonyesha kuwa VecMap inazalisha upachikaji wa neno wa moduli katika nafasi ya kati ya lugha ambayo ina athari kwenye kiainishi cha mkondo wa chini kinachotumika kwa Ujifunzaji hamishi wa zero-shot. |
| **1 Introduction** | **1 Utangulizi** |
| In the development of egalitarian Natural Language Processing (NLP) systems, cross-lingual word embeddings are gaining prominence. According to distributional theory, words that appear in comparable contexts have semantic commonalities (Villegas et al., 2016). As a result, word embeddings have paved the way for NLP technology advancement. | Katika uundaji wa mifumo ya uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP) yenye usawa, upachikaji wa neno kati ya lugha unazidi kupata umaarufu. Kwa sababu hiyo, upachikaji wa maneno umewezesha maendeleo ya teknolojia ya NLP. |
| The use of word embedding representation has provided satisfactory performance for many NLP applications and associated applications (Gutiérrez and Keith, 2018). Word embeddings have been used for feature engineering (Tang et al., 2014) and transfer learning purposes (Bataa and Wu, 2019). Translation models, sentiment analysis tasks and classification tasks have all benefited. These improvements have been primarily in high-resource languages, such as English, due to the abundance of labelled corpora in these languages. The lack of labelled corpora in low-resourced languages has come at a disadvantage in advancing NLP technologies within this space. | Matumizi ya uwakilishi wa upachikaji wa maneno yametoa utendaji wa kuridhisha kwa matumizi mengi ya NLP na matumizi yanayohusiana (Gutiérrez na Keith, 2018). Mifano ya tafsiri, kazi za uchambuzi wa hisia na kazi za uainishaji zote zimefaidika. Maboresho haya yamekuwa hasa katika lugha zenye rasilimali nyingi, kama vile Kiingereza, kutokana na wingi wa korasi zilizo na lebo katika lugha hizi. | **2 Background & Related Work** | **2 Usuli na Kazi Husika** |
| The popular methods that have been used to represent tokens as vectors have been Bag-of-Words (BoW), Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) and Pointwise Mutual Information (PMI). However, these techniques create sparse and large matrices and also create vectors that do not capture semantics (Villegas et al., 2016). | Mbinu maarufu ambazo zimetumika kuwakilisha tokeni kama vekta zimekuwa Mfuko wa Maneno (BoW), TF-IDF na Taarifa za Pamoja za Pointi (PMI). Hata hivyo, mbinu hizi huunda matriki yenye vijenzi vingi vya sifuri na makubwa na pia huunda vekta ambazo hazinasi semantiki (Villegas et al., 2016). |
| Innovative advancements have led to the emergence of new techniques using neural networks. These methods include the popular Word2Vec models (Mikolov et al., 2013) and attention based models (Vaswani et al., 2017). These models have achieved state-of-the-art performance on adaption and transfer learning tasks in NLP (Devlin et al., 2018). | Maendeleo ya kibunifu yamesababisha kuibuka kwa mbinu mpya zinazotumia mitandao ya neva. Mbinu hizi zinajumuisha miundo maarufu ya Word2Vec (Mikolov et al., 2013) na miundo inayotegemea uangalifu (Vaswani et al., 2017). Miundo hii imepata utendaji wa hali ya juu kwenye kazi za urekebishaji na Ujifunzaji hamishi katika NLP (Devlin et al., 2018). |
| The disadvantage of monolingual word embeddings is that they struggle with transferring between languages that are dissimilar (Ruder et al., 2019b). Multilingual word embeddings trained on multiple languages have also shown to perform poorly for low-resource languages (Wu and Dredze, 2020). | Hasara ya upachikaji wa maneno wa lugha moja ni kwamba wanatatizika kuhamisha kati ya lugha ambazo hazifanani (Ruder et al., 2019b). Upachikaji wa maneno wa lugha nyingi uliofunzwa kwa lugha nyingi pia umeonyesha kufanya vibaya kwa lugha zenye rasilimali chache (Wu and Dredze, 2020). |
| Approaches for cross-lingual representation learning have been proposed to address this issue (Ruder et al., 2019a). There are two types of cross-lingual word embedding methods: mapping and post-hoc approaches (Ruder et al., 2019a). Initial mapping approaches required the use of a bilingual mapping dictionary between the languages of study, but recent improvements have introduced adversarial methods. Adversarial approaches presume that the input monolingual spaces are isomorphic and hence reducing the requirement of a bilingual mapping dictionary (Ruder et al., 2019a). However, it has been shown that adverserial methods perform poorly when the monolingual embeddings are not isomorphic (Søgaard et al., 2018). | Mbinu za ujifunzaji wa uwakilishi kati ya lugha zimependekezwa kushughulikia suala hili (Ruder et al., 2019a). Kuna aina mbili za mbinu za upachikaji wa maneno kati ya lugha: mbinu za ramani na mbinu za baada ya ukweli (Ruder et al., 2019a). Mbinu za awali za ramani zilihitaji matumizi ya kamusi ya ramani ya lugha mbili kati ya lugha zinazochunguzwa, lakini maboresho ya hivi karibuni yameanzisha mbinu za kishindani. Mbinu za kishindani hudhania kwamba nafasi za lugha moja za pembejeo zina umbo sawa na hivyo kupunguza hitaji la kamusi ya uchoraji wa lugha mbili (Ruder et al., 2019a). Hata hivyo, imeonyeshwa kwamba mbinu za kishindani hufanya vibaya wakati upachikaji wa lugha moja hauna umbo sawa (Søgaard et al., 2018). |
| Downstream NLP tasks have been proven to benefit from cross-lingual word embeddings. For example, cross-lingual word embeddings have increased the performance of machine translation (Conneau and Lample, 2019) and Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging (Kim et al., 2017) tasks as a transfer learning strategy. | Kazi za NLP za mkondo wa chini zimethibitishwa kunufaika kutokana na upachikaji wa maneno kati ya lugha. Kwa mfano, upachikaji wa maneno kati ya lugha umeongeza utendaji wa tafsiri ya mashine (Conneau and Lample, 2019) na kazi za kuweka lebo za aina za maneno (POS) (Kim et al., 2017) kama mkakati wa Ujifunzaji hamishi. |
| VecMap has been used to create a mapping between English and Welsh and the resulting embedding were used to train a zero-shot and fewshot learning Welsh sentiment classifier (Espinosa-Anke et al., 2021). In the South African context, VecMap was used to develop a Sepedi-Setswana cross-lingual word embedding and adopted a semantic assessment method to analyse the similarity between pairs of Setswana and Sepedi terms (Makgatho et al., 2021) and used in a noisy, multilingual question-answering challenge to train an LSTM classification model (Daniel et al., 2019). | VecMap imetumika kuunda ramani kati ya Kiingereza na Kiwelshi na upachikaji uliotokana ulitumika kufunza kiainishaji cha hisia cha Kiwelshi cha kujifunza bila mifano na kwa mifano michache (Espinosa-Anke et al., 2021). Katika muktadha wa Afrika Kusini, VecMap ilitumika kutengeneza upachikaji wa maneno kati ya lugha wa Sepedi-Setswana na kupitisha mbinu ya tathmini ya semantiki kuchanganua ufanano kati ya jozi za istilahi za Setswana na Sepedi (Makgatho et al., 2021) na kutumika katika changamoto ya kujibu maswali yenye kelele na ya lugha nyingi kufunza modeli ya uainishaji ya LSTM (Daniel et al., 2019). |
| Zero-shot transfer learning has not been widely used for South African news classification. Marivate et al. (2020) used an annotated corpus of local news items to create a news classifier for Setswana and Sepedi. Instead of using static word embedding as those used by VecMap, using contextual word embeddings from a fine-tuned BERT model has achieved impressive results for a zero-shot learning Named Entity Recognition (NER) task for isiZulu (Wang et al., 2020). However, this work would require significant computing and has not been applied to news classification. To our knowledge, this is the first work that uses cross-lingual word embeddings generated from VecMap to create a news article classifier from English to isiZulu. | Ujifunzaji hamishi bila mifano haujatumika sana kwa uainishaji wa habari za Afrika Kusini. Marivate et al. (2020) walitumia korasi yenye maelezo ya vipengee vya habari za ndani kuunda kiainishaji cha habari kwa Setswana na Sepedi. Badala ya kutumia upachikaji wa maneno tuli kama ule unaotumiwa na VecMap, kutumia upachikaji wa maneno wa kimuktadha kutoka kwa modeli ya BERT iliyorekebishwa vizuri kumepata matokeo ya kuvutia kwa kazi ya kujifunza bila mifano ya Utambuzi wa Majina ya Watu, Mahali na Mashirika (NER) kwa isiZulu (Wang et al., 2020). Hata hivyo, kazi hii ingehitaji kompyuta kubwa na haijatumika kwa uainishaji wa habari. Kwa ufahamu wetu, hii ni kazi ya kwanza inayotumia upachikaji wa maneno kati ya lugha unaozalishwa kutoka VecMap kuunda kiainishaji cha makala ya habari kutoka Kiingereza hadi isiZulu. |

| **3.2 Monolingual Word Embeddings** | **3.2 Upachikaji wa Maneno wa Lugha Moja** |

| **3.3 News Article Classifier** | **3.3 Kiainishaji cha Makala ya Habari** |
| The generated cross-lingual word embeddings are used to train a classifier on the English corpus. We compare four algorithms and assess their performance using cross-validation. The models we train are Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machines, Logistic Regression, and Gradient Boosted Machines. The best model is then used to classify unseen isiZulu news articles in the cross-lingual embedding space. | Upachikaji wa maneno kati ya lugha uliozalishwa unatumika kufunza kiainishaji kwenye korasi ya Kiingereza. | **5 Conclusion** | **5 Hitimisho** |
| In this work we use VecMap to create cross-lingual word embeddings between English and isiZulu. We have shown that VecMap generates modular word embeddings in the cross-lingual space due to the monolingual word embeddings generated by FastText. Hence, we generate cross-lingual embeddings that are used to train a classifier that performs good on English news. However, we were unable to transfer the learning on unseen isiZulu news articles. | Katika kazi hii tunatumia VecMap kuunda upachikaji wa maneno kati ya lugha kati ya Kiingereza na isiZulu. Tumeonyesha kuwa VecMap inazalisha upachikaji wa maneno wa moduli katika nafasi ya kati ya lugha kutokana na upachikaji wa maneno wa lugha moja unaozalishwa na FastText. Kwa hivyo, tunazalisha upachikaji kati ya lugha unaotumika kufunza kiainishaji kinachofanya vizuri kwenye habari za Kiingereza. Hata hivyo, hatukuweza kuhamisha ujifunzaji kwenye makala ya habari ya isiZulu ambayo hayajaonekana. |
| In future work, we would like to examine the performance of VecMap using a larger corpus for isiZulu. Additionally, it would also be advantageous to apply the modularity metric to optimize the hyperparameters of FastText in order to generate appropriate monolingual embeddings for the task. | Katika kazi ya baadaye, tungependa kuchunguza utendaji wa VecMap kwa kutumia korasi kubwa zaidi ya isiZulu. Zaidi ya hayo, ingekuwa na faida pia kutumia kipimo cha umoduli ili kuboresha vigezo vya juu vya FastText ili kuzalisha upachikaji wa lugha moja unaofaa kwa kazi hiyo. |

| We also note that downstream model was trained using European news article titles and that can have an impact on the performance of identifying events that are uniquely for South African news. | Pia tunabainisha kuwa modeli ya chini ya mkondo ilifunzwa kwa kutumia vichwa vya habari vya makala ya habari za Ulaya na hilo linaweza kuwa na athari kwenye utendaji wa kutambua matukio ambayo ni ya kipekee kwa habari za Afrika Kusini. |
