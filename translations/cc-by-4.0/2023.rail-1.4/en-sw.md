| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| This paper describes the SpeechReporting Corpus, an online collection of corpora annotated for a range of discourse phenomena. The corpora contain folktales from 7 lesser-studied West African languages. Apart from its value for theoretical linguistics, especially for the study of reported speech, the database is an important resource for the preservation of intangible cultural heritage of minority languages and the development and testing of crosslinguistically applicable computational tools. | Makala haya yanaelezea SpeechReporting Corpus, mkusanyiko wa mtandaoni wa korasi zilizofafanuliwa kwa ajili ya aina mbalimbali za matukio ya mazungumzo. Korasi hizo zina hadithi za kimapokeo kutoka lugha 7 za Afrika Magharibi ambazo hazijasomwa sana. Mbali na thamani yake kwa isimu ya kinadharia, hasa kwa ajili ya utafiti wa usemi ulioripotiwa, hifadhidata hii ni rasilimali muhimu kwa ajili ya uhifadhi wa urithi wa kitamaduni usioonekana wa lugha za wachache na kwa ajili ya kuendeleza na kujaribu zana za kikompyuta zinazoweza kutumika kwa lugha mbalimbali. |

| **2 West African storytelling traditions** | **2 Tamaduni za usimuliaji hadithi za Afrika Magharibi** |
| In traditional rural societies of West Africa, the acute feeling of loss is related to the diminishing role played by culturally significant discourse practices: even in communities that retain traditional social organization and economy, modern Western cultural practices seep into daily life with new forms of entertainment (television, radio broadcasts) and education (compulsory Western-style schooling). Under the pressure from these new practices, traditional forms of knowledge transmission - including techniques of storytelling and instruction - become unimportant, and may eventually go out of use. | Katika jamii za jadi za vijijini za Afrika Magharibi, hisia kali ya upotezaji inahusiana na kupungua kwa jukumu linalochukuliwa na mazoea ya mawasiliano yenye umuhimu wa kitamaduni: hata katika jamii zinazobaki na utaratibu wa kijamii na uchumi wa jadi, mazoea ya kitamaduni ya Magharibi ya kisasa yanaingia katika maisha ya kila siku kwa aina mpya za burudani (runinga, redio) na elimu (masomo ya aina ya Magharibi yanayolazimishwa). Chini ya shinikizo kutoka kwa mazoea mapya haya, aina za jadi za usambazaji wa maarifa – ikiwa ni pamoja na mbinu za kusimulia hadithi na kufundisha – hupoteza umuhimu, na hatimaye zinaweza kuacha kutumika. |
| In many local communities across Africa, storytelling is more than a favorite pastime. Viewed as a vital part of cultural heritage, it serves as a central medium for the transmission of cultural knowledge. Storytelling traditions have accumulated special linguistic techniques that respond to the needs of specific practices of textual production and performance. As storytelling traditions vanish with older generations of speakers, they take along with them an array of linguistic tools on which such specialized techniques relied (Nikitina, 2018). | Katika jamii nyingi za kienyeji kote Afrika, usimulizi wa hadithi ni zaidi ya burudani pendwa. Ukizingatiwa kama sehemu muhimu ya urithi wa kitamaduni, hutumika kama chombo kikuu cha upitishaji wa maarifa ya kitamaduni. Maadili ya kusimulia hadithi yamekusanya mbinu maalum za lugha zinazojibu mahitaji ya mazoezi maalum ya uzalishaji na utendaji wa maandishi. Kadiri tamaduni za usimulizi wa hadithi zinavyotoweka pamoja na vizazi vya wazee wa wazungumzaji, hubeba pamoja nazo safu ya zana za kiisimu ambazo mbinu hizo maalum zilitegemea (Nikitina, 2018). |
| While oral traditions of West Africa have received considerable attention from anthropologists (Finnegan, 1970, 2012), their linguistic aspects have not been subject to systematic investigation. Our knowledge of the special ways in which language is used in traditional genres is largely limited to observations of frequent use of special vocabulary and opening/closing formulae (Cosentino, 1980), singing (Innes, 1965; Burnim, 1976; Azuonye, 1999), and various forms of repetition (Finnegan, 1967, 1977). The goal of our database is to start filling this gap using an interdisciplinary approach combining rigorous analysis of primary data with meticulous attention to genre characteristics and culture-specific contexts of textual production. | Ingawa mila za mdomo za Afrika Magharibi zimepata umakini mkubwa kutoka kwa wanaanthropolojia (Finnegan, 1970, 2012), vipengele vyao vya lugha havijafanyiwa uchunguzi wa kimfumo. Uelele wetu wa njia maalum ambazo lugha hutumika katika aina za jadi unazuiwa kwa kiasi kikubwa kwa uchunguzi wa matumizi mara kwa mara ya istilahi maalum na fomula za kufungua/kufunga (Cosentino, 1980), uimbaji (Innes, 1965; Burnim, 1976; Azuonye, 1999), na aina mbalimbali za kurudia (Finnegan, 1967, 1977). Lengo la database yetu ni kuanza kujaza pengo hili kwa kutumia mbinu ya kisayansi inayounganisha uchambuzi wa kina wa data ya msingi na kuzingatia kwa makini sifa za aina na muktadha maalum wa utamaduni wa uzalishaji wa maandishi. |

| **4 Workflow and tools** | **4 Mchakato wa kazi na zana** |
| The project unites multiple collaborators that work in different frameworks and use different tools for data documentation and analysis. As a result, we had two basic workflows. In one, segmentation and transcription are done in SayMore (Hatton, 2013) or ELAN (n.a., 2022; Sloetjes and Wittenburg, 2008). Segmented and transcribed texts are glossed in Toolbox or Flex and then imported to ELAN in order to add annotations of reported discourse. The other workflow allows researchers to use only one software product, ELAN-CorpA for segmentation, transcription, glossing and annotation of reported discourse. | Mradi huu unaunganisha washiriki wengi ambao hufanya kazi katika mifumo tofauti na kutumia zana tofauti kwa ajili ya nyaraka za data na uchambuzi. | **5 Annotation of reported discourse** | **5 Ufafanuzi wa usemi ulioripotiwa** |
| Annotation of reported discourse consists of four levels: the function of the construction's elements; the construction's syntactic type; the semantic type of the discourse report; and the encoding of participants within the discourse report. They correspond to four additional ELAN tiers (in our template, qt, rp, typ and par, respectively). Figure 1 is an example of our annotation of a Gizey sentence in an ELAN file. | Uchambuzi wa maelezo yaliyoripotiwa unajumuisha viwango vinne: kazi ya vipengele vya muundo; aina ya sintaksia ya muundo; aina ya maana ya ripoti ya maelezo; na uandishi wa washiriki ndani ya ripoti ya maelezo. Hizi zinalingana na ngazi nne za ziada za ELAN (kwenye mfano wetu, qt, rp, typ na par, mtawalia). Kielelezo 1 ni mfano wa ufafanuzi wetu wa sentensi ya Kigizey katika faili ya ELAN. |
| Explained below are the basic principles of annotation that are relevant to searching in the corpus interface. | Yaliyoelezwa hapa chini ni kanuni za msingi za ufafanuzi ambazo zinafaa kwa utafutaji katika kiolesura cha korasi. |
| A reported speech construction consists of different elements; for example, in John said: Hello! the reported utterance (Hello!) is introduced by a clause describing the speech event (John said). In the Gizey example in Figure 1, "she says" is expressed by a Quotative, while "give millet; this red mare of mine..." is a Discourse report. The semantic type of the Discourse report is Command. | Muundo wa usemi ulioripotiwa unajumuisha vipengele tofauti; kwa mfano, katika John alisema: Hello! usemi ulioripotiwa (Hello!) unatambulishwa na kishazi kinachoelezea tukio la usemi (John alisema). Katika mfano wa Gizey katika Kielelezo 1, "anasema" inaonyeshwa na Quotative, wakati "toa mchele; hii nyekundu mare yangu..." ni ripoti ya hotuba. Aina ya semantiki ya ripoti ya Diskosi ni Amri. |

| **6 Searching in Tsakorpus** | **6 Kutafuta katika Tsakorpus** |
| Equipping the annotated corpora with a web-based search interface makes them more accessible both to linguists and to language communities. We made our corpora available online with the help of the Tsakorpus platform. The platform was mostly developed independently of the project. However, a number of features were added specifically to accommodate the needs of the SpeechReporting database. | Kuwapa korasi zilizofafanuliwa kiolesura cha utafutaji cha wavuti huwafanya zipatikane kwa urahisi zaidi kwa wanaisimu na kwa jamii za lugha. Tuliweka korasi zetu mtandaoni kwa msaada wa jukwaa la Tsakorpus. Sehemu kubwa ya jukwaa hilo ilibuniwa bila kutegemea mradi huo. Hata hivyo, vipengele kadhaa viliongezwa mahususi ili kukidhi mahitaji ya hifadhidata ya SpeechReporting. |
| Search queries in the online interface are formed by clicking on buttons and filling out text fields. A single-word query can include constraints on the word, its lemma, its part of speech and/or its glosses. All fields can handle Boolean functions (, for AND, \| for OR, ~ for NOT). Word and lemma search can include regular expressions and provide instant suggestions when the user starts typing. Multi-word queries consist of several single-word queries with additional distance constraints. | Hoja za utafutaji katika kiolesura cha mtandaoni huundwa kwa kubofya vitufe na kujaza sehemu za maandishi. Hoja ya neno moja inaweza kujumuisha vikwazo kuhusu neno, lemma yake, aina zake za maneno na/au maelezo yake. Sehemu zote zinaweza kushughulikia vitendakazi vya Boolean (, kwa AND, \| kwa OR, ~ kwa NOT). Utafutaji wa neno na lemma unaweza kujumuisha semi za kawaida na kutoa mapendekezo ya papo hapo wakati mtumiaji anapoanza kuandika. Maswali ya maneno mengi yanajumuisha maswali kadhaa ya neno moja pamoja na vikwazo vya umbali vya ziada. |
| When clicking "Search sentences", the user gets randomly ordered search hits, split into pages. The sound associated with a particular search hit, if any, can be played by clicking on that hit. | Unapo bonyeza "Tafuta sentensi", mtumiaji hupata matokeo ya utafutaji yaliyopangwa kwa nasibu, yakigawanywa katika kurasa. Sauti inayohusishwa na hit fulani ya utafutaji, kama ipo, inaweza kuchezwa kwa kubofya kwenye hit hiyo. |
| One limitation of Tsakorpus is that its basic search unit is a sentence (or any sentence-like segment of text). It is not possible to search for units that are either larger than a "sentence", or smaller than a "sentence" but larger than a word. ELAN segments (which normally represent intonational units) were reinterpreted as "sentences" in Tsakorpus. However, our discourse annotation often consists of multi-word spans that are either smaller than a sentence or transcend the segment sentence. In order to make them searchable, we add values of all discourse annotations that appear anywhere within a sentence as sentence-level metadata. This way, a query like "Quotative AND a word glossed as say" will return all sentences that contain both a Quotative span and a word glossed as "say", but they will not necessarily overlap. This option was added to Tsakorpus in the course of the project. Nevertheless, the exact spans inside sentences that have discourse annotations are highlighted with different colors in the search hits. | Kizuizi kimoja cha Tsakorpus ni kwamba kitengo chake cha msingi cha utafutaji ni sentensi (au sehemu yoyote ya maandishi inayofanana na sentensi). Haiwezekani kutafuta vitengo ambavyo ni vikubwa kuliko "sentensi", au vidogo kuliko "sentensi" lakini vikubwa kuliko neno. Sehemu za ELAN (ambazo kwa kawaida zinawakilisha vitengo vya kiimbo) zilifasiriwa upya kama "sentensi" katika Tsakorpus. Ili kuzifanya ziweze kutafutwa, tunaongeza thamani za maelezo yote ya diskosi yanayojitokeza popote ndani ya sentensi kama metadata ya kiwango cha sentensi. Kwa njia hii, swali kama "Quotative NA neno lenye glosi ya say" litarudisha sentensi zote zilizo na kipande cha Quotative na neno lenye glosi ya "say", lakini si lazima vipishane. Chaguo hili liliongezwa kwenye Tsakorpus wakati wa mradi. Hata hivyo, mistari hususa iliyo ndani ya sentensi zenye maelezo ya hotuba inakaziwa kwa rangi tofauti - tofauti katika sehemu za utafutaji. |

When searching in the corpora, the user can choose between selecting a specific language and searching in all language sub-corpora at once. In the latter case, the search query must include annotation that is uniform across the sub-corpora. This includes annotation of reported discourse (see Section 5) and part-of-speech tags in the UD format (de Marneffe et al., 2021). | Korpusi ya mtandaoni inajumuisha lugha zote zinazopatikana katika hifadhidata ya ripoti za Discourse. Wakati wa kutafuta katika korasi, mtumiaji anaweza kuchagua kati ya kuchagua lugha maalum na kutafuta katika korasi ndogo zote za lugha kwa wakati mmoja. Katika hali ya pili, hoja ya utafutaji lazima ijumuishe ufafanuzi ambao ni sawa katika korasi ndogo zote. Hii inajumuisha ufafanuzi wa usemi ulioripotiwa (tazama Sehemu ya 5) na vitambulisho vya aina za maneno katika muundo wa UD (de Marneffe et al., 2021). |
| Currently, the corpus interface is available in English and Russian. The French interface is under construction. | Kwa sasa, kiolesura cha korasi kinapatikana kwa Kiingereza na Kirusi. Kiolesura cha Kifaransa kiko katika ujenzi. |
| **7 Dissemination** | **7 Usambazaji** |
| Target users of the SpeechReporting Corpus are linguists and anthropologists who are interested in traditional narratives. | Watumiaji walengwa wa SpeechReporting Corpus ni wanaisimu na wanaanthropolojia ambao wanavutiwa na masimulizi ya kimapokeo. |
| Besides academic uses, this corpus is a valuable source of materials for language communities to keep their languages and linguistic traditions alive; first of all, by simply having online access to recorded narration sessions of some of their folktales. In addition, we make materials, such as story-books, that the communities can use for educational purposes. Moreover, our project has received additional funding from the Humanités Numériques et Science Ouverte program of the Sorbonne Nouvelle in Paris for producing animated YouTube videos of the recorded folktales and spreading them among the linguistic communities and wider audiences. | Mbali na matumizi ya kitaaluma, korasi hii ni chanzo muhimu cha nyenzo kwa jamii za lugha ili kudumisha lugha zao na mila zao za lugha; kwanza kabisa, kwa kuwa na ufikiaji wa mtandaoni wa vipindi vya masimulizi vilivyorekodiwa vya baadhi ya hadithi zao za kale. Aidha, tunatengeneza vifaa, kama vile vitabu vya hadithi, ambavyo jamii zinaweza kutumia kwa madhumuni ya elimu. Zaidi ya hayo, mradi wetu umepokea ufadhili wa ziada kutoka kwa programu ya Humanités Numériques et Science Ouverte ya Sorbonne Nouvelle huko Paris kwa kutengeneza video za YouTube zenye uhuishaji wa hadithi za watu zilizorekodiwa na kueneza kati ya jamii za lugha na wasikilizaji wengi zaidi. |
| Furthermore, the availability of open-access annotated linguistic data of minority African languages is important for the development of machine learning based technologies, which currently under-represent these languages. | Zaidi ya hayo, upatikanaji wa data za kiisimu zilizofunguliwa na zilizo na maelezo ya lugha za Afrika za wachache ni muhimu kwa maendeleo ya teknolojia zinazotegemea ujifunzaji wa mashine, ambazo kwa sasa hazina uwakilishi wa kutosha wa lugha hizi. |
| **8 Concluding remarks** | **8 Kuhitimisha maoni** |
| The SpeechReporting Corpus provides meticulously annotated corpora of low-resourced indigenous languages spoken in West Africa. It also offers a digital representation of reported speech constructions on different levels of analysis (morphology, syntax and semantics), which opens a potential of a new understanding of a range of discursive phenomena. The SpeechReporting Corpus offers open access tools for comparable annotation of data from different languages. It contributes to the accessibility of previously unpublished traditional narratives in indigenous languages spoken in West Africa. | Korasi ya SpeechReporting inatoa korasi zilizofafanuliwa kwa makini za lugha za kiasili zenye rasilimali chache zinazozungumzwa Afrika Magharibi. Pia inatoa uwakilishi wa kidigitali wa miundo ya hotuba iliyoripotiwa katika ngazi tofauti za uchambuzi (morfolojia, sintaksia na semantiki), ambayo inafungua uwezo wa uelewa mpya wa aina mbalimbali za matukio ya mazungumzo. Korasi ya SpeechReporting inatoa zana za ufikiaji huria kwa ajili ya ufafanuzi unaolinganishwa wa data kutoka lugha tofauti. Huchangia kupatikana kwa masimulizi ya kitamaduni ambayo hapo awali hayakuchapishwa katika lugha za wenyeji zinazozungumzwa Afrika Magharibi. |
| **Limitations** | **Mapungufu** |
| One limitation of this corpus is the difficulty of comparing between the different languages. It is hard to identify typologically applicable categories based on limited amounts of data. For example, when deciding on which discourse categories to annotate, we had to make sure that we could use the same vocabulary for all the languages in our sample. | Kizuizi kimoja cha korasi hii ni ugumu wa kulinganisha kati ya lugha tofauti. Ni vigumu kutambua kategoria zinazoweza kutumika kitipolojia kwa kuzingatia kiasi kidogo cha data. Kwa mfano, wakati wa kuamua ni kategoria zipi za mazungumzo za kufafanua, ilitubidi kuhakikisha kwamba tunaweza kutumia msamiati uleule kwa lugha zote katika sampuli yetu. |
| An additional limitation is the possible lack of consistency between the different sub-corpora. The cross-checking of the data is done manually. This is a tedious task that is susceptible to human error, but it is necessary to improve the quality of individual data sets. | Kizuizi cha ziada ni uwezekano wa ukosefu wa uthabiti kati ya korasi ndogo tofauti. Ukaguzi wa data unafanywa kwa mikono. Hii ni kazi yenye kuchosha ambayo inaweza kukumbwa na makosa ya kibinadamu, lakini ni muhimu ili kuboresha ubora wa seti za data binafsi. |
| Furthermore, we have discovered that it is challenging to bring together a perfect team for a project that is both linguistic and technological in nature. | Zaidi ya hayo, tumegundua kwamba ni vigumu kuleta kikundi kikamilifu kwa ajili ya mradi ambao ni wa kilugha na wa kitekinolojia. |
| Another possible limitation is related to transparency. Considering possible future uses of our corpora, we have tried to make the annotations as transparent as possible and have documented them all on our website. | Kikwazo kingine kinachowezekana kinahusiana na uwazi. Kwa kuzingatia matumizi ya baadaye ya korasi zetu, tumejaribu kufanya ufafanuzi uwe wazi iwezekanavyo na tumeyaandika yote kwenye tovuti yetu. |
| Interdisciplinarity is another challenge: the kind of data that is suitable for dissemination in the communities is slightly different from the kind of data that is of primary interest from the point of view of linguistic theory. | Uwiano wa masomo mbalimbali ni changamoto nyingine: aina ya data inayofaa kwa usambazaji katika jamii ni tofauti kidogo na aina ya data inayokuwa na umuhimu wa kwanza kutoka kwa mtazamo wa nadharia ya lugha. |
