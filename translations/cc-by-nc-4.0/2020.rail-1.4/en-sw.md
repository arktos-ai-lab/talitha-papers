| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Setswana language is one of the Bantu languages written disjunctively. Some of its parts of speech such as qualificatives and some adverbs are made up of multiple words. That is, the part of speech is made up of a group of words. The disjunctive style of writing poses a challenge when a sentence is tokenized or when tagging. A few studies have been done on identification of multi-word parts of speech. In this study we go further to tokenize complex parts of speech which are formed by extending basic forms of multi-word parts of speech. The parts of speech are extended by recursively concatenating more parts of speech to a basic form of parts of speech. We developed rules for building complex relative parts of speech. A morphological analyzer and Python NLTK are used to tag individual words and basic forms of multi-word parts of speech respectively. Developed rules are then used to identify complex parts of speech. Results from a 300 sentence text files give a performance of 74%. The tagger fails when it encounters expansion rules not implemented and when tagging by the morphological analyzer is incorrect. | Lugha ya Setswana ni mojawapo ya Lugha za Kibantu zinazoandikwa kwa mtindo wa kutenganisha maneno. Baadhi ya sehemu zake za usemi kama vile visifa na baadhi ya vielezi hufanywa na maneno mengi. Yaani, sehemu ya usemi imefanyizwa kwa kikundi cha maneno. Mtindo wa uandishi wa kutenganisha maneno unaleta changamoto wakati sentensi inapogawanywa katika vipande au wakati wa kuweka tagi. Tafiti chache zimefanywa kuhusu utambuzi wa aina za maneno zenye maneno mengi. Katika utafiti huu tunaenda mbali zaidi kugawanya aina changamano za maneno ambazo huundwa kwa kupanua maumbo ya msingi ya aina za maneno zenye maneno mengi. Aina za maneno hupanuliwa kwa kuunganisha kwa kurudia aina zaidi za maneno kwenye umbo la msingi la aina za maneno. Tuliunda kanuni za kujenga aina changamano za maneno za uhusiano. Kichanganuzi cha kimofolojia na Python NLTK vinatumika kuweka tagi kwa maneno ya kibinafsi na maumbo ya msingi ya aina za maneno zenye maneno mengi mtawalia. Kanuni zilizotengenezwa baadaye hutumika kutambua sehemu ngumu za usemi. Matokeo kutoka kwa faili za maandishi za sentensi 300 yanatoa utendaji wa 74%. Kuweka tagi kunashindwa wakati kunapokumbana na kanuni za upanuzi ambazo hazijatekelezwa na wakati uwekaji tagi na kichanganuzi cha kimofolojia si sahihi. |

| **1. Introduction** | **1. Utangulizi** |
| Setswana is a Bantu language spoken by about 4.4 million people in Southern Africa covering Botswana, where it is the national and majority language, Namibia, Zimbabwe and South Africa. The majority of speakers, about 3.6 million, live in South Africa, where the language is officially recognized. Setswana is closely related to Southern and Northern Sotho languages spoken in South Africa. There have been few attempts in the development of Setswana language processing tools such as part of speech tagger, spell checkers, grammar checkers and machine translation. | Setswana ni lugha ya Kibantu inayozungumzwa na takriban watu milioni 4.4 katika Kusini mwa Afrika ikijumuisha Botswana, ambako ni lugha ya kitaifa na ya wengi, Namibia, Zimbabwe na Afrika Kusini. Wazungumzaji wengi, takriban milioni 3.6, wanaishi Afrika Kusini, ambako lugha hiyo inatambuliwa rasmi. Setswana inahusiana kwa karibu na lugha za Sotho ya Kusini na ya Kaskazini zinazozungumzwa Afrika Kusini. Kumekuwa na majaribio machache katika utengenezaji wa zana za kuchakata lugha ya Setswana kama vile kitambulishi cha aina za maneno, vikagua tahajia, vikagua sarufi na tafsiri ya mashine. |

Setswana as a low resourced language is limiting corpus research pertaining to much needed significant amount of information about a word and its neighbours, useful in further development of other applications such as information retrieval, collocation and frequency analysis, machine translation and speech synthesis, among other NLP applications. Therefore, there is need to develop basic Setswana processing tools that are accurate and usable to other systems. | Setswana kama lugha nyinginezo inakabiliwa na matatizo ya utata kuhusu matumizi ya neno na hii ina athari kubwa katika uwekaji tagi wa aina za maneno (POS). | Parts of speech tagging identifies parts of speech for a given language in a sentence. The output of a POS tagger is used for other application such as machine learning, grammar checking and also for language analysis. The complexity of POS tagging varies from language to language. There are different approaches to part of speech tagging, the most prominent being statistical and rule-based approaches (Brants 2000, Brill 1992,1995 and Charniak 1997). Statistical approaches require test data to learn words formations and order in a language. They work well where adequate training data is readily available. We could not find a readily available tagged corpus to use. We therefore developed a rule based approach. Rule based techniques require the development of rules based on the language structure. | Uwekaji tagi wa aina za maneno hutambua aina za maneno kwa lugha fulani katika sentensi. Matokeo ya kitambulishi cha POS hutumiwa kwa matumizi mengine kama vile ujifunzaji wa mashine, ukaguzi wa sarufi na pia kwa uchanganuzi wa lugha. Utata wa uwekaji tagi wa POS hutofautiana kutoka lugha moja hadi nyingine. Kuna mbinu tofauti za kuweka alama sehemu ya hotuba, zinazojulikana zaidi ni mbinu za takwimu na zinazotegemea sheria (Brants 2000, Brill 1992,1995 na Charniak 1997). Njia za takwimu zinahitaji data ya majaribio kujifunza muundo na mpangilio wa maneno katika lugha. Zinafanya kazi vizuri pale data ya mafunzo ya kutosha inapatikana kwa urahisi. Hatukuweza kupata mkusanyiko wa maandishi uliowekwa tagi unaopatikana kwa urahisi wa kutumia. Kwa hiyo tukaunda mbinu inayotegemea kanuni. Mbinu zinazotegemea kanuni zinahitaji uundaji wa kanuni kulingana na muundo wa lugha. |
| Setswana like some Bantu languages is written disjunctively. That is, words that together play a particular function in a sentence are written separately. For the sentence to be properly analysed such words have to be grouped together to give the intended meaning. There are several orthographic words in Setswana such as concords which alone do not have meaning but with other words they give the sentence its intended meaning. Some of these words also play multiple roles in sentences and are frequently used. Such 'words' includes include a, le, ba, se, lo, mo, ga, fa, ka. Without grouping the words, some words could be classified in multiple categories. This problem has been looked at as a tokenization problem in some studies (Faaß et al 2009, Pretorius et al 2009 and Talajard and Bosch 2006) | Setswana kama baadhi ya Lugha za Kibantu huandikwa kwa njia ya kutenganisha. Yaani, maneno ambayo kwa pamoja yanatekeleza kazi fulani katika sentensi huandikwa kwa kutengana. Ili sentensi iweze kuchambuliwa vyema maneno kama hayo yanapaswa kuwekwa pamoja ili kutoa maana iliyokusudiwa. Kuna maneno kadhaa ya kiothografia katika Setswana kama vile viambishi ambavyo pekee havina maana lakini pamoja na maneno mengine hutoa maana iliyokusudiwa ya sentensi. Baadhi ya maneno haya pia hutekeleza majukumu mengi katika sentensi na hutumiwa mara kwa mara. 'Maneno' kama hayo yanajumuisha a, le, ba, se, lo, mo, ga, fa, ka. Bila kuweka maneno pamoja, baadhi ya maneno yanaweza kuainishwa katika kategoria nyingi. Tatizo hili limetazamwa kama tatizo la utokenishaji katika baadhi ya tafiti (Faaß et al 2009, Pretorius et al 2009 na Talajard na Bosch 2006). |
| Setswana parts of speech include verbs, nouns, qualificatives, adverbs and pronouns. Verbs and nouns are open classes and could take several forms. Studies in parts of speech tagging have concentrated on copulative and auxiliary verbs and nouns because of this (Faaß et al 2009 and Pretorius et al 2009). Most of POS taggers have focused on tagging individual words. However, Setswana has POS in particular qualificatives and some adverbs that are made up of several words and in some cases about a dozen words (Cole 1955, Mogapi 1998 and Malema et al 2017). Setswana qualificatives include possessives, adjectives, relatives, enumeratives and quantitatives. Adverbs are of time, manner and location. | Aina za maneno za Setswana zinajumuisha vitenzi, nomino, vifafanuzi, vielezi na viwakilishi. Vitenzi na nomino ni makundi huru na vinaweza kuchukua maumbo kadhaa. Tafiti katika uwekaji tagi wa aina za maneno zimejikita kwenye vitenzi vya unganishi na visaidizi na nomino kwa sababu ya hili (Faaß et al 2009 na Pretorius et al 2009). Vingi vya vitambulishi vya POS vimejikita kwenye kuweka alama kwa maneno binafsi. Hata hivyo, Setswana ina POS hasa visifa na baadhi ya vielezi ambavyo vinaundwa na maneno kadhaa na katika baadhi ya visa takriban dazeni ya maneno (Cole 1955, Mogapi 1998 na Malema et al 2017). Visivyo na sifa vya Setswana vinajumuisha vya umiliki, viambishi sifa, vya uhusiano, vya kuhesabu na vya kiasi. † Vielezi ni vya wakati, namna na mahali. |
| A few studies have been done on tokenization and parts of speech tagging for Setswana and Northern Sotho which is closely related to Setswana (Faaß et al 2009 and Malema et al 2017). These studies have not covered tokenization of complex parts of speech. Adverbs, possessives and relatives have a recursive structure which allows them to be extended resulting in complex structures containing several POS. Complex in this case, we mean in terms of length and use of multiple POS to build one part of speech. | Tafiti chache zimefanywa kuhusu ugawaji wa maneno na uwekaji tagi wa aina za maneno kwa Setswana na Kisotho cha Kaskazini ambacho kinahusiana kwa karibu na Setswana (Faaß et al 2009 na Malema et al 2017). | This paper investigates identification of Setswana complex qualificatives and adverbs using part of speech tagger. We present basic rules on how to identify complex POS such as adverbs, possessives and relatives. The proposed method tags single words and then builds complex tags based on developed expansion rules. The rules have been tested for relatives and preliminary results show that most rules are consistent and work most of the time. | Karatasi hii inachunguza utambuzi wa vivumishi changamano na vielezi vya Setswana kwa kutumia kitambulishi cha aina za maneno. | **2. Setswana Complex POS** | **2. POS Changamano za Setswana** |
| As stated above adverbs, possessives and relatives have a recursive structure that allow a simple POS to be extended into a complex POS. We have noted that in Setswana sentence structures, the verb can be followed by noun (object) or an adverb as also stated in the structure of Setswana noun and verb phrases (Letsholo and Matlhaku 2014). We have also noted that nouns could be followed by qualificatives. Thus a simple sentence could be expanded by stating the object the verb is acting on and how, where and when the verb action is performed. The object could be described by using qualificatives and demonstratives. | Kama ilivyoelezwa hapo juu, vielezi, vimilikishi na virejeshi vina muundo wa kujirudia unaoruhusu POS rahisi kupanuliwa kuwa POS changamano. | **5. Conclusions** | **5. Hitimisho** |
| In this paper we presented a rule based approach to identifying Setswana complex parts of speech. The idea is to implement the recursive structure of complex parts of speech. The recursive structure is expressed in the form of rules which are based on simple verb and noun phrase structures. A prototype tagger was developed with the help of Python NLTK regular expressions. Preliminary results show that the proposed technique works well. However, for it to be effective, all the rules and structures of complex POS must be documented. In this study we did not exhaust all relative structures. We plan to develop the idea further by developing more rules and include other parts of speech. | Katika makala hii tuliwasilisha mbinu inayotegemea kanuni ya kutambua aina changamano za maneno za Setswana. Wazo ni kutekeleza muundo wa kujirudia wa aina changamano za maneno. Muundo wa kujirudia unaonyeshwa kwa namna ya kanuni ambazo zinategemea miundo sahili ya virai vya vitenzi na nomino. Kifaa cha awali cha kuweka tagi kilitengenezwa kwa msaada wa misemo ya kawaida ya Python NLTK. Matokeo ya awali yanaonyesha kuwa mbinu iliyopendekezwa inafanya kazi vizuri. Hata hivyo, ili iwe na ufanisi, sheria na miundo yote ya POS changamano lazima iandikishwe. Katika utafiti huu hatukuchunguza kikamilifu miundo yote ya uhusiano.
