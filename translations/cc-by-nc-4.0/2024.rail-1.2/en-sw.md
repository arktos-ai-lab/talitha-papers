| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| This work is part of the Kallaama project, whose objective is to produce and disseminate national languages corpora for speech technologies developments, in the field of agriculture. Except for Wolof, which benefits from some language data for natural language processing, national languages of Senegal are largely ignored by language technology providers. However, such technologies are keys to the protection, promotion and teaching of these languages. Kallaama focuses on the 3 main spoken languages by Senegalese people: Wolof, Pulaar and Sereer. These languages are widely spoken by the population, with around 10 million of native Senegalese speakers, not to mention those outside the country. However, they remain under-resourced in terms of machine-readable data that can be used for automatic processing and language technologies, all the more so in the agricultural sector. We release a transcribed speech dataset containing 125 hours of recordings, about agriculture, in each of the above-mentioned languages. These resources are specifically designed for Automatic Speech Recognition purpose, including traditional approaches. To build such technologies, we provide textual corpora in Wolof and Pulaar, and a pronunciation lexicon containing 49,132 entries from the Wolof dataset. | Kazi hii ni sehemu ya mradi wa Kallaama, ambao lengo lake ni kuzalisha na kusambaza korasi za lugha za kitaifa kwa ajili ya maendeleo ya teknolojia za hotuba, katika uwanja wa kilimo. Isipokuwa kwa Wolof, ambayo inanufaika na baadhi ya data za lugha kwa ajili ya uchakataji wa lugha asilia, lugha za kitaifa za Senegal kwa kiasi kikubwa hazizingatiwi na watoa huduma wa teknolojia ya lugha. Hata hivyo, teknolojia kama hizo ni funguo za ulinzi, uendelezaji na ufundishaji wa lugha hizi. Kallaama inalenga lugha 3 kuu zinazozungumzwa na watu wa Senegal: Wolof, Pulaar na Sereer. Lugha hizi zinazungumzwa sana na idadi ya watu, ikiwa na takriban wasemaji wa asili wa Senegal milioni 10, bila kusahau wale walio nje ya nchi. | **2.1. Description** | **2.1. Maelezo** |
| As mentioned in section 1, no resources are available to build LVCSR systems in Pulaar nor Sereer. Only a small amount exist in Wolof, but none focus on agriculture. The Kallaama project aims to fill this gap by producing several dozen hours of transcribed and annotated localized audio data, to train speech recognition systems in three of the Senegal's main national languages: Wolof, Sereer and Pulaar. | Kama ilivyotajwa katika sehemu ya 1, hakuna rasilimali zinazopatikana za kujenga mifumo ya LVCSR katika Pulaar wala Sereer. Kiasi kidogo tu kipo katika Wolof, lakini hakuna kinachozingatia kilimo. Mradi wa Kallaama unalenga kuziba pengo hili kwa kuzalisha makumi kadhaa ya saa za data za sauti zilizoandikwa na kufafanuliwa za kienyeji, ili kufunza mifumo ya utambuzi wa usemi katika lugha tatu kuu za kitaifa za Senegal: Wolof, Sereer na Pulaar. |
| The choice of these 3 languages was guided by the number of speakers in the country. There are around 5 million native speakers of Wolof, 3.5 million native speakers of Pulaar and 1.3 million native speakers of Sereer (Leclerc, 2023), which represent three quarter of the total population. These are the 3 most widely spoken languages in Senegal, and they are also spoken cross several borders. | Uchaguzi wa lugha hizi 3 uliongozwa na idadi ya wazungumzaji nchini. Kuna takriban milioni 5 za wazungumzaji asilia wa Wolof, milioni 3.5 za wazungumzaji asilia wa Pulaar na milioni 1.3 za wazungumzaji asilia wa Sereer (Leclerc, 2023), ambazo zinawakilisha robo tatu ya jumla ya idadi ya watu. Hizi ndizo lugha 3 zinazozungumzwa zaidi nchini Senegal, na pia zinazungumzwa kuvuka mipaka kadhaa. |

Speech recognition is the main technological barrier to be overcome to develop voice-based services for people with little or no literacy. Agriculture plays an important role in rural activities in Senegal. It is one of the pillars of the Senegalese economy, estimated to contribute 15% of GDP in 2022 as mentioned in the Annual Agricultural Survey of DAPSA (2023), and a large proportion of the population remains directly dependent on it. | Data zinazozalishwa ni matamko ya asili, ya hiari, yenye msamiati katika muktadha, yaliyoundwa ili kuendeleza mifano ya utambuzi wa usemi ya msamiati mkubwa, hasa inayohusiana na kikoa cha kilimo. | **3.1. Wolof** | **3.1. Wolof** |

| **3.2. Pulaar** | **3.2. Pulaar** |
| Pulaar is part of the Fulfulde languages. Fulfulde is spoken in about 20 sub-Saharan African countries, by nearly 30 million people, from West to Central Africa. "Pulaar" refers to the variant spoken in Senegal. Pulaar is the native language of about 3.5 millon of the Senegalese people, making it the second most widely spoken language in the country. | **3.3. Sereer** | **3.3. Sereer** |

| **5. Collection methodology** | **5. Mbinu ya ukusanyaji** |
| **5.1. Audio recordings and transcriptions** | **5.1. Rekodi za sauti na maandishi** |
| Audio recordings. The recordings are about agriculture. The recorded consist of farmers, agricultural advisers, and agri-food business managers. All the data is produced by Jokalante, a Senegalese company specialising in the dissemination of information about agriculture in local languages. Type of recordings comprise interactive radio programmes, focus groups, voice messages, push messages and interviews. Therefore, spontaneous speech is prevailing. Quality of audio may vary depending on the type of programme. For instance, focus group are made outdoor and so noises may arise from the outside (cars, wind, birds, additional voices). In radio programmes, music and jingles sometimes also appear. A selection from these recordings were transcribed, resulting in over a hundred hours of spontaneous speech in the three targeted languages (see Section 6.1). | Rekodi za sauti. Rekodi zinahusu kilimo. Waliorekodiwa wanajumuisha wakulima, washauri wa kilimo, na wasimamizi wa biashara za kilimo na chakula. Data zote zinatolewa na Jokalante, kampuni ya Kisenegali inayobobea katika usambazaji wa taarifa kuhusu kilimo katika lugha za kienyeji. Aina za rekodi zinajumuisha vipindi vya redio shirikishi, vikundi vya majadiliano, ujumbe wa sauti, ujumbe wa push na mahojiano. Kwa hiyo, usemi wa papo hapo unatawala. Ubora wa sauti unaweza kutofautiana kulingana na aina ya kipindi. Kwa mfano, kikundi cha majadiliano hufanyika nje na hivyo kelele zinaweza kutokea kutoka nje (magari, upepo, ndege, sauti za ziada). Katika vipindi vya redio, muziki na vijingle wakati mwingine pia huonekana. Uchaguzi kutoka kwa rekodi hizi ulinakiliwa, na kusababisha zaidi ya saa mia moja za usemi wa papo hapo katika lugha tatu zilizolengwa (tazama Sehemu ya 6.1). |
| Transcriptions. To produce written form of the audio recordings, we asked the transcribers to follow the rules edited by the Centre of Applied Linguistics of Dakar (CLAD), which coordinates the orthographic standardization of the national languages in Senegal. Despite that caution, it was very difficult to obtain a standard form in the writing of languages concerned in the present work. As mentioned by Robert (2022) for Wolof, official rules are rarely used by the population (as example, advertisements are often written with alternative forms), even if an official orthography is established since 1971. The same situation appears for Pulaar and Sereer, and this is primarily due to the fact that the national languages are taught very little in the education system. In addition, the transcription work involved recordings of spontaneous speech, making the work all the more complex and time-consuming. Transcription task was performed by 3 students in Linguistics, in the language they natively speak. They used the dedicated Transcriber tool to achieve the task. The work took 9 months to complete. Then, 3 qualified experts, specialised in the languages of the transcripts, reviewed a subpart of the transcriptions produced by the students. At first, we were aiming to verify half of the transcriptions produced, for each language. But it was an ambitious goal given the complexity and arduousness of the task required. Nonetheless, nearly 13 hours of speech transcription were checked in Wolof, 11 hours in Pulaar and 11 hours in Sereer within the allotted time. | Unukuzi. Ili kutengeneza umbo la maandishi la rekodi za sauti, tuliwaomba wanakili kufuata sheria zilizohaririwa na Kituo cha Isimu Tumizi cha Dakar (CLAD), ambacho kinaratibu usanifishaji wa tahajia wa lugha za kitaifa nchini Senegal. Licha ya tahadhari hiyo, ilikuwa vigumu sana kupata umbo sanifu katika uandishi wa lugha zinazohusika katika kazi hii. Kama ilivyotajwa na Robert (2022) kwa Wolof, sheria rasmi hazitumiwi sana na idadi ya watu (kwa mfano, matangazo mara nyingi huandikwa kwa maumbo mbadala), hata kama tahajia rasmi imeanzishwa tangu 1971. Hali hiyohiyo inaonekana kwa Pulaar na Sereer, na hii inatokana hasa na ukweli kwamba lugha za kitaifa hufundishwa kidogo sana katika mfumo wa elimu. Aidha, kazi ya unukuzi ilihusisha rekodi za usemi wa kawaida, na kufanya kazi hiyo kuwa ngumu zaidi na kuchukua muda mrefu. Kazi ya unukuzi ilifanywa na wanafunzi 3 wa isimu, katika lugha wanayozungumza kwa asili. Walitumia zana maalum ya Transcriber kukamilisha kazi hiyo. Kazi hiyo ilichukua miezi 9 kukamilika. Kisha, wataalamu 3 waliohitimu, waliobobea katika lugha za manukuu, walipitia sehemu ndogo ya manukuu yaliyotolewa na wanafunzi. Mwanzoni, tulikuwa tunalenga kuhakiki nusu ya manukuu yaliyotolewa, kwa kila lugha. Lakini lilikuwa lengo kabambe kutokana na uchangamano na ugumu wa kazi iliyohitajika. Hata hivyo, karibu saa 13 za unukuzi wa hotuba zilikaguliwa katika Wolof, saa 11 katika Pulaar na saa 11 katika Sereer ndani ya muda uliotengwa. |
| **5.2. Texts collection** | **5.2. Ukusanyaji wa maandishi** |
| Senegalese languages are low-resourced. Very few data in the targeted languages were unearth. First of all, no documents on agriculture were found. Since the observations from Renaudier (2012) who mentioned that the local press is predominantly written in French, with only a few newspapers available in Wolof, and that no press were available in Sereer at the time, the situation remains unchanged. | Lugha za Senegal zina rasilimali chache. Data chache sana katika lugha zilizolengwa ziligunduliwa. Kwanza kabisa, hakuna nyaraka kuhusu kilimo zilizopatikana. Tangu uchunguzi wa Renaudier (2012) uliotaja kwamba vyombo vya habari vya ndani vimeandikwa kwa kiasi kikubwa katika Kifaransa, vikiwa na magazeti machache tu yanayopatikana katika Wolof, na kwamba hakuna vyombo vya habari vilivyopatikana katika Sereer wakati huo, hali bado haijabadilika. |
| Most of the written sources found were in Wolof. The Wolof corpus we distribute is composed of the books, Wikipedia articles (dump from summer 2023), the first book of the New Testament, two historic blogs about Hubert Fichte (a German novelist) and Cheikh Ahmadou Bamba (a theologian), publicly available online articles from newspapers. Written data in Wolof can also be retrieve from open source research projects (in particular, the ALFFA project and Masakhane ). We choose not to add them to our release as they are already clean and easy to get. | Vyanzo vingi vya maandishi vilivyopatikana vilikuwa katika Wolof. | We found a very little amount of writings in Pulaar. | About Sereer, although it is the third most widely spoken language in Senegal, gathering written data poses a significant challenge. Zaidi ya hayo, mwandishi anaonyesha kwamba watumiaji wa intaneti hutumia Kiwolofu kukosoa, kuonyesha chaguo za kisiasa na ushirikiano wa siri, na kutukana. Kwa sababu hizi zote, tulipendelea kutokusanya data za kimaandishi kutoka kwenye mabaraza. |

| **7.1. Writing rules** | **7.1. Kanuni za uandishi** |
| It was particularly hard to find qualified experts for checking the quality of the produced transcriptions. We ask each expert to make a report of their reviews. For Wolof, the expert declared that the work was quite easy. To complete the work within the allocated time, he managed to check nearly 13 hours of speech transcriptions and the conclusion was very encouraging. He noted a very good quality of work, with very few mistakes. In contrast, the two qualified experts hired to review the transcriptions in Pulaar and Sereer declared a tedious work. In spite of this, they manage to verify around 11 hours of audio recordings each. They raised numerous mistakes and warned us that their work would be more about rewriting than simple checking and correction. | **7.2. Spoken dialects** | **7.2. Lahaja zinazozungumzwa** |
| The fact that recordings are produced throughout the Senegal made the transcription work much more complex, because of the several dialectal variations of Pulaar and Sereer which are spoken in the country (see sections 3.2 and 3.3). We selected the audio recordings at the very beginning of the project, before the transcriber hiring. But Pulaar or Sereer transcribers sometimes listened to programmes recorded that they did not understand because of a conversation in a dialect that they do not speak. Consequently, we had to carry out a second recordings collection campaign that took into account the specific dialects spoken by the transcribers. | Ukweli kwamba rekodi zinatolewa kote Senegal ulifanya kazi ya unukuzi kuwa ngumu zaidi, kwa sababu ya tofauti kadhaa za lahaja za Pulaar na Sereer zinazozungumzwa nchini humo (tazama sehemu 3.2 na 3.3). Tulichagua rekodi za sauti mwanzoni kabisa mwa mradi, kabla ya kuajiri wanukuu. Lakini wanakili wa Kipulaar au Kiserere wakati mwingine walisikiliza vipindi vilivyorekodiwa ambavyo hawakuvielewa kwa sababu ya mazungumzo katika lahaja ambayo hawaizungumzi. Kwa hiyo, ilitubidi kufanya kampeni ya pili ya ukusanyaji wa rekodi iliyozingatia lahaja mahususi zinazozungumzwa na wanakili. |
| This process also highlights the need to take account of the semantic subtleties between dialectal varieties, especially when dealing with a particular subject (in this case agriculture, but it could be health or finance) and illustrates the challenges inherent in accurately and exhaustively preserving the meaning of words in these languages. | Mchakato huu pia unaangazia hitaji la kuzingatia hila za semantiki kati ya aina za lahaja, hasa wakati wa kushughulikia somo fulani (katika kesi hii kilimo, lakini inaweza kuwa afya au fedha) na unaonyesha changamoto za asili katika kuhifadhi kwa usahihi na kikamilifu maana ya maneno katika lugha hizi. |
| **8. Limitations** | **8. Mapungufu** |
| Transcription work is a very challenging task, and to produce a transcript from spontaneous speech, when overlapping events occur, sometimes in noisy environments, is even more so. Add to this the use of specialised software that is unfamiliar to the workers, with keyboards that are not adapted to writing the language, and the start of the work becomes even more tedious. | Kazi ya unukuzi ni kazi yenye changamoto kubwa, na kutoa nakala kutoka kwa hotuba ya kawaida, wakati matukio yanayoingiliana yanapotokea, wakati mwingine katika mazingira yenye kelele, ni changamoto zaidi. Ongeza kwa hili matumizi ya programu maalumu ambayo haifahamiki kwa wafanyakazi, yenye kibodi ambazo hazijabadilishwa kwa ajili ya kuandika lugha, na mwanzo wa kazi unakuwa mgumu zaidi. |
| Despite the care of all the workers involved in this project, this dataset contains some transcription mistakes, and the spelling used may not correspond exactly to the expected standards, as pointed out in Section 7. Only one transcriber was selected per language to carry out the transcription work. Perhaps some mistakes could have been avoided if more transcribers were doing the job (supposing this is possible, since the number of skilled people is very limited). But, due to production costs, we have chosen to provide the community with a larger set of transcribed data rather than increasing the number of transcribers. In this way, we have been able to increase the number of subjects covered on agriculture. A larger speech dataset is also more suitable for large-scale studies, such as phonetic and phonological research on Atlantic languages, a field where works lack. | Licha ya uangalifu wa wafanyakazi wote waliohusika katika mradi huu, seti hii ya data ina makosa kadhaa ya unukuzi, na tahajia iliyotumika inaweza isilingane kabisa na viwango vinavyotarajiwa, kama ilivyoonyeshwa katika Sehemu ya 7. Mnakili mmoja tu alichaguliwa kwa kila lugha kutekeleza kazi ya unukuzi. Pengine baadhi ya makosa yangeweza kuepukika ikiwa wanakili zaidi wangekuwa wakifanya kazi (ikidhaniwa hili linawezekana, kwa kuwa idadi ya watu wenye ujuzi ni ndogo sana). Lakini, kutokana na gharama za uzalishaji, tumechagua kuipatia jamii seti kubwa zaidi ya data iliyonakiliwa badala ya kuongeza idadi ya wanakili. Kwa njia hii, tumeweza kuongeza idadi ya mada zinazoshughulikiwa kuhusu kilimo. Seti kubwa zaidi ya data ya usemi pia inafaa zaidi kwa tafiti za kiwango kikubwa, kama vile utafiti wa kifonetiki na kifonolojia kuhusu lugha za Kiatlantiki, uwanja ambao kazi zinakosekana. |
| **9. Conclusion and Opportunities** | **9. Hitimisho na Fursa** |
| In this paper, we present the work carried out to create a transcribed speech dataset on Wolof, Pulaar and Sereer, the 3 most widely spoken languages in Senegal. This dataset comprises 55h of audio recordings in Wolof, 32h in Pulaar and 38h in Sereer, all along with their corresponding transcriptions. We also provide more generic text corpora in Wolof and Pulaar, as well as a Wolof phonetic lexicon along with its G2P model. These resources can be used for setting up traditional ASR systems. | Katika makala haya, tunawasilisha kazi iliyofanywa ili kuunda seti ya data ya usemi iliyonakiliwa kuhusu Wolof, Pulaar na Sereer, lugha 3 zinazozungumzwa zaidi nchini Senegal. Seti hii ya data inajumuisha saa 55 za rekodi za sauti katika Wolof, saa 32 katika Pulaar na saa 38 katika Sereer, zote pamoja na manukuu yake yanayolingana. Tunatoa pia korasi zaidi za jumla za maandishi katika Wolof na Pulaar, pamoja na leksimu ya kifonetiki ya Wolof pamoja na modeli yake ya G2P. Rasilimali hizi zinaweza kutumika kwa ajili ya kuunda mifumo ya jadi ya ASR. |
| As pointed out by many recent studies (Joshi et al., 2020; van Esch et al., 2022; Ruder et al., 2022; Adebara and Abdul-Mageed, 2022), a lot of languages with large speaker populations still are under-represented in natural language processing (NLP) studies and applications, reinforcing inequalities such as knowledge access. We hope that this work will stimulate interest in the development of applications that incorporate the vernacular languages of Senegal, but also that it will be a source of inspiration and encouragement to develop the same kind of resources in order to progress towards the inclusion of languages in the world of AI. | Kama ilivyobainishwa na tafiti nyingi za hivi karibuni (Joshi et al., 2020; van Esch et al., 2022; Ruder et al., 2022; Adebara and Abdul-Mageed, 2022), lugha nyingi zenye idadi kubwa ya wazungumzaji bado hazijawakilishwa vya kutosha katika tafiti na matumizi ya uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP), jambo linaloimarisha ukosefu wa usawa kama vile upatikanaji wa maarifa. Tunatumaini kwamba kazi hii itachochea hamu ya kutengeneza programu zinazojumuisha lugha za kienyeji za Senegal, lakini pia kwamba itakuwa chanzo cha msukumo na kutiwa moyo wa kutengeneza aina sawa ya rasilimali ili kusonga mbele kuelekea ujumuishaji wa lugha katika ulimwengu wa AI. |
| Opportunities offered by this dataset are numerous. From a scientific perspective, the speech dataset released can be exploited for instance, to study phonetic phenomena occurring in a spontaneous context, to study speech interaction, or to study the impact of spontaneous and noisy speech on recognition systems. From a technical perspective, this dataset can be used to solve various AI tasks, including speech modelling (such as speech-to-text or spoken language understanding), automatic response modelling (as QA answering), and language modelling (used from scratch or used to fine-tuned a pre-trained multilingual model). From a technological perspective, it can be utilised to develop speech recognition systems, generic or specific to the agricultural sector, as well as localised conversational agents for answering questions on agricultural topics related to the Senegal context and in national languages. | Fursa zinazotolewa na seti hii ya data ni nyingi. Kwa mtazamo wa kisayansi, seti ya data ya usemi iliyotolewa inaweza kutumika, kwa mfano, kuchunguza matukio ya kifonetiki yanayotokea katika muktadha wa hiari, kuchunguza mwingiliano wa usemi, au kuchunguza athari za usemi wa hiari na wenye kelele kwenye mifumo ya utambuzi. Kwa mtazamo wa kiufundi, seti hii ya data inaweza kutumika kutatua kazi mbalimbali za AI, ikiwa ni pamoja na uundaji wa kielelezo cha usemi (kama vile ubadilishaji wa usemi kuwa maandishi au uelewa wa lugha inayozungumzwa), uundaji wa kielelezo cha majibu ya kiotomatiki (kama kujibu QA), na uundaji wa kielelezo cha lugha (kutumika kuanzia mwanzo au kutumika kurekebisha kielelezo cha lugha nyingi kilichofunzwa awali). Kwa mtazamo wa kiteknolojia, inaweza kutumika kutengeneza mifumo ya utambuzi wa usemi, ya jumla au mahususi kwa sekta ya kilimo, pamoja na mawakala wa mazungumzo waliobinafsishwa kwa kujibu maswali kuhusu mada za kilimo zinazohusiana na muktadha wa Senegal na kwa lugha za kitaifa. |
