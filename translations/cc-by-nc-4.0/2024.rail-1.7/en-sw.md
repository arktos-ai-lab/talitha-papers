| This paper presents the first publicly available UD treebank for Tswana, Tswana-Popapolelo. The data used consists of the 20 Cairo CICLing sentences translated to Tswana. After preprocessing these sentences with detailed POS (XPOS) and converting them to universal POS (UPOS), we proceeded to annotate the data with dependency relations, documenting decisions for the language specific constructions. | **2.1. Linguistic Background** | **2.1. Historia ya Lugha** |
| Tswana (ISO-639-3 tsn) is a Bantu language spoken in the north western parts of South Africa, the eastern parts of Namibia which border on Botswana and in Botswana, where it is the national language and most of the people are first language speakers. It is one of the 12 official languages of South Africa and is spoken by 8,3% of the population (Statistics South Africa, 2023), making it the 6th most frequent home language. Next to sign language and two Germanic languages (Afrikaans and English), the official languages comprise nine Bantu languages: Four Nguni languages (Ndebele, Xhosa, Zulu, and Swati), three Sotho languages (Northern Sotho, Southern Sotho, Tswana), as well as Venḓa, and Tsonga. Tswana is classified in the South-Eastern Zone of Bantu languages. These Bantu languages are divided in language groups and Tswana is included in the Sotho language group (group S31) (Maho, 2003). | Tswana (ISO-639-3 tsn) ni lugha ya Kibantu inayozungumzwa katika sehemu za kaskazini magharibi mwa Afrika Kusini, sehemu za mashariki mwa Namibia zinazopakana na Botswana na nchini Botswana, ambako ni lugha ya taifa na watu wengi ni wazungumzaji wa lugha ya kwanza. Ni mojawapo ya lugha rasmi 12 za Afrika Kusini na inazungumzwa na asilimia 8.3 ya idadi ya watu (Statistics South Africa, 2023), na kuifanya kuwa lugha ya nyumbani ya 6 kwa wingi. Especially the last two characteristics are important in the context of syntactic annotation. | Lugha za Kibantu zina idadi ya sifa za kiisimu zinazozifanya ziwe tofauti kwa kiasi kikubwa na lugha nyingi za Kihindi-Kiulaya (van der Velde et al., 2022): zote ni lugha za toni; zinatumia mfumo tata wa ngeli (Katamba, 2003) na mofolojia yao ya nomino na vitenzi ina uambatizi wa hali ya juu na ni yenye tija sana (Katamba, 1993). Nguni languages, on the other hand, have adopted a conjunctive writing system (Louwrens and Poulos, 2006). In this context, a distinction is made between linguistic words and orthographic words. For languages like English or Afrikaans, a linguistic word and an orthographic word largely coincide. For the conjunctively written languages, one orthographic word corresponds to one or more linguistic words. For disjunctively written languages like Tswana, however, several orthographic words can correspond to one linguistic word. | **2.2. Universal Dependencies (UD)** | **2.2. Universal Dependencies (UD)** |
| Universal dependencies (UD) is an international, collaborative project with two main aims: to develop a common framework describing the grammatical structure of the world's languages (de Marneffe et al., 2021) and to create treebanks for various languages applying this framework (Nivre et al., 2020). The project strives to produce cross-linguistically consistent treebanks (with language-specific extensions where necessary) describing syntactic structures as well as morphological features. This framework allows for comparisons between languages (including languages with free word order), research from a language typology perspective as well as the development of multilingual parsers. As stated in the introduction to the UD project: "The annotation scheme is based on an evolution of (universal) Stanford dependencies (de Marneffe et al., 2021), Google universal part-of-speech tags (Petrov et al., 2012), and the Interset interlingua for morphosyntactic tag sets (Zeman, 2008). " The syntactic relations in UD are represented as dependency trees rather than phrase structure trees which makes the annotated data easier to use and interpret in downstream tasks. Currently, there are over 250 treebanks in more than 140 different languages available. | Universal dependencies (UD) ni mradi wa kimataifa, shirikishi wenye malengo makuu mawili: kuunda mfumo wa pamoja unaoelezea muundo wa kisarufi wa lugha za dunia (de Marneffe et al., 2021) na kuunda treebanks kwa lugha mbalimbali kwa kutumia mfumo huu (Nivre et al., 2020). Mradi unajitahidi kuzalisha treebank zinazolingana kati ya lugha (pamoja na viendelezi mahususi vya lugha inapohitajika) zinazoelezea miundo ya kisintaksia pamoja na sifa za kimofolojia. Mfumo huu unaruhusu ulinganisho kati ya lugha (ikiwa ni pamoja na lugha zenye mpangilio huru wa maneno), utafiti kutoka kwa mtazamo wa tipolojia ya lugha pamoja na uundaji wa vichanganuzi vya lugha nyingi. Kama ilivyoelezwa katika utangulizi wa mradi wa UD: "Mpango wa ufafanuzi unategemea mageuzi ya vitegemezi vya (kimataifa) Stanford (de Marneffe et al., 2021), lebo za aina za maneno za kimataifa za Google (Petrov et al., 2012), na lugha ya kati ya Interset kwa seti za lebo za mofosintaksia (Zeman, 2008)." Mahusiano ya kisintaksia katika UD yanawakilishwa kama miti ya vitegemezi badala ya miti ya muundo wa vishazi jambo ambalo hufanya data iliyofafanuliwa iwe rahisi kutumia na kufasiriwa katika kazi zinazofuata. Kwa sasa, kuna zaidi ya treebank 250 katika zaidi ya lugha 140 tofauti zinazopatikana. |
| Our choice of using UD stems from the intended use of UD treebanks that benefits both the computational and linguistic research communities. As de Marneffe et al. (2021) point out, UD needs to comply with a number of (competing) criteria which include a) linguistic requirements, such as achieving a satisfactory level of annotation for linguistic analysis of individual languages and being good for highlighting structural similarities across related languages, b) computational needs, i.e. being suitable for parsing with high accuracy and supporting downstream natural language processing tasks, and, last but not least, c) pragmatic requisites, namely being suitable for rapid, consistent annotation by a human annotator and easily comprehensible by non-linguist users. | Uchaguzi wetu wa kutumia UD unatokana na matumizi yaliyokusudiwa ya treebank za UD ambayo yananufaisha jamii za utafiti wa kikompyuta na wa kiisimu. Kama de Marneffe et al. (2021) wanavyoashiria, UD inahitaji kufuata idadi ya vigezo (vinavyoshindana) ambavyo vinajumuisha a) mahitaji ya lugha, kama vile kufikia kiwango cha kutosha cha ufafanuzi kwa uchambuzi wa lugha wa lugha binafsi na kuwa nzuri kwa kuonyesha utafutaji wa muundo kati ya lugha zinazohusiana, b) mahitaji ya kompyuta, yaani kuwa inafaa kwa uchanganuzi kwa usahihi wa juu na kusaidia kazi za uchakataji wa lugha asilia za baadaye, na, mwisho lakini si chini, c) mahitaji ya kimatumizi, yaani kuwa inafaa kwa ufafanuzi wa haraka na thabiti na mtaalamu wa binadamu na kueleweka kwa urahisi na watumiaji wasio wa lugha. |
| An added benefit of joining an endeavour like UD is the available infrastructure in terms of how-tos on contributing, validation scripts, support from the UD community and the visibility, availability and re-usability of the annotated data through official releases. | Faida ya ziada ya kujiunga na juhudi kama UD ni miundombinu inayopatikana katika suala la miongozo ya kuchangia, hati za uthibitishaji, usaidizi kutoka kwa jumuiya ya UD na mwonekano, upatikanaji na utumikaji tena wa data iliyofafanuliwa kupitia matoleo rasmi. |

| An important premise when annotating universal dependencies is the presence of parts-of-speech (POS), more specifically universal POS (UPOS) (Petrov et al., 2012). | **6.1. Disjunctive Orthography and Verbal Constructions in Tswana** | **6.1. Tahajia ya Kutenganisha na Miundo ya Vitenzi katika Tswana** |
| As has been described in section 2.1, a disjunctive writing style has been adopted for Tswana. Especially for verbal prefixes, this means that a large number of orthographic tokens preceding the verb would in traditional linguistics be seen more as morphemes rather than "proper" words. The proper identification of words is generally taken as an imperative preprocessing step for syntactic description. In this regard, the disjunctively written verbal prefixes of Tswana cause orthographic tokens which are part of linguistic words (Taljard and Bosch, 2006) and therefore compromise the lexical integrity of verbs. Tswana verbal prefixes carry inflectional information while suffixes carry inflectional as well as derivational information (Krüger, 2006, 268). These verbal prefixes also carry both morphological and syntactic information which makes it difficult to assign UPOS tags and syntactic relations to them. | Kama ilivyoelezwa katika sehemu ya 2.1, mtindo wa uandishi wa kutenganisha umepitishwa kwa Tswana. Hasa kwa viambishi awali vya vitenzi, hii inamaanisha kwamba idadi kubwa ya tokeni za tahajia zinazotangulia kitenzi katika isimu ya kimapokeo zingeonekana zaidi kama mofimu badala ya maneno "sahihi". Utambuzi sahihi wa maneno kwa ujumla huchukuliwa kama hatua muhimu ya awali ya uchakataji kwa maelezo ya kisintaksia. Kwa mtazamo huu, viambishi awali vya vitenzi vya Tswana vilivyoandikwa kwa njia ya kutenganisha husababisha tokeni za tahajia ambazo ni sehemu ya maneno ya kiisimu (Taljard and Bosch, 2006) na hivyo kuhatarisha uadilifu wa kileksika wa vitenzi. Viambishi awali vya vitenzi vya Tswana hubeba taarifa za unyambulishaji huku viambishi tamati vikibeba taarifa za unyambulishaji na pia za uundaji wa maneno (Krüger, 2006, 268). Viambishi awali hivi vya vitenzi pia hubeba taarifa za kimofolojia na kisintaksia jambo ambalo hufanya iwe vigumu kuvipangia lebo za UPOS na mahusiano ya kisintaksia. |
| One obvious solution to address this problem is to adjust the tokenisation to reflect linguistic words, rather than orthographic words. More details on the implication of tokenisation is provided in section 6.5. | Suluhisho moja dhahiri la kushughulikia tatizo hili ni kurekebisha ugawaji wa maneno ili kuakisi maneno ya kiisimu, badala ya maneno ya kiothografia. | **6.1.2. Auxiliary Verbs** | **6.1.2. Vitenzi Vya Msaada** |
| An auxiliary verb enriches the meaning of the complementary main verb, copulative verb or another auxiliary verb phrase and can add semantic information regarding the mood, tense, aspect and/or polarity of a verb. For example, the auxiliary verb ne expresses a relative past tense indicating that the action was taking place or had taken place at some point in the past. If the complementary verb is in the present tense then it indicates an action that is incomplete and continuing at a certain moment in the past. | In relation to linguistic analyses: In traditional (structural) grammatical descriptions, the left hand boundary of Tswana verbs is considered to be the first prefix of such a verb, even if it is written disjunctively. This implies that verbs such as ke a mo rata in 2.1 would be tokenised as one word, namely a verb. This verb would constitute a sentence in itself and would be the predicate of the sentence. The syntactic analysis of the sentence would thus not indicate the pronominal value of the subject and object concords so as to indicate that the sentence contains a subject and object (Taljard and Bosch, 2006; Louwrens and Poulos, 2006; Krüger, 2006; Cole, 1955; Pretorius et al., 2015). | **7. Conclusion and Future Work** | **7. Hitimisho na Kazi za Baadaye** |
| This paper contains the description of the first publicly available UD treebank for Tswana, based on the 20 translated Cairo CICLing sentences. The resulting treebank shows that this was a successful first endeavour to apply UD to a Bantu language and forms the basis for further annotation of Tswana to a more extensive set. The main benefit of starting with such a small data set is that many of the most problematic annotations can be discussed in detail, and the corresponding outcomes can be documented without needing a substantial reannotation of the data at a later stage. As would be expected, not all issues have been resolved yet and some decisions had to be made on how to best apply the existing framework to a novel language with unique linguistic characteristics. We hope the detailed report on the issues encountered will also help others when annotating new Sotho and Bantu languages in UD. | Makala hii ina maelezo ya treebank ya kwanza ya UD inayopatikana kwa umma kwa Tswana, kulingana na sentensi 20 za Cairo CICLing zilizotafsiriwa. Faida kuu ya kuanza na seti ndogo ya data kama hii ni kwamba maelezo mengi yenye matatizo zaidi yanaweza kujadiliwa kwa kina, na matokeo yanayolingana yanaweza kuandikwa bila kuhitaji ufafanuzi mpya mkubwa wa data katika hatua ya baadaye. Kama inavyotarajiwa, si masuala yote yametatuliwa bado na baadhi ya maamuzi yalipaswa kufanywa kuhusu jinsi bora ya kutumia mfumo uliopo kwa lugha mpya yenye sifa za kipekee za kiisimu.
