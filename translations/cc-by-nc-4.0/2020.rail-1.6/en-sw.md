| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Linguistic fieldworkers collect and archive metadata as part of the language resources (LRs) that they create, but they often work in resource-constrained environments that prevent them from using computers for data entry. In such situations, linguists must complete time-consuming and error-prone digitization tasks that limit the quantity and quality of the resources and metadata that they produce (Thieberger & Berez 2012; Margetts & Margetts 2012). This paper describes a method for entering linguistic metadata into mobile devices using the Open Data Kit (ODK) platform, a suite of open source tools designed for mobile data collection. The method was incorporated into two community-based language documentation projects in Tanzania, involving twelve researchers simultaneously collecting data in four administrative regions (Griscom & Harvey 2019). Through the identification of project-specific data dependencies and redundancies, a number of efficiencies were built into the metadata entry system. These include the use of closed vocabularies, unique data entry forms for distinct data collector categories, and separate forms for entering participant and resource metadata. The resulting system serves as the basis for the ongoing development of general purpose bilingual English-Swahili metadata entry tools, to be made available for use by other researchers working in East Africa. | Watafiti wa lugha za uwandani hukusanya na kuhifadhi metadata kama sehemu ya rasilimali za lugha (LRs) wanazounda, lakini mara nyingi hufanya kazi katika mazingira yenye ukomo wa rasilimali yanayowazuia kutumia kompyuta kwa ajili ya kuingiza data. Katika hali kama hizo, wanaisimu lazima wakamilishe kazi za uwekaji kidijitali zinazochukua muda mrefu na zenye kukabiliwa na makosa ambazo zinapunguza wingi na ubora wa rasilimali na metadata wanazozalisha (Thieberger & Berez 2012; Margetts & Margetts 2012). Makala hii inaelezea mbinu ya kuingiza metadata ya lugha katika vifaa vya mkononi kwa kutumia jukwaa la Open Data Kit (ODK), seti ya zana za chanzo wazi zilizoundwa kwa ajili ya ukusanyaji wa data za mkononi. Mbinu hii ilijumuishwa katika miradi miwili ya ukusanyaji wa nyaraka za lugha za jamii nchini Tanzania, ambayo ilihusisha watafiti kumi na wawili waliokusanya data kwa wakati mmoja katika mikoa minne ya utawala (Griscom & Harvey 2019). | **1. Introduction** | **1. Utangulizi** |
| Collecting linguistic data to support the creation of LRs for indigenous African languages often involves working with communities in areas where regular access to electricity and internet may be limited. These resource restrictions often lead data collectors to utilize paper-based methods that produce non-digital data which must then later be digitized. Digitization is time-consuming and can introduce additional errors to data, so a method that removes digitization from the workflow has distinct advantages (Thieberger & Berez 2012: 92; Margetts & Margetts 2012: 16). The methods and tools described in this paper enable data collectors, working individually or in teams, to create rich digital metadata in remote regions without the need for a computer or internet connection at the time of metadata creation. | Kukusanya data za lugha ili kuunga mkono uundaji wa LR kwa lugha za kienyeji za Afrika mara nyingi huhusisha kufanya kazi na jamii katika maeneo ambako upatikanaji wa umeme na mtandao wa intaneti kwa kawaida huenda ukawa na mipaka. Vikwazo hivi vya rasilimali mara nyingi husababisha wakusanyaji wa data kutumia mbinu za karatasi ambazo huzalisha data zisizo za kidijiti ambazo lazima baadaye zibadilike kuwa za kidijiti. Uwekaji wa kidijitali unachukua muda na unaweza kuingiza makosa ya ziada kwenye data, kwa hivyo mbinu inayondoa uwekaji wa kidijitali kutoka kwenye mtiririko wa kazi ina faida maalum (Thieberger & Berez 2012: 92; Margetts & Margetts 2012: 16). Mbinu na zana zilizoelezwa katika makala hii huwawezesha wakusanyaji data, wakifanya kazi kibinafsi au kwa timu, kuunda metadata tajiri za kidijitali katika maeneo ya mbali bila hitaji la kompyuta au muunganisho wa intaneti wakati wa uundaji wa metadata. |

There are also multiple linguistic metadata creation tools currently available, such as ProFormA2, Arbil, COMEDI, and CMDI-Maker (Fallucchi, Steffen & De Luca 2019). All metadata creation tools currently available require either a computer or a stable internet connection to function properly. | Metadata za ubora wa juu ni muhimu kwa ugunduzi wa rasilimali (Good 2002), lakini pia kwa kujibu maswali ya utafiti yanayohusisha taarifa za nje ya kiisimu (Kendall 2008; Kendall 2011). Pia kuna zana nyingi za uundaji wa metadata za lugha zinazopatikana sasa, kama vile ProFormA2, Arbil, COMEDI, na CMDI-Maker (Fallucchi, Steffen & De Luca 2019). | **2. Open Data Kit** | **2. Open Data Kit** |
| ODK is a free and open-source software platform for collecting and managing data in resource-constrained environments, and it includes three primary applications of relevance to linguistic metadata collection: ODK Build, a web application for creating custom forms for data entry based on the XForm standard, ODK Aggregate, a Java server application to store, analyze, and export form data, and ODK Collect, an Android application that allows for the entry of data directly into mobile devices. With all three of these components working together, teams of researchers can collect data quickly and simultaneously in remote areas, and all of their data can be compiled together on a single server. Figure 2 shows a schematic of the workflow during data collection: data collectors upload their data to an ODK Aggregate server from their mobile devices, and then a data reviewer compiles the data and exports it from the server for analysis. | ODK ni jukwaa la programu huria na ya chanzo wazi kwa ajili ya kukusanya na kudhibiti data katika mazingira yenye vikwazo vya rasilimali, na inajumuisha programu tatu kuu zinazohusiana na ukusanyaji wa metadata ya lugha: ODK Build, programu ya wavuti ya kuunda fomu maalum za kuingiza data kulingana na kiwango cha XForm, ODK Aggregate, programu ya seva ya Java ya kuhifadhi, kuchambua, na kuhamisha data ya fomu, na ODK Collect, programu ya Android inayoruhusu kuingiza data moja kwa moja kwenye vifaa vya simu. | **3.1 Identifying Metadata Needs** | **3.1 Kutambua Mahitaji ya Metadata** |
| A first step in developing a new tool or method is the identification of research values and desiderata (Good 2010). | The archive deposits for ELDP projects hosted on ELAR use a metadata profile that includes components for deposit, bundle, resource, and participant metadata (Duin et al. 2019). Bundles in ELAR are used to group together different types of resources and participants, so we also needed a method that would facilitate this grouping and correctly categorize resources and participants. The different types of resources include audio and video recordings, as well as text data such as transcriptions and translations. The two categories of participants include researchers and speakers. | Amana za kumbukumbu za miradi ya ELDP zinazopangishwa kwenye ELAR hutumia wasifu wa metadata unaojumuisha vipengele vya metadata ya amana, kifurushi, rasilimali, na mshiriki (Duin et al. 2019). | **3.2.1 Closed Vocabularies and Form Sets** | **3.2.1 Msamiati Funge na Seti za Fomu** |
| Although many components in the ELAR metadata profile are not restricted to a closed set of possible values, within the context of a research project the value of many components is either constant (e.g. target language, project) or restricted to a closed set (e.g. researcher, equipment used). | **3.3 Setting up the ODK System** | **3.3 Kuweka Mfumo wa ODK** |
| Installation of the mobile device and server components of the ODK system was straightforward and did not require significant technical expertise. | During a five-day language documentation training workshop, all local researchers were given instruction on the use of ODK for metadata creation. Local researchers practiced entering data, saving forms, and uploading to the Aggregate server. During the training itself, some minor modifications were made to the forms, including the reordering and rewording of questions, based on feedback from the local researchers. After the training, and once data collection had been initiated, a few additional modifications were made to the forms. For example, an additional question was added to the recording session metadata forms for the local researchers and PIs to create a unique filename for each recording, which includes the recording date in ISO format (YYYYMMDD), a two-letter code for the researcher who created the recording, and an alphabetic system for organizing the recordings based on the order in which they were created. These filenames are used for all of the resource files associated with a given recording. After local researchers experienced repeated difficulties with data management, it was determined that adding the filename question would make it easier for them to bundle resource files after data collection. Follow-up visits to research stations provided opportunities to give continued feedback on metadata collection. Common issues included inconsistencies in the spelling of participant names and misunderstandings about meta-linguistic descriptions such as interactivity and speech genre,. The most frequent mistake initially was simply forgetting to enter metadata, either for a resource or a participant. | Wakati wa warsha ya siku tano ya mafunzo ya nyaraka za lugha, watafiti wote wa ndani walipewa maelekezo juu ya matumizi ya ODK kwa ajili ya uundaji wa metadata. | **3.4 Metadata Processing** | **3.4 Usindikaji wa Metadata** |
| Through the method described here, metadata is entered into mobile devices and then uploaded to an ODK Aggregate server. The data from that server can then be exported as a comma-separated value (.CSV) file, or streamed live to Google Sheets. All of the submissions for each form can be exported together. In order to produce metadata files in the appropriate format for archiving with ELAR, data from the CSV files for participant and resource metadata forms need to be linked together. A Python script was created to produce the final metadata files for each bundle to be deposited in ELAR. A bundle is a group of associated resources and participants. The script compiles information from the resource and participant metadata and identifies the types of resource files associated with each recording session to create a bundle that can be deposited in ELAR. For example, if a given entry in the resource metadata specifies that two speaker-participants were involved in the creation of the recording, then the script uses the participant names in the resource metadata to extract additional metadata for those two speaker-participants from their corresponding entries in the participant metadata, and the script then creates a bundled metadata file using the extracted information. | Kupitia njia iliyoelezwa hapa, metadata huingizwa kwenye vifaa vya mkononi na kisha kupakiwa kwenye seva ya ODK Aggregate. Data kutoka kwa seva hiyo inaweza kisha kusafirishwa kama faili ya thamani iliyotenganishwa kwa koma (.CSV), au kusambazwa moja kwa moja hadi Google Sheets. Mawasilisho yote ya fomu kila moja yanaweza kutolewa pamoja. Ili kuzalisha faili za metadata katika muundo unaofaa kwa ajili ya kuhifadhi na ELAR, data kutoka kwenye faili za CSV za fomu za metadata ya washiriki na rasilimali zinahitaji kuunganishwa pamoja. Hati ya Python iliundwa ili kuzalisha faili za mwisho za metadata kwa kila kifurushi kitakachowekwa katika ELAR. Kifurushi ni kikundi cha rasilimali na washiriki wanaohusiana. Hati hiyo inakusanya taarifa kutoka kwa metadata ya rasilimali na mshiriki na kutambua aina za faili za rasilimali zinazohusiana na kila kikao cha kurekodi ili kuunda kifurushi kinachoweza kuwekwa katika ELAR. Kwa mfano, ikiwa ingizo fulani katika metadata ya rasilimali linabainisha kwamba washiriki-wazungumzaji wawili walihusika katika uundaji wa rekodi, basi hati hiyo hutumia majina ya washiriki katika metadata ya rasilimali kutoa metadata ya ziada kwa washiriki-wazungumzaji hao wawili kutoka kwa maingizo yao yanayolingana katika metadata ya mshiriki, na kisha hati hiyo huunda faili ya metadata iliyofungwa kwa kutumia taarifa iliyotolewa. |
| **4. Method Assessment** | **4. Njia ya Tathmini** |
| The competing goals of achieving representative data volume and data accessibility, collectively described by some as the "reproducibility crisis" (Gezelter 2015), can be addressed within the domain of linguistic fieldwork in at least two ways: the active participation of the speech community in data collection ("crowd-sourcing"), and the strategic use of computational technologies ("automation"). The ODK linguistic metadata method attempts to utilize both solutions, and has a number of notable advantages over non-digital entry methods. The method has the potential to increase the quality, quantity, and consistency of linguistic data and metadata deposited in language archives. It does so not just by reducing processing bottlenecks, which enables linguists to spend more time analyzing or collecting data when they would otherwise be manually digitizing data, but also by opening the door to increased involvement of speech communities in the language documentation process, which has been shown to benefit research outputs by producing linguistic data sets that are more diverse and representative (Czaykowska-Higgins 2009). The system is not without its limitations, however. As with any metadata entry system, open text fields will still contain errors that must be checked either manually or through an automated system of some kind. Additional training and feedback may reduce the error rate, but it is not reasonable to expect error-free metadata with any system that utilizes open text fields. The submissions for updated versions of forms need to be manually compiled together with the submissions for previous versions, at least in the current version of the ODK Aggregate software. The significance of this task depends on the volume and timing of updates made to forms. If forms are submitted through ODK Collect using a previous version that has since been deleted from the device, then those forms can no longer be viewed locally on the device. Again, the significance of this limitation depends on the volume and timing of updates. Perhaps the biggest limitation, however, is that the output of the ODK suite must be formatted according to the metadata profile of the corresponding language archive. This requires some coding and therefore restricts the pool of researchers capable of designing a project-specific implementation to those with coding knowledge or access to someone with that knowledge. | Malengo yanayoshindana ya kufikia wingi wa data uwakilishi na ufikiaji wa data, ambayo kwa pamoja yanaelezewa na baadhi ya watu kama "mgogoro wa urudiaji wa matokeo" (Gezelter 2015), yanaweza kushughulikiwa ndani ya kikoa cha utafiti wa lugha kwa angalau njia mbili: ushiriki hai wa jamii ya wazungumzaji katika ukusanyaji wa data ("crowd-sourcing"), na matumizi ya kimkakati ya teknolojia za kikompyuta ("automation"). Njia ya metadata ya lugha ya ODK inajaribu kutumia suluhisho zote mbili, na ina faida kadhaa za kushangaza ikilinganishwa na njia za kuingiza data zisizo za kidijitali. Mbinu hiyo ina uwezo wa kuongeza ubora, wingi, na uthabiti wa data za kiisimu na metadata zinazowekwa kwenye kumbukumbu za lugha. Inafanya hivyo si tu kwa kupunguza vikwazo vya uchakataji, ambavyo huwawezesha wanaisimu kutumia muda mwingi zaidi kuchanganua au kukusanya data wakati ambapo vinginevyo wangekuwa wakibadilisha data kuwa za kidijitali kwa mikono, bali pia kwa kufungua mlango wa ushiriki ulioongezeka wa jamii za wazungumzaji katika mchakato wa uhifadhi wa lugha, ambao umeonyeshwa kunufaisha matokeo ya utafiti kwa kuzalisha seti za data za kiisimu ambazo ni tofauti zaidi na uwakilishi zaidi (Czaykowska-Higgins 2009). Hata hivyo, mfumo huo haukosi vikwazo vyake. Kama ilivyo kwa mfumo wowote wa kuingiza metadata, sehemu za maandishi huria bado zitakuwa na makosa ambayo lazima yakaguliwe ama kwa mikono au kupitia mfumo wa kiotomatiki wa aina fulani. Mafunzo ya ziada na maoni yanaweza kupunguza kiwango cha makosa, lakini si ya maana kutarajia metadata bila makosa na mfumo wowote unaotumia uwanja wa maandishi wazi. Mawasilisho ya matoleo yaliyosasishwa ya fomu yanahitaji kukusanywa kwa mikono pamoja na mawasilisho ya matoleo ya awali, angalau katika toleo la sasa la programu ya ODK Aggregate. Umuhimu wa kazi hii unategemea kiasi na wakati wa sasisho zilizofanywa kwenye fomu. Ikiwa fomu zinawasilishwa kupitia ODK Collect kwa kutumia toleo la awali ambalo tayari limefutwa kutoka kwenye kifaa, basi fomu hizo haziwezi tena kutazamwa ndani ya kifaa hicho. Tena, umuhimu wa kikwazo hiki unategemea kiasi na wakati wa sasisho. Pengine kizuizi kikubwa zaidi, hata hivyo, ni kwamba matokeo ya programu ya ODK lazima yaundwe kulingana na wasifu wa metadata wa hifadhi ya lugha husika. Hili linahitaji uandikaji wa msimbo na kwa hiyo linapunguza kundi la watafiti wanaoweza kubuni utekelezaji mahususi wa mradi kwa wale wenye ujuzi wa uandikaji wa msimbo au wanaoweza kufikia mtu mwenye ujuzi huo. |
| **5. Towards a Standardized System** | **5. Kuelekea Mfumo Sanifu** |
| One way to decrease the learning curve for the ODK metadata system is to develop a set of standardized general purpose forms, based on one or more common metadata profiles, and an accompanying processing script. The Hadza and Ihanzu community language documentation projects in Tanzania are now serving as the foundation for the creation of such a set of tools. Initially, these tools will be based on the ELAR metadata profile and restricted to English and Swahili interfaces, which should be useful for researchers working with endangered language communities in East Africa. In the future, it is planned to expand the tools to include a French interface and metadata profiles for other common language archives and data repositories. | Njia moja ya kupunguza ugumu wa kujifunza mfumo wa metadata wa ODK ni kuunda seti ya fomu sanifu za madhumuni ya jumla, zikiegemezwa kwenye wasifu mmoja au zaidi ya metadata ya kawaida, na hati ya usindikaji inayoambatana. Miradi ya ukusanyaji wa nyaraka za lugha za jamii za Hadza na Ihanzu nchini Tanzania sasa inatumika kama msingi wa kuunda seti ya zana kama hizo. Hapo awali, zana hizi zitategemea wasifu wa metadata wa ELAR na zitadhibitiwa kwa violesura vya Kiingereza na Kiswahili, jambo ambalo linapaswa kuwa na manufaa kwa watafiti wanaofanya kazi na jamii za lugha zilizo hatarini kutoweka katika Afrika Mashariki. Katika siku zijazo, mpango ni kueneza zana hizi ili kujumuisha kiolesura cha Kifaransa na profaili za metadata kwa hifadhi nyingine za lugha za kawaida na hazina za data. |
| **6. Conclusion** | **6. Hitimisho** |
| The piloted ODK linguistic metadata system offers a number of advantages when compared to manual data entry methods. The removal of digitization and the use of closed-vocabularies increase the accuracy and speed of metadata entry. This is significant because it allows for the creation of large and representative datasets, which are a primary goal of language documentation (Himmelmann 1998; Himmelmann 2006; Woodbury 2003). The scalability of the ODK system also allows teams of data collectors to work together, which can allow for increased community engagement and collaboration. | Mfumo wa metadata ya lugha ya ODK unatoa faida kadhaa ikilinganishwa na mbinu za kuingia data kwa mikono. Kuondolewa kwa uwekaji dijiti na matumizi ya msamiati funge huongeza Usahihi na kasi ya uingizaji wa metadata. Hii ni muhimu kwa sababu inaruhusu uundaji wa seti kubwa na zinazowakilisha data, ambazo ni lengo kuu la nyaraka za lugha (Himmelmann 1998; Himmelmann 2006; Woodbury 2003). Uwezo wa kupanuka wa mfumo wa ODK pia unaruhusu timu za wakusanyaji data kufanya kazi pamoja, jambo ambalo linaweza kuruhusu ongezeko la ushirikishwaji wa jamii na ushirikiano. |
| The system specifically developed for the Hadza and Ihanzu community language documentation projects relies on project-specific and repository-specific closed vocabularies and constant values, but these specificities inform the design of general purpose metadata entry tools. It is hoped that these tools will make the possibility of digital metadata creation a reality for researchers working throughout remote regions of Africa. | Mfumo uliobuniwa hasa kwa ajili ya miradi ya nyaraka za lugha za jamii ya Hadza na Ihanzu unategemea istilahi zilizofungwa na thamani thabiti maalum kwa mradi na hifadhidata, lakini sifa hizi maalum zinaelekeza muundo wa zana za kuingiza metadata za matumizi ya jumla. Inatumainiwa kwamba zana hizi zitafanya uwezekano wa uundaji wa metadata ya kidijitali kuwa ukweli kwa watafiti wanaofanya kazi katika maeneo ya mbali ya Afrika. |
