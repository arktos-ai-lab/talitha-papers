| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| In this paper, we compare four state-of-the-art neural network dependency parsers for the Semitic language Amharic. As Amharic is a morphologically-rich and less-resourced language, the out-of-vocabulary (OOV) problem will be higher when we develop data-driven models. This fact limits researchers to develop neural network parsers because the neural network requires large quantities of data to train a model. We empirically evaluate neural network parsers when a small Amharic treebank is used for training. Based on our experiment, we obtain an 83.79 LAS score using the UDPipe system. Better accuracy is achieved when the neural parsing system uses external resources like word embedding. Using such resources, the LAS score for UDPipe improves to 85.26. Our experiment shows that the neural networks can learn dependency relations better from limited data while segmentation and POS tagging require much data. | Katika makala haya, tunalinganisha vichanganuzi tegemezi vinne vya kisasa vya mtandao wa neva kwa lugha ya Kisemiti ya Kiamhari. Ukweli huu unawazuia watafiti kuunda vichanganuzi vya mtandao wa neva kwa sababu mtandao wa neva unahitaji kiasi kikubwa cha data ili kufunza mfano. Tunatathmini kimajaribio vichanganuzi vya mtandao wa neva wakati treebank ndogo ya Kiamhari inapotumika kwa mafunzo. Kulingana na jaribio letu, tunapata alama ya 83.79 ya LAS kwa kutumia mfumo wa UDPipe. | **1. Introduction** | **1. Utangulizi** |
| Dependency parsing is the task of analyzing the dependency structure of a given input sentence automatically (Kübler et al., 2009). It requires a series of decisions to form the syntactic structure in the light of dependency relations. Nowadays, dependency grammar is gaining popularity because of its capability to handle predicate-argument structures that are needed in other NLP applications (Mcdonald et al., 2005). In addition, dependency grammar is recommended for languages that have free word order (Kübler et al., 2009; Tsarfaty et al., 2010). However, while dependency parsing is adaptable to many languages, it performs less well with morphologically rich languages like Arabic, Basque, and Greek (Dehdari et al., 2011). It is confirmed in (Habash, 2010), that languages like Arabic, Hebrew, and Amharic present a special challenges for the design of a dependency grammar due to their complex morphology and agreement. | Uchambuzi wa utegemezi ni kazi ya kuchambua muundo wa utegemezi wa sentensi iliyotolewa kiotomatiki (Kübler et al., 2009). | Starting from the mid 20 th century, research in NLP has shifted to neural networks. In this line of research, language is represented in the form of non-linear features. The approach is inspired by the the way computation works in the brain (Goldberg, 2017). It is applied in areas such as machine translation, computer vision, and speech recognition. With regards to parsing, the wave of neural network parsers was started in 2014 by Chen and Manning (Chen and Manning, 2014), who presented a fast and accurate transition-based parser using neural networks. Since then other parsing models have employed various techniques such as stack LSTM (Dyer et al., 2015; Kiperwasser and Goldberg, 2016), global normalization (Andor et al., 2016), biaffine attention (Dozat and Manning, 2017) or recurrent neural network grammars (Dyer et al., 2016; Kuncoro et al., 2017). Due to the existence of a treebank for different languages and the shared task of CoNLL 2017 (Zeman et al., 2017) and 2018 (Zeman et al., 2018), large improvements in dependency parsing using neural networks have been reported. For instance, the neural graph-based parser of Dozat et al. (Dozat et al., 2017) won the CoNLL2017 UD Shared Task. In the CoNLL2018 UD Shared Task, the winning system was that of Che et al. (Che et al., 2018). These systems have improved the neural network approach to parsing through the application of optimization functions and external resources such as word embedding. Kutokana na kuwepo kwa treebank kwa lugha mbalimbali na kazi ya pamoja ya CoNLL 2017 (Zeman et al., 2017) na 2018 (Zeman et al., 2018), maboresho makubwa katika uchanganuzi wa vitegemezi kwa kutumia mitandao ya neva yameripotiwa. | **2. Background** | **2. Usuli** |
| A parsing system may use a model which is learned from a treebank to predict the grammatical structure for new sentences. This method of parser development is called data-driven parsing. The goal of data-driven dependency parsing is to learn to accurately predict dependency graphs from the treebank. Following the universal dependency (UD) guidelines, Binyam et al. (Seyoum et al., 2018) developed a treebank for Amharic. In building this resource, they followed a pipeline process. Clitics like prepositions, articles, negation operators, etc. were segmented manually from their host. Then the segmented data were annotated for POS, morphological information and syntactic dependencies based on the UD annotation schema. | Mfumo wa uchanganuzi unaweza kutumia mfano ambao umefunzwa kutoka kwa treebank ili kutabiri muundo wa sarufi kwa sentensi mpya. Mbinu hii ya uundaji wa kichanganuzi inaitwa uchanganuzi unaoongozwa na data. Lengo la uchanganuzi wa vitegemezi unaoongozwa na data ni kujifunza kutabiri kwa usahihi grafu za vitegemezi kutoka kwenye treebank. Kufuatia mwongozo wa utegemezi wa kimataifa (UD), Binyam et al. (Seyoum et al., 2018) walitengeneza benki ya miti kwa lugha ya Amharic. Katika kujenga rasilimali hii, walifuata mchakato wa bomba. Vibandikaji kama vile vihusishi, viambishi, viendeshaji vya ukanushaji, n.k. viligawanywa kwa mikono kutoka kwa mwenyeji wao. Kisha data iliyogawanywa ilifafanuliwa kwa POS, taarifa za kimofolojia na vitegemezi vya kisintaksia kulingana na schema ya ufafanuzi ya UD. |

| **3. Neural Network Parsers** | **3. Vichanganuzi vya Mtandao wa Neva** |
| In recent years, many fast and accurate dependency parsers have been made available publicly. Due to the shared task on dependency parsing and the presence of treebanks for different languages, every year new parsing methods have been introduced (Lavelli, 2016; Zeman et al., 2017). Some of the systems require a lot of resources and large treebanks while others are easy to adapt to new languages that have few resources. With this background, we have selected four off-the-shelf parsing systems to test for Amharic. The systems are: UDPipe, JPTDP, UUParser and Turku. | Katika miaka ya hivi karibuni, vichanganuzi vingi vya utegemezi vya haraka na sahihi vimepatikana kwa umma. Kutokana na kazi ya pamoja ya uchanganuzi wa utegemezi na uwepo wa treebank kwa lugha tofauti, kila mwaka mbinu mpya za uchanganuzi zimeanzishwa (Lavelli, 2016; Zeman et al., 2017). Baadhi ya mifumo inahitaji rasilimali nyingi na treebank kubwa huku mingine ikiwa rahisi kuzoeleka kwa lugha mpya zenye rasilimali chache. Kwa msingi huu, tumechagua mifumo minne ya uchanganuzi iliyo tayari kutumika ili kuifanyia majaribio kwa Kiamhariki. Mifumo hiyo ni: UDPipe, JPTDP, UUParser na Turku. |
| **3.1. UDPipe** | **3.1. UDPipe** |
| UDPipe is an open-source and a trainable pipeline parsing system. It performs sentence segmentation, tokenization, part-of-speech tagging, lemmatization, morphological analysis, and dependency parsing (Straka et al., 2016). After a model is trained on the CoNLL-U format, it performs both sentence segmentation and tokenization jointly using a single-layer bidirectional Gated recurrent unit (GRU) network (Straka et al., 2017). The tasks of POS tagging, lemmatization, and morphological analysis, are performed using the MorphoDiTa of (Straková et al., 2014). | UDPipe ni mfumo wa uchanganuzi wa mnyororo wa mchakato unaoweza kufunzwa na wa chanzo wazi. Hufanya ugawaji wa sentensi, ugawaji wa vipashio, uwekaji tagi za aina za maneno, ulemishaji, uchanganuzi wa kimofolojia, na uchanganuzi wa vitegemezi (Straka et al., 2016). Baada ya mfumo kufunzwa kwa kutumia muundo wa CoNLL-U, inafanya mgawanyiko wa sentensi na kutenganisha alama kwa pamoja kwa kutumia mtandao wa Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) wa tabaka moja unaotembea pande zote mbili (Straka et al., 2017). Kazi za kuweka alama za aina ya neno (POS tagging), kutafuta msingi wa maneno (lemmatization), na uchambuzi wa umbo la maneno (morphological analysis), zinafanywa kwa kutumia MorphoDiTa ya (Straková et al., 2014). |
| The parsing system of UDPipe is based on Parsito, (Straková et al., 2014) a transition-based system which is able to parse both non-projective and projective sentences. For non-projective sentences, it employs the arc-standard system of Nivre (Nivre, 2014). To handle non-projective sentences, it has an extra transition called "swap" that reorders two words. It uses neural network classifiers to predict correct transitions. For the purpose of improving parsing accuracy, it adds search-based oracles. It also includes optional beam search decoding, similar to that of Zhang and Nivre (Zhang and Nivre, 2011). | Mfumo wa uchanganuzi wa UDPipe umejengwa juu ya Parsito, (Straková et al., 2014) mfumo unaotegemea mpito ambao unaweza kuchanganua sentensi zote mbili zisizo za kikadirio na za kikadirio. Kwa sentensi zisizo za kikadirio, hutumia mfumo wa arc-standard wa Nivre (Nivre, 2014). Ili kushughulikia sentensi zisizo na makadirio, ina mpito wa ziada unaoitwa "swap" ambao hupanga upya maneno mawili. Inatumia viainishi vya mtandao wa neva kutabiri mipito sahihi. Kwa lengo la kuboresha usahihi wa uchambuzi, inaongeza oracles zinazotegemea utafutaji. Pia inajumuisha usimbuaji wa hiari wa utafutaji wa boriti, sawa na ule wa Zhang na Nivre (Zhang na Nivre, 2011). |
| **3.2. jPTDP** | **3.2. jPTDP** |
| jPTDP is a joint model for part-of-speech (POS) tagging and dependency parsing (Nguyen and Verspoor, 2018). It was released in two versions; for our experiment, we used the latest version, jPTDP v2.0. This model is based on the BIST graph-based dependency parser of Kiperwasser and Goldberg (Kiperwasser and Goldberg, 2016). Given word tokens in an input sentence, the tagging component uses a BiLSTM to learn latent feature vectors representing the tokens. Then the tagging component feeds these feature vectors into a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) with one hidden layer to predict POS tags. | jPTDP ni muundo wa pamoja wa kuweka tagi za aina za maneno (POS) na uchanganuzi wa utegemezi (Nguyen na Verspoor, 2018). Ilitolewa katika matoleo mawili; kwa jaribio letu, tulitumia toleo la hivi karibuni, jPTDP v2.0. Muundo huu unategemea kichanganuzi cha utegemezi cha BIST kinachotegemea grafu cha Kiperwasser na Goldberg (Kiperwasser na Goldberg, 2016). Kwa kuzingatia tokeni za maneno katika sentensi ya pembejeo, sehemu ya kuweka tagi hutumia BiLSTM kujifunza vekta za sifa fiche zinazowakilisha tokeni. Kisha kipengele cha kuweka tagi hulisha vekta hizi za sifa kwenye perceptron ya tabaka nyingi (MLP) yenye tabaka moja iliyofichwa ili kutabiri tagi za POS. |
| The parsing component uses another BiLSTM to learn a set of latent feature representations which are based on both the input tokens and the predicted POS tags. These representations are fed to one MLP to decode dependency arcs and another MLP to label the predicted dependency arcs. | Kipengele cha uchanganuzi kinatumia BiLSTM nyingine kujifunza seti ya uwakilishi fiche wa sifa ambao unategemea tokeni za pembejeo na tagi za POS zilizotabiriwa. Uwakilishi huu hulishwa kwenye MLP moja ili kusimbua tao za utegemezi na MLP nyingine ili kuweka lebo kwenye tao za utegemezi zilizotabiriwa. |
| **3.3. UUParser** | **3.3. UUParser** |
| UUParser (version 2.3) is a pipeline system for dependency parsing that consists of three components (de Lhoneux et al., 2017). The first component performs joint word and sentence segmentation, the second predicts POS tags and morphological features, and the third predicts dependency relation from the words and the POS tags (Nivre, 2008). The word and sentence segmentation is jointly modeled as character-level sequence labeling, employing bidirectional recurrent neural networks (BiRNN) together with CRF (de Lhoneux et al., 2017). | UUParser (toleo la 2.3) ni mfumo wa bomba la uchanganuzi wa utegemezi ambao unajumuisha vipengele vitatu (de Lhoneux et al., 2017). Kipengele cha kwanza hufanya ugawaji wa pamoja wa neno na sentensi, cha pili hutabiri tagi za POS na sifa za kimofolojia, na cha tatu hutabiri uhusiano wa utegemezi kutoka kwa maneno na tagi za POS (Nivre, 2008). Ugawaji wa neno na sentensi kwa pamoja hutengenezwa kama uwekaji lebo wa mfuatano wa kiwango cha herufi, kwa kutumia mitandao ya neva ya kujirudia ya pande mbili (BiRNN) pamoja na CRF (de Lhoneux et al., 2017). |
| The predictions of POS tags and morphological features are accomplished using a Meta-BiLSTM model with context-sensitive token encoding. This method is adopted from the work of Bohnet et al. (Bohnet et al., 2018). The method applies BiLSTM to modeling both words and characters at the sentence level, giving the model access to the sentence context. The character and word models are combined in the Meta-BiLSTMs. In the Meta-BiLSTM, they concatenate the output, for each word, (of its context sensitive character and word-based embedding) and pass to another BiLSTM to create an additional combined context sensitive encoding. This is followed by a final MLP, whose output is passed onto a linear layer for POS tag prediction. | Utabiri wa tagi za POS na sifa za kimofolojia unakamilishwa kwa kutumia modeli ya Meta-BiLSTM yenye usimbaji wa tokeni unaozingatia muktadha. Mbinu hii imechukuliwa kutoka kwa kazi ya Bohnet et al. (Bohnet et al., 2018). Mbinu hii hutumia BiLSTM kuiga maneno na herufi zote katika kiwango cha sentensi, na hivyo kuipa modeli ufikiaji wa muktadha wa sentensi. Modeli za herufi na neno zinaunganishwa katika Meta-BiLSTM. Katika Meta-BiLSTM, wanachanganya pato, kwa kila neno, (ya uigaji wa herufi na maneno unaozingatia muktadha) na kuituma kwa BiLSTM nyingine ili kuunda uigaji wa ziada unaozingatia muktadha. Hii hufuatiwa na MLP ya mwisho, ambayo matokeo yake hupitishwa kwenye safu ya mstari kwa utabiri wa kitambulisho cha POS. |
| The third component is dependency parsing, in which a greedy transition-based parser (Nivre, 2008) is applied, following the framework of Kiperwasser and Goldberg (Kiperwasser and Goldberg, 2016). The framework learns representations of tokens in context using BiLSTM. Both the token context and the transition (arc labels) are trained together with a multi-layer perceptron. This enables the model to predict transition and arc labels based on a few BiLSTM vectors. The authors also introduce a static-dynamic oracle, which allows the parser to learn from nonoptimal configurations at training time. | Sehemu ya tatu ni uchanganuzi wa utegemezi, ambapo kichanganuzi chenye tamaa kinachotegemea mpito (Nivre, 2008) kinatumika, kikifuata mfumo wa Kiperwasser na Goldberg (Kiperwasser and Goldberg, 2016). Mfumo huu hujifunza uwakilishi wa tokeni katika muktadha kwa kutumia BiLSTM. Muktadha wa tokeni na mpito (lebo za tao) zote zinafunzwa pamoja kwa kutumia perceptroni ya tabaka nyingi. Hii inawezesha modeli kutabiri lebo za mpito na tao kulingana na vekta chache za BiLSTM. Waandishi pia wanaanzisha oracle tuli-tendaji, ambayo inaruhusu kichanganuzi kujifunza kutoka kwa usanidi usio bora wakati wa mafunzo. |
| **3.4. Turku Parser** | **3.4. Turku Parser** |
| Turku is a neural parsing pipeline for segmentation, morphological tagging, dependency parsing and lemmatization (Kanerva et al., 2018). For sentence segmentation and tokenization, the system relies on the output of UDPipe. The pipeline allows pre-trained embeddings to be included in the training. | Turku ni bomba la uchanganuzi wa neva kwa ajili ya ugawaji, uwekaji tagi wa kimofolojia, uchanganuzi wa utegemezi na ulematizishaji (Kanerva et al., 2018). Kwa ugawaji wa sentensi na utokenishaji, mfumo unategemea matokeo ya UDPipe. Mnyororo huo unaruhusu upachikaji uliofunzwa awali kujumuishwa katika mafunzo. |
| The tagging is done using the system of Dozat et al. (Dozat et al., 2017) which applies a time-distributed affine classifier to the tokens within a sentence. Tokens are first embedded with a word encoder. The encoder sums up a learned token embedding, a pre-trained token embedding, and a token embedding encoded from the sequence of its characters using a unidirectional LSTM. Next, a bidirectional LSTM reads the sequence of embedded tokens in a sentence to create a context-sensitive token representations. These representations are then transformed with ReLU layers separately for each affine tag classification layer (namely UPOS and XPOS). These two classification layers are trained jointly by summing their cross-entropy losses. | Uwekaji tagi unafanywa kwa kutumia mfumo wa Dozat et al. (Dozat et al., 2017) ambao unatumia kiainishi cha aina ya 'affine' kinachosambazwa kwa wakati kwa tokeni ndani ya sentensi. Ishara huingizwa kwanza kwa kisimbuzi cha neno. Kisimbuzi kinajumlisha upachikaji wa tokeni uliofunzwa, upachikaji wa tokeni uliofunzwa awali, na upachikaji wa tokeni uliosimbwa kutoka kwa mfuatano wa herufi zake kwa kutumia LSTM ya mwelekeo mmoja. Ifuatayo, LSTM ya pande mbili inasoma mfuatano wa tokeni zilizopachikwa katika sentensi ili kuunda uwakilishi wa tokeni unaozingatia muktadha. Uwakilishi huu kisha hubadilishwa kwa kutumia tabaka za ReLU kivyake kwa kila tabaka la uainishaji wa lebo za affine (yaani UPOS na XPOS). Tabaka hizi mbili za uainishaji hufunzwa pamoja kwa kujumlisha hasara zao za entropi ya msalaba. |
| Lemmatization is another pipeline in the Turku parser in which the researchers develop their own lemmatization component. The system considers lemmatization as a sequence-to-sequence translation problem. They consider a word as an input, a sequence of characters which are concatenated with a sequence of its part-of-speech and morphological tags. The output is based on the corresponding lemma represented as a sequence of characters. The researchers essentially train their system to translate the word form characters and morphological tags into lemma characters. They use a deep attention encoder-decoder network with a two-layered bidirectional LSTM encoder for reading the sequence of input characters and morphological tags. As a result, they obtained vectors for the sequence encoder. During the decoding phase, they applied beam search with a size five. | Ulemmatizaji ni mnyororo mwingine katika kifaa cha kuchanganua cha Turku ambapo watafiti wanakuza sehemu yao wenyewe ya ulemmatizaji. Mfumo huu huchukulia lemmatization kama tatizo la tafsiri ya mlolongo-kwa-mlolongo. | The task of syntactic labeling in the Turku parser is based on the system developed by Dozat et al. (Dozat et al., 2017). The researchers follow methods similar to those that they implemented for the POS tagging module, where tokens were embedded with a word encoder. The word encoder, then, sums up the learned token embedding, a pre-trained token embedding, and a token embedding encoded from the sequence of its characters using unidirectional LSTM. The embedded tokens are then concatenated together with respective POS embeddings. BiLSTM then reads the sequence of embedded tokens in a sentence so that the system has context-aware token representations. The token representations are then transformed using four different ReLU layers separately for two different biaffine classifiers. The classifier scores possible relations (HEAD) and their dependency types (DEPREL), and best predictions are later decoded to form a tree. These relations and type classifiers are again trained jointly by summing up their cross entropy losses. Refer to (Dozat and Manning, 2017) and (Dozat et al., 2017) for the detailed process. | Kazi ya lebo ya sintaksia katika kifaa cha kuchanganua cha Turku inategemea mfumo uliobuniwa na Dozat et al. (Dozat et al., 2017). Watafiti wanafuata mbinu zinazofanana na zile walizotekeleza kwa moduli ya kuweka lebo za POS, ambapo tokeni zilipachikwa kwa kisimbuzi cha neno. Kisimbuzi cha neno, basi, hujumlisha upachikaji wa tokeni uliojifunza, upachikaji wa tokeni uliofunzwa awali, na upachikaji wa tokeni uliosimbwa kutoka kwa mfuatano wa herufi zake kwa kutumia LSTM ya mwelekeo mmoja. Tokeni zilizopachikwa kisha huunganishwa pamoja na upachikaji husika wa POS. BiLSTM kisha husoma mfuatano wa tokeni zilizopachikwa katika sentensi ili mfumo uwe na uwakilishi wa tokeni unaozingatia muktadha. Uwakilishi wa tokeni kisha hubadilishwa kwa kutumia tabaka nne tofauti za ReLU kando kwa ajili ya viainishi viwili tofauti vya biaffine. Kiainishi hiki hupatia alama mahusiano yanayowezekana (HEAD) na aina zake za utegemezi (DEPREL), na utabiri bora zaidi huchambuliwa baadaye ili kuunda mti. Viainishi hivi vya mahusiano na aina vinafunzwa tena kwa pamoja kwa kujumlisha hasara zao za entropi mtambuka. Angalia (Dozat and Manning, 2017) na (Dozat et al., 2017) kwa mchakato wa kina. |
| **4. Comparing the Neural Network Parsers** | **4. Kulinganisha Vichanganuzi vya Mtandao wa Neva** |
| Before we discuss the comparison, we describe the experimental set up we followed. The standard practice of preparing data is to divide the data into training, development and test set, usually 80 percent for training, and 10 percent each for development and testing. However, the data set we have is too small to be divided into such proportions. Instead, we carry out ten-fold cross-validation (Zeman et al., 2017), randomly selecting and grouping an equal number of sentences into ten sets. The data we used for this paper are freely available at http://github.com/Binyamephrem/Amharic-treebank. During the training phase, the data in the nine sets are used as a training set and tested against the sentences in the remaining set. | Kabla ya kuzungumzia kulinganisha, tunaelezea mpangilio wa majaribio tuliofuata. | **5. Summary and Future Directions** | **5. Muhtasari na Maelekezo ya Baadaye** |
| This paper has presented a comparison of neural network parsers. Based on our comparison, we obtained an LAS score of 83.79 using the UDPipe system. This can be enhanced to 85.26 with external resources, i.e., word embedding. From the experiments we can recommend what will work better for Amharic. A parser for Amharic requires a segmentation of clitics before tagging. For this task, we recommend UDPipe. However, the performance of the segmentation need to be enhanced as it affects the tagging and attachment accuracy greatly. | Makala hii imewasilisha ulinganisho wa michanganuzi ya mtandao wa neva. | We have compared both pipeline and joint models for tagging and syntactic parsing. From the pipeline systems, the Turku parsing system achieves better results in the tagging task. However, we have noted that parsing systems that follow the pipeline approach need to have a more efficient segmentation and tagging module. Since errors propagate from one pipeline to another, the parsing or dependency attachment is severely affected. If a joint model is preferred, one needs to consider morphological tagging in addition to POS tagging. Our experiment shows that the joint model is a promising research area for further studies. The jPTDP only focuses on POS and attachment information. | Tumelinganisha mifano ya bomba na ya pamoja kwa ajili ya uwekaji tagi na uchanganuzi wa kisintaksia.
