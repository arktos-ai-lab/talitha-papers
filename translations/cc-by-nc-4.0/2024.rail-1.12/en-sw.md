| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| Recent research in natural language processing (NLP) has achieved impressive performance in tasks such as machine translation (MT), news classification, and question-answering in high-resource languages. However, the performance of MT leaves much to be desired for low-resource languages. This is due to the smaller size of available parallel corpora in these languages, if such corpora are available at all. NLP in Ethiopian languages suffers from the same issues due to the unavailability of publicly accessible datasets for NLP tasks, including MT. To help the research community and foster research for Ethiopian languages, we introduce EthioMT - a new parallel corpus for 15 languages. We also create a new benchmark by collecting a dataset for better-researched languages in Ethiopia. We evaluate the newly collected corpus and the benchmark dataset for 23 Ethiopian languages using transformer and fine-tuning approaches. | Utafiti wa hivi karibuni katika uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP) umepata utendaji wa kuvutia katika kazi kama vile tafsiri ya mashine (MT), uainishaji wa habari, na kujibu maswali katika lugha zenye rasilimali nyingi. Hata hivyo, utendaji wa MT bado hauridhishi kwa lugha zenye rasilimali chache. Hii inatokana na ukubwa mdogo wa korasi sambamba zinazopatikana katika lugha hizi, ikiwa korasi hizo zinapatikana kabisa. NLP katika lugha za Ethiopia inakabiliwa na matatizo yale yale kutokana na kutopatikana kwa seti za data zinazopatikana kwa umma kwa kazi za NLP, ikiwemo MT. Ili kusaidia jumuiya ya watafiti na kukuza utafiti kwa lugha za Ethiopia, tunatanguliza EthioMT - korasi mpya sambamba kwa lugha 15. Pia tunaunda kigezo kipya kwa kukusanya seti ya data kwa lugha zilizofanyiwa utafiti zaidi nchini Ethiopia. Tunatathmini korasi mpya iliyokusanywa na seti ya data ya kigezo kwa lugha 23 za Ethiopia kwa kutumia mbinu za Transformer na uboreshaji modeli. |

| **1. Introduction** | **1. Utangulizi** |
| In recent years, due to advances in deep learning approaches such as the development of transformers (Vaswani et al., 2017), machine translation (MT), a core task in natural language processing (NLP), has shown dramatic improvements in terms of coverage and translation quality (Wang et al., 2021). It is well-known that a critical requirement for advancing MT is the availability of parallel corpora. The availability of parallel corpora is also necessary to facilitate the incorporation of languages in MT applications like Google Translation, Bing, and DeepL (Van der Meer, 2019). The majority of the languages in the world do not have access to such translation tools since only a few high-resource languages have received significant attention (Tonja et al., 2023b). | Katika miaka ya hivi karibuni, kutokana na maendeleo katika mbinu za Ujifunzajikina kama vile utengenezaji wa Transformer (Vaswani et al., 2017), tafsiri ya mashine (MT), kazi ya msingi katika uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP), imeonyesha maboresho makubwa katika upanuzi na ubora wa tafsiri (Wang et al., 2021). Inajulikana vyema kwamba hitaji muhimu la kuendeleza MT ni upatikanaji wa korasi sambamba. Upatikanaji wa korasi sambamba pia ni muhimu ili kuwezesha ujumuishaji wa lugha katika programu za MT kama vile Google Translation, Bing, na DeepL (Van der Meer, 2019). Idadi kubwa ya lugha duniani hazina ufikiaji wa zana kama hizo za tafsiri kwa kuwa ni lugha chache tu zenye rasilimali nyingi zimepokea uangalizi mkubwa (Tonja et al., 2023b). |
| Most models and methods developed for high-resource languages do not work well in low-resource settings (Costa-jussà et al., 2022; Tonja et al., 2023b; King, 2015). Low-resource languages have also suffered from language technology designs (Joshi et al., 2019; Tonja et al., 2022). Creating powerful novel methods for language applications is challenging when resources are limited and only a small amount of even unlabeled data is available. The problem is exacerbated when no parallel dataset exists for specific languages (Joshi et al., 2020; Ranathunga et al., 2023; Adebara and Abdul-Mageed, 2022). | Miundo na mbinu nyingi zilizotengenezwa kwa ajili ya lugha zenye rasilimali nyingi hazifanyi kazi vizuri katika mazingira ya rasilimali chache (Costa-jussà et al., 2022; Tonja et al., 2023b; King, 2015). Lugha zenye rasilimali chache pia zimetaabika kutokana na miundo ya teknolojia ya lugha (Joshi et al., 2019; Tonja et al., 2022). Kuunda mbinu mpya zenye nguvu kwa ajili ya programu za lugha ni changamoto wakati rasilimali ni chache na kiasi kidogo tu cha hata data isiyo na lebo kinapatikana. Tatizo linazidishwa wakati hakuna seti ya data sambamba iliyopo kwa lugha maalum (Joshi et al., 2020; Ranathunga et al., 2023; Adebara na Abdul-Mageed, 2022). |
| Ethiopia is a country that stands out for its remarkable cultural and linguistic diversity, with over 85 spoken languages (Woldemariam, 2007). Only a few languages of Ethiopia have received attention in the area of NLP research and application development. Most languages have been left behind due to resource limitation (Costa-jussà et al., 2022; Tonja et al., 2023b). It is hard to find publicly available datasets for Ethiopian languages to pursue NLP research because many researchers do not make their datasets publicly accessible (Tonja et al., 2023b). The unavailability of benchmark datasets and results for NLP tasks, including MT, makes research for newcomers and interested parties very difficult. This is obviously more difficult for languages with limited data in different digital forms. | Ethiopia ni nchi inayojitokeza kwa utofauti wake wa ajabu wa kitamaduni na wa lugha, ikiwa na zaidi ya lugha 85 zinazozungumzwa (Woldemariam, 2007). Ni lugha chache tu za Ethiopia zimepokea uangalizi katika eneo la utafiti wa NLP na uundaji wa matumizi. Lugha nyingi zimeachwa nyuma kutokana na ukomo wa rasilimali (Costa-jussà et al., 2022; Tonja et al., 2023b). Ni vigumu kupata seti za data zinazopatikana kwa umma kwa ajili ya lugha za Ethiopia ili kuendeleza utafiti wa NLP kwa sababu watafiti wengi hawafanyi seti zao za data kupatikana kwa umma (Tonja et al., 2023b). Kutopatikana kwa seti za data za kigezo na matokeo kwa kazi za NLP, ikiwa ni pamoja na MT, hufanya utafiti kwa wageni na pande zenye nia kuwa mgumu sana. Hili kwa wazi ni gumu zaidi kwa lugha zenye data chache katika aina mbalimbali za kidijitali. |
| This paper introduces EthioMT: a parallel corpus for low-resource Ethiopian languages paired with English, and a benchmark dataset and experimental results for 23 Ethiopian languages. Our contributions are the following: (1) We create a new parallel corpus for 15 Ethiopian languages paired with English. (2) We introduce the first benchmark dataset and results for relatively better resourced Ethiopian (Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya and Somali) languages. (3) We evaluate MT performance with the new corpus and present benchmark results. (4) We open-source the parallel corpus to foster collaboration and facilitate research and development in low-resource Ethiopian languages. | Makala haya yanatanguliza EthioMT: korasi sambamba ya lugha za Ethiopia zenye rasilimali chache zilizooanishwa na Kiingereza, na seti ya data ya kigezo na matokeo ya majaribio kwa lugha 23 za Ethiopia. Michango yetu ni ifuatayo: (1) Tunaunda korasi mpya sambamba ya lugha 15 za Ethiopia zilizooanishwa na Kiingereza. (2) Tunawasilisha seti ya kwanza ya vigezo vya data na matokeo kwa lugha za Ethiopia (Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya na Kisomali) zenye rasilimali za kutosha. (3) Tunatathmini utendaji wa MT kwa kutumia korasi mpya na kuwasilisha matokeo ya kigezo. (4) Tunafanya korasi sambamba kuwa Programu huria ili kukuza ushirikiano na kuwezesha utafiti na maendeleo katika lugha za Kiethiopia zenye rasilimali chache. |

| **3.1.1. Afro-Asiatic language family** | **3.1.1. Familia ya lugha ya Afro-Asiatic** |
| The Afro-Asiatic language family comprises about 250 languages spoken in North Africa, parts of subSaharan Africa, and the Middle East. Languages belonging to this family are grouped into six sub-groups: Berber, Chadic, Cushitic, Egyptian, Omotic, and Semitic (Epstein and Kole, 1998). EthioMT contains languages belonging to the Omotic, Cushitic, and Semitic sub-groups. | Familia ya lugha ya Afro-Asiatic inajumuisha takriban lugha 250 zinazozungumzwa Afrika Kaskazini, sehemu za Afrika Kusini mwa Jangwa la Sahara, na Mashariki ya Kati. Lugha zinazohusika na familia hii zimepangwa katika vikundi vidogo sita: Berber, Chadic, Cushitic, Egyptian, Omotic, na Semitic (Epstein na Kole, 1998). EthioMT ina lugha zinazohusika na vikundi vidogo vya Omotic, Cushitic, na Semitic. |
