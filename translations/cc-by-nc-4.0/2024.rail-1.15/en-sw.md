| **Abstract** | **Muhtasari** |
| A significant number of research studies have been presented for detecting hate speech in social media during the last few years. However, the majority of these studies are in English. Only a few studies focus on Arabic and its dialects (especially the Algerian dialect) with a smaller number of them targeting sexism detection (or hate speech against women). Even the works that have been proposed on Arabic sexism detection consider two classes only (hateful and non-hateful), and three classes(adding the neutral class) in the best scenario. This paper aims to propose the first fine-grained corpus focusing on 13 classes. However, given the challenges related to hate speech and fine-grained annotation, the Kappa metric is relatively low among the annotators (i.e. 35%). This work in progress proposes three main contributions: 1) Annotation of different categories related to hate speech such as insults, vulgar words or hate in general. 2) Annotation of 10,000 comments, in Arabic and Algerian dialects, automatically extracted from Youtube. 3) Highlighting the challenges related to manual annotation such as subjectivity, risk of bias, lack of annotation guidelines, etc. | Idadi kubwa ya tafiti za utafiti zimewasilishwa kwa ajili ya kugundua matamshi ya chuki katika mitandao ya kijamii katika miaka michache iliyopita. | **1. Introduction** | **1. Utangulizi** |
| Hate speech is commonly defined as a language to express hatred against a specific person or a group based on certain key characteristics such as religion, gender, race, sexual orientation, and various disability forms (Shannaq et al., 2022). The excessive use of social media leads to the rise of antisocial behaviours illustrated in the spread of online hate speech, offensive language and cyber-bullying (Shannaq et al., 2022). Authorities in many countries are recognizing hate speech as a serious problem as it can lead to depression which hurts people's health and relationships. It can also lead to suicide in more serious scenarios (Boucherit and Abainia, 2022). | Matamshi ya chuki kwa kawaida hufafanuliwa kama lugha ya kueleza chuki dhidi ya mtu maalum au kikundi kwa kuzingatia sifa fulani muhimu kama vile dini, jinsia, rangi, mwelekeo wa kijinsia, na aina mbalimbali za ulemavu (Shannaq et al., 2022). Matumizi ya kupindukia ya mitandao ya kijamii husababisha kuongezeka kwa tabia zisizo za kijamii zinazodhihirishwa katika kuenea kwa matamshi ya chuki mtandaoni, lugha ya kukera na uonevu wa kimtandao (Shannaq et al., 2022). Mamlaka katika nchi nyingi zinatambua matamshi ya chuki kama tatizo kubwa kwani yanaweza kusababisha mfadhaiko ambao hudhuru afya na mahusiano ya watu. Yanaweza pia kusababisha kujiua katika hali mbaya zaidi (Boucherit and Abainia, 2022). |
| With the online proliferation of hate speech, a significant number of research studies focusing on how to classify and detect this kind of speech have been presented in the last few years. The majority of these studies detect general hate speech (Caiani et al., 2021; Pamungkas et al., 2018; Almatarneh et al., 2019; Kalaivani and Thenmozhi, 2021) and only a few studies (de Paula et al., 2021) focused on the detection of hate speech against women (only by distinguishing between hateful and non-hateful comments). However, almost all studies are dedicated to English. This is mainly due to the lack of resources (lexicons and corpora that are constructed for other languages such as Arabic). To bridge the gap, the role of this paper is to propose a fine-grained manually annotated corpus including 10,000 YouTube comments and 13 classes: 0 (no hate), i (insult), v (vulgar), h (hate), s (without relationships with women), b (positive), p (a problem in the annotation), e (emojis only), c (passage from Coran, Muslims book), iv (insults and vulgar in the same time), ih (insult and hate in the same time), vh (vulgar and hate in the same time), ivh (insult, vulgar and hate in the same time). This corpus will be freely available to the research after its publication. The main conclusion from this work was that annotators tend to disagree more frequently when they have to deal with different annotation classes. | Kwa kuongezeka kwa matamshi ya chuki mtandaoni, idadi kubwa ya tafiti za utafiti zinazolenga jinsi ya kuainisha na kugundua aina hii ya matamshi zimewasilishwa katika miaka michache iliyopita. Tafiti nyingi hizi hugundua matamshi ya chuki kwa ujumla (Caiani et al., 2021; Pamungkas et al., 2018; Almatarneh et al., 2019; Kalaivani and Thenmozhi, 2021) na ni tafiti chache tu (de Paula et al., 2021) zilizolenga ugunduzi wa matamshi ya chuki dhidi ya wanawake (kwa kutofautisha tu kati ya maoni yenye chuki na yasiyo na chuki). Hata hivyo, karibu tafiti zote zimejitolea kwa Kiingereza. Hii ni hasa kutokana na ukosefu wa rasilimali (kamusi na korasi ambazo zimeundwa kwa lugha nyingine kama vile Kiarabu). Ili kuziba pengo hilo, jukumu la makala hii ni kupendekeza korasi yenye ufafanuzi ya kina iliyofanywa kwa mikono ikijumuisha maoni 10,000 ya YouTube na madarasa 13: 0 (hakuna chuki), i (tusi), v (matusi), h (chuki), s (bila mahusiano na wanawake), b (chanya), p (tatizo katika ufafanuzi), e (emojis pekee), c (kifungu kutoka Coran, kitabu cha Waislamu), iv (tusi na matusi kwa wakati mmoja), ih (tusi na chuki kwa wakati mmoja), vh (matusi na chuki kwa wakati mmoja), ivh (tusi, matusi na chuki kwa wakati mmoja). Korasi hii itapatikana kwa uhuru kwa utafiti baada ya kuchapishwa kwake. Hitimisho kuu kutokana na kazi hii lilikuwa kwamba wafafanuzi huwa na tabia ya kutokubaliana mara nyingi zaidi wanapolazimika kushughulikia madarasa tofauti ya ufafanuzi. |
| **2. Arabic Hate Speech in social media: Challenges** | **2. Matamshi ya Chuki ya Kiarabu katika mitandao ya kijamii: Changamoto** |
| Arabic is a language spoken by more than 330 million people as a native language. It is the fifth most spoken language in the world. Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) is usually the official language used in school whereas the classical is used in the Holy Qur'an (Muslim's book) (ESI, 2016; Guellil et al., 2020b). Another form of Arabic is the Arabic dialects which are used in daily life conversations. Also, Arabic in social media can be written either by using Arabic letters or Arabizi (Latin letters) (Guellil et al., 2021). 55% of the text in social media was written in Arabic (2017) (Haddad et al., 2020). Arabic Natural Language Processing (NLP) applications have to deal with several complex challenges in addition to the common challenges related to any NLP problems(Guellil and Faical, 2017; Guellil et al., 2018). | Kiarabu ni lugha inayozungumzwa na zaidi ya watu milioni 330 kama lugha ya asili. Ni lugha ya tano inayozungumzwa zaidi duniani. MSA kwa kawaida ni lugha rasmi inayotumika shuleni ilhali Kiarabu cha kale kinatumika katika Kurani Tukufu (kitabu cha Waislamu) (ESI, 2016; Guellil et al., 2020b). Aina nyingine ya Kiarabu ni lahaja za Kiarabu ambazo hutumika katika mazungumzo ya maisha ya kila siku. Pia, Kiarabu katika mitandao ya kijamii kinaweza kuandikwa ama kwa kutumia herufi za Kiarabu au Arabizi (herufi za Kilatini) (Guellil et al., 2021). Asilimia 55 ya maandishi katika mitandao ya kijamii yaliandikwa kwa Kiarabu (2017) (Haddad et al., 2020). Programu za uchakataji wa lugha asilia (NLP) wa Kiarabu zinapaswa kukabiliana na changamoto kadhaa tata pamoja na changamoto za kawaida zinazohusiana na matatizo yoyote ya NLP (Guellil and Faical, 2017; Guellil et al., 2018). |
| Arabic is known for its challenges, scarcity of resources and complexity. Detecting hate speech for Arabic content is a complex task (Husain, 2020). Different challenges can be raised when detecting hate speech in Arabic text: 1) The informal language using short forms and slang. 2) The use of dialects (Boucherit and Abainia, 2022). 3) The diversity of the Arabic language dialects (Husain, 2020). 4) The use of Arabizi (Guellil et al., 2020a) | Kiarabu kinajulikana kwa changamoto zake, uhaba wa rasilimali na utata wake. Changamoto tofauti zinaweza kutokea wakati wa kugundua matamshi ya chuki katika maandishi ya Kiarabu: 1) Lugha isiyo rasmi inayotumia vifupisho na misimu. 2) Matumizi ya lahaja (Boucherit and Abainia, 2022). 3) Utofauti wa lahaja za lugha ya Kiarabu (Husain, 2020). 4) Matumizi ya Arabizi (Guellil et al., 2020a) |

| **4.2. Data annotation** | **4.2. Ufafanuzi wa data** |
| For the annotation, we randomly select 10,000 comments containing MSA and Algerian dialects written in Arabic and Arabizi. This corpus also contains some comments in French and others in English (As most of the Arabic people are bilingual). The annotation was done by three annotators, native speakers of Arabic and its dialects (2 women and one man). | **6. Conclusion** | **6. Hitimisho** |
| We constructed in this paper the first fine-grained corpus for Arabic/Algerian dialect hate speech against women detection. We focus on Arabic/Algerian dialect but we plan to extend this construction to other dialects such as Morrocan or Tunisian. We plan to extend this construction to other African languages as well. This corpus includes 14 labels and is distinguished among the general hate, insults and vulgar comments. Our future would be to automatically review some disagreements related to the mismatch of labels, upper-case, etc. We also plan to have this annotation reviewed by a fourth annotator who will have access to the different assigned labels in addition to the comments. We also plan to use the constructed corpus in order to train ML algorithms for fine-grained classification. | Katika makala hii tumejenga korasi ya kwanza yenye viwango vya kina kwa ajili ya kugundua matamshi ya chuki dhidi ya wanawake katika lahaja ya Kiarabu/Kialjeria. Tunazingatia lahaja ya Kiarabu/Algeria lakini tuna mpango wa kupanua ujenzi huu kwa lahaja nyingine kama vile ya Moroko au Tunisia. Tunapanga kupanua ujenzi huu kwa Lugha za Afrika nyingine pia. Korasi hii inajumuisha lebo 14 na inatofautishwa kati ya chuki ya jumla, matusi na maoni machafu. Mustakabali wetu utakuwa ni kupitia kiotomatiki baadhi ya kutokubaliana kunakohusiana na kutolingana kwa lebo, herufi kubwa, n.k. Tunapanga pia kuwa na ufafanuzi huu ukaguliwe na mfafanuzi wa nne ambaye atapata ufikiaji wa lebo tofauti zilizokabidhiwa pamoja na maoni.
