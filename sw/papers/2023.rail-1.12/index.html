Muhtasari

Lengo la makala haya ni kutoa mwanga fulani kuhusu uhusiano kati ya tahajia ya alama za matamshi na sauti zisizo wazi katika tahajia hizi zinazotumika katika kuandika mkusanyiko wa Lugha ya Igala. Vokali 30 zilitambuliwa (vokali fupi 5, na vokali 25 za kati hadi ndefu za aina mbalimbali) pamoja na diphthongu 8. Mifano katika mfumo wa sentensi na tafsiri ilitolewa. Makala yanachanganya hadi aina saba za alama za matamshi ili kukabiliana vyema na tatizo linalokumbana mara kwa mara la kutamka maneno katika maandishi yaliyoandikwa katika lugha ya kigeni na wasemaji wasio wazawa na wanafunzi kwa viashiria saidizi vilivyotolewa ili kuongoza watumiaji wa mwisho jinsi ya kutamka maneno kwa kutumia aina za alama za matamshi na uwakilishi wa sauti wa aina hizi ambao umetolewa hapa katika unukuzi wa mdomo wa maneno kwa kutumia mshazari mara mbili.

Maneno Muhimu

vokali, Igala, Lugha, Rasilimali

1. Utangulizi

Lugha ya Igala inazungumzwa hasa katika Wilaya ya Seneta ya Mashariki ya Jimbo la Kogi, Nigeria (Oguagha, 1980) ndani ya uratibu wa kijiografia ulio kati ya 6 30 na 80 kaskazini mwa latitudo na 6 30 na 7 40 mashariki mwa longitudo (Saleh na Yunusa, 2013). Watu hao wanakaa eneo ambalo ni takriban maili 4,982 za njia ya kupita ya mito Niger-Benue wakijumlisha zaidi ya wasemaji milioni 2 wa lugha hiyo (Saleh na Yunusa, 2013). Wikipedia.com ilieleza kwamba; “Kiigala ni tawi la Kiyoruboidi la familia ya lugha ya Volta–Niger, kinachozungumzwa na watu wa Igala, Agatu, Idoma, na Bassa”. Kwa mtazamo huo huo Obayemi, (1980) alitambua Kiigala kuwa, “miongoni mwa huluki zinazoonekana zaidi katika eneo la makutano ya Niger-Benue.

Katika utafiti huu, kipaumbele kikuu ni kuonyesha baadhi ya irabu muhimu na leksika zinazotumika katika mawasiliano ya kila siku katika nchi ya Igala. Inapaswa kusisitizwa kwamba maneno yaliyoonyeshwa katika kazi hii kwa vyovyote vile si 'yanayojitosheleza', kwani hii ni tone tu kutoka katika bahari ya maneno yaliyopo ndani ya mfumo wa leksikografia wa Kiigala. Herufi za Kilatini pamoja na alama zake za kifonetiki na sauti zilitumika kama vigezo vya marejeleo.

2 Irabu na Maneno katika Lugha ya Kiigala

Kamusi ya Oxford Advanced Learner's ya Kiingereza ilifafanua leksika kama; “maneno na vishazi vyote vinavyotumika katika lugha au somo fulani;

maneno na vishazi vyote vinavyotumika na kujulikana na mtu fulani au kikundi fulani cha watu” (Hornby, 2015). Kuhusiana na Kiigala kama ilivyo kwa lugha nyingine, uundaji wa kileksika huanza kwa kuunganisha mifuatano ya sauti au alfabeti ili kuunda mofimu ambazo kutokana nazo maneno huundwa. Kwa sasa, hakuna mfumo wa kialfabeti au wa tahajia wa kujitengenezea au sanifu unaojulikana wa kuandika lugha ya Kiigala isipokuwa ishara na alama za kiesoteriki; kwa kawaida sakafuni, zinazotumiwa na waganga wa kienyeji, ambazo hazijulikani kwa idadi kubwa ya watu na ambazo pia hazikutumikia malengo rasmi ndani ya jamii. Katikati ya udhaifu huu unaosababishwa na ukosefu huu wa fonimu asilia, waandishi kuanzia enzi ya kisasa, wanajulikana kwa kuchagua alama za kifonetiki za Kilatini au fonimu za Kiarabu ili kunakili au kufasili maneno yenye asili ya Kiigala, jambo ambalo wakati mwingine hurejelewa Afrika Magharibi kama a'jami au ajami (Mc Laughlin, 2017).

Pamoja na ukweli kwamba Kiigala ni lugha yenye msingi wa toni (Rodriguez, 2014; Dawson et al. 2015; Adeniyi, 2016), wakati wa kujaribu kuchunguza muundo wa lugha ya Kiigala, umakini mwingi wa wasomi umekuwa kwenye aina za maneno, bila juhudi nyingi kuelekezwa kwenye toni na sauti na matokeo yake ni kwamba bila ujuzi wa awali wa lugha maneno yaliyoandikwa ni magumu kutamka, katika baadhi ya matukio hata kwa tafsiri ya Kiingereza ya haya kutolewa, na kuhusu zile kategoria za waandishi waliojaribu kueleza toni kwa kutumia tahajia za kiakifu, bila ujuzi wa awali wa kiakifu kwa wasomaji, bado ni vigumu kwa wasomaji kufasiri kilichoandikwa, zaidi ya hayo, kupitia kushindwa mara kwa mara kwa waandishi kutoa visanduku vya unukuzi wa mshazari mara mbili au mabano ya mdomo ya sauti zinazowakilishwa na alama za tahajia katika kazi zao, ukweli kwamba alama hizi hutoa sauti fulani kutoka sehemu moja hadi nyingine na kutoka mwandishi mmoja hadi mwingine hufanya iwe vigumu sana kwa wasomaji kufahamu mfumo wa sauti unaodokezwa na waandishi hawa.

Abraham (2017; 2019) vilevile, Emah na Ojonugwa (2020) wakati wa kutumia tahajia ya kiakifu kuandika maneno, kwa sehemu kuonyesha jinsi tofauti za mwinuko wa sauti hubadilisha maana za maneno kama ilivyowahi kuzingatiwa na Yip (2002), walishindwa kutoa unukuzi wa mshazari mara mbili wa sauti zinazowakilishwa na alama za tahajia zilizojumuishwa. Kigalaonline.wordpress.com2 ilifanya jaribio lililokaribia kurekebisha mapungufu yaliyotajwa hapo juu lakini ikaishia kutambua aina tatu tu kwa kila moja ya vokali kuu tano na vokali mbili za ziada (ó na é kusikika /ó:/ na /é:/ [Sic] - au kama ilivyo katika umbo la kifonolojia linalotambulika zaidi /eu/ na /ei/), jambo ambalo lilitoa vokali kumi na saba tu kwa gharama ya vokali 30 + diphthongu 8 zinazotambuliwa katika karatasi hii haswa. Upungufu huu haukomei kwa lugha ya Kiigala pekee. Lugha nyingine kama hizo ambazo bado zinakwama kwenye kizingiti cha elimu ya jadi kufikia mwanzo wa Karne ya 20, hasa kuhusiana na makundi ya lugha zenye rasilimali chache barani Afrika pia zinakabiliwa na tatizo hili haswa, jambo ambalo Ken Lodge, alilizingatia hivi:

Elimu ya kimapokeo kwa kiasi kikubwa inapuuza lugha zinazozungumzwa… umakini mdogo hulipwa kwa maelezo ya usemi kwa njia isiyoegemea upande wowote. Kwa hiyo tunahitaji mbinu ya kuelezea usemi kwa maneno yasiyoegemea upande wowote na yanayoweza kuthibitishwa, tofauti na mikabala ya kawaida ambayo kwa kawaida huelezea sauti kama ''ngumu'', ''laini'', ''kali'', na kadhalika, ambayo inaweza tu kueleweka na mtu anayetumia maelezo hayo (Lodge, 2009).

Katika muktadha wa karatasi hii hasa, alfabeti za Kilatini zilipitishwa kwa ajili ya kuunda sauti za Kiigala, maneno, na miundo ya vishazi kwa kutumia alama za diakritiki zenye mifumo ya kifonimu sawa na Blench (2011). Fonimu zote za Kiingereza zilipitishwa isipokuwa herufi, Q, V, S, X, na Z ambazo zilitupiliwa mbali kama konsonanti kwa kukosa umuhimu kama ilivyo kwa Kiyoruba (Kasali et al. 2021) wakati herufi 'c' inatumika tu kwa kuunganishwa na herufi 'h' ili kutoa digrafu /t∫/ ambayo hutumika kuandika maneno kama vile ūchōnā (jina la mtu linalomaanisha 'muumba' au 'muumba' tu kwa maana pana zaidi ya neno), kama inavyoweza kupatikana kwa maneno ya Kiingereza kama chicken na kitchen. Zaidi ya hayo, herufi 'c' haitumiki katika kuandika maneno yenye sauti /k/ ambayo mara nyingi huandikwa kwa herufi 'c' kama vile can /kæn/, wala haiwezi kutumika kuandika neno lenye sauti /s/ kama vile ceiling kwa kuwa hii ingekuwa sílīni /si:lini/ au kwa kweli chílīni /t∫i:lini/ kwa sababu ya kutokuwepo kabisa kwa konsonanti 's' ndani ya mfumo wa kileksika wa Kiigala. Vivyo hivyo, badala ya kuandika neno quantity kwa herufi 'q', katika tahajia yetu ya Kiigala, tahajia ya quantity ingeonekana kama 'kwontity'.

Kuandika vokali wakati wa uundaji wa maneno mara nyingi huhusisha kupitishwa kwa mojawapo kati ya mitindo mitatu mbadala. Ya kwanza ni matumizi ya kipekee ya alfabeti za kawaida ambapo ni vokali tu ambazo hazijapambwa kwa glifi ndizo zingetumika. Aina hii ya kwanza haijumuishi matumizi ya alama za kiimbo kwa namna yoyote na ikiwa irabu zitatengenezwa, hilo litategemea mchanganyiko mwafaka wa alfabeti za kawaida ili kuzalisha maneno ambayo hayajaainishwa katika mpangilio wa herufi uliopo kama ilivyo katika tahajia ya neno Igalaa katika wiktionary.org ambapo ‘a' mbili zilizopigiwa mstari katika neno hilo Igalaa hazipendekezi tu irabu ndefu (yaani; /a:/) bali urudiaji wa irabu kama /a/ + /a/ kutoa sauti inayofanana sana na 'are are' inayoteleza. Aina ya pili ya njia hizi zilizopo ambazo mwandishi huyu hapa anadhani kuwa kwa kweli haziwezekani inategemea kabisa matumizi ya alama za kiimbo, ikiwa si katika kuandika herufi za kawaida za orthografia basi katika uchoraji wa mabano ya mdomo ya maneno kama ilivyo katika Webster's II New College Dictionary. Mtindo wa tatu unahusisha mchanganyiko wa aina zile nyingine mbili zinazopingana kabisa, yaani; upokeaji wa irabu za kawaida na irabu zenye msingi wa alama za kiimbo. Yoyote kati ya aina hizi tatu inatosha kushawishi mradi tu zinafuata kwa uthabiti kanuni za kawaida za mofofonemia. Ni aina ya tatu ya mbinu hizi-ile ya kuchanganya alfabeti ya Kilatini ya kawaida na alama za kiimbo za Kilatini, ndiyo itakayotumika katika kazi hii mahususi.

Katika mabano ya mdomo yaliyotolewa katika Webster's II New College Dictionary na thesaraus.yourdictionary.com, irabu fupi ziliwakilishwa kwa matumizi ya breve- aina ya alama ya kiimbo inayowasilishwa kama nusu ya chini ya duara juu ya irabu, yaani; ă, ĕ, ĭ, ŏ, ŭ, lakini katika kuandika kazi hii, badala ya kutoa herufi hizi kwa kutumia breve, ziliandikwa kwa usahili iwezekanavyo, yaani; ‘a, e, i, o, u'. Hilo likiwa limesemwa, jambo lingine la kutofautiana linahusu sauti ambazo orthografia nyingine za alama za kiimbo zilizotumiwa na kamusi hizi zinazoheshimika zilizalisha kuhusiana na funguo zetu wenyewe hapa. Katika Webster's II New College Dictionary (1999), macron ilipitishwa ili kuzalisha aina ya kipekee ya irabu ndefu ō ∼ /eu/ kama ilivyo katika neno old /ōld/ kwa kiasi kikubwa kama ilivyotumiwa na Dawson et al. (2015) na ā ∼ kama ilivyo katika neno said /sād/, lakini katika kazi hii, kwa kuchochewa na upekee wa lugha ya Igala ambao kwa kiasi fulani unaitenganisha na Kiingereza na dai la awali la Wells (2001) kwamba macron imeidhinishwa kwa kuanzisha eneo la kati kati ya irabu fupi na irabu ndefu.

Kwa hiyo makroni zilitumika katika kazi hii, si kwa ajili ya utoaji wa sauti ā = /ei/ na ō = /eu/ kwa maana hizi katika kazi hii zinaonyeshwa kwa kutumia herufi ‘o' na ‘e' zenye nukta juu yake (yaani; ȯ = /eu/ na ė = /ei/). Ingawa, kuna maboresho machache hivi karibuni, maandishi mengi ya awali, yakifuata hasa pendekezo la Kamati ya Orthografia ya Kiigala ya 1984 ambayo ilitambua ọ na ẹ zenye nukta chini kwa toni za chini o ∼ /o˩/ | e ∼ /ee˩˩/ (yaani; kama katika tahajia ya ọjọ /oo˩˩joo˩˩/ katika Okwoli (1996) na Ahiaba (2015), na ẹrẹ /ee˩˩ree˩˩/ katika Ayegba et al. (2017) kwa ajili ya kutamka sauti zinazoshuka za /o/ na /e/, wakati kundi la wanne lilihifadhi maumbo ya kawaida ya alfabeti ‘o' na ‘e' kwa ajili ya maumbo yanayopanda na kushuka ya sauti /eu/ na /ei/ kama inavyotumika kwa Kiyoruba katika aroadedictionary.com, wengi wa wasomi hawa walitambua irabu 7 pekee pamoja na kile kinachoitwa sauti 24 za konsonanti (Ojoaogwu, 2017) au alama 32 kwa jumla kulingana na Miachi na Armstrong (1986), jambo ambalo lilimaanisha waliacha idadi kadhaa ya irabu na hivyo kupunguza matumizi ya makroni katika kazi ya waandishi waliofuata isipokuwa tu Omachonu (2011) na kwa kiasi fulani, Dawson et al. (2015) ambao aidha waliitumia kwa heshima kubwa kama ilivyofanywa katika karatasi hii au kama ilivyofanywa katika Webster's II New College Dictionary.

Hivyo, matumizi ya maki na mstari wa juu katika makala hii yanafuata mfano unaofanana na matumizi yake na Karshima (2012) kuhusu lugha ya Tiv au kuhusu neno la Igala ìkpā (begi) katika Rodriguez (2014); lisichanganywe na íkpa (miguu inayopinda ndani), ambapo ā = /æ/ kama katika matamshi ya Kiingereza cha Marekani ya maneno ‘plan’ na ‘stand’ katika oxfordlearnersdictionary.com kama sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /a/ na /a:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA, ē = /ɛ/ kama katika neno la Kiingereza ‘any’, yaani; sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /e/ na /e:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA) kama ilivyotumika kuhusu neno ònēkèlē katika Rodriguez (2015) ambalo mwandishi wa karatasi hii yenyewe hapa tena angeandika upya kama ōnēkēlē (mume), ī = /i:/ > /x/ > /i/, yaani; sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /i/ na /i:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA, ō = /ɔ/ kama katika ‘o’ ya kati ya nyuma iliyozungukwa ya msingi inayotumika katika kutamka neno la Kiingereza ‘but’ au neno la Igala ōkō /ɔkɔ/ kuhusu ndege au meli ambalo linatofautiana na òkò /o˩koo˩˩/ (kifaru) au okā /okæ/ (mtindo, yaani; akrobati) au ókō /o:kɔ/ (pesa), na ‘ō’ ikiwa ni sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /o/ na /o:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA, ū = /u:/ > /x/ > /u/ kama katika Kilithuania, Kilivonia, na Kimaori, yaani; sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /u/ na /u:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA. X katika mabano ya mdomo inawakilisha sauti ya kifonemiki isiyojulikana kwa orthografia za kiakribati ī & ū ∼ x. Uamuzi wa kutupilia mbali breve katika karatasi hii una msingi wa namna mbili. Kwanza ilikuwa kuhakikisha uundaji wa mfumo thabiti wa orthografia wenye irabu safi zinazotumika katika maumbo yao ya asili ya orthografia kama ilivyo katika maandishi mengi ya lugha ya Kiingereza, bila kujali marekebisho yoyote ya kimaandishi au sifa ambayo nyongeza ya glifi zinazokuja kwa herufi ya kwanza tangu hapo inaweza kutoa. Sababu ya pili ilitokana na hitaji la kuepuka mzigo wa ndani wa ziada usio wa lazima wa kuziandika kwa herufi iliyoongezwa wakati zingeweza kubaki wazi au bila mabadiliko ya sehemu na bado kudumisha kazi zake kama irabu fupi.

Kutotumika kuelekea breve kama ilivyofanywa hapa hakukuwa jaribio la makusudi la kubatilisha sifa za Webster's II New College Dictionary, busara zao bado ni halali sana, hasa kuhusiana na utafiti wa lugha ya Kiingereza. Mwandishi huyu anafahamu vyema ukweli usio na shaka kwamba akiachwa na uteuzi wa vifunguo vya kuashiria visivyotabirika, ni wajibu wa kila mwandishi kuchagua kwa uangalifu maumbo ya kifonotaktiki na kimofotaktiki ambayo yanakubaliana na lugha inayoshughulikiwa (Pretorius na Bosch, 2009), hata hivyo inapaswa kuzingatiwa kwamba matumizi ya ā = /ei/ na ō = /eu/, licha ya kutokuwa makosa kwa namna yoyote, hayalingani hata hivyo na mtiririko asilia wa kifonolojia uliowekwa katika alfabeti yao ya mzizi 'a' ambayo hutoa sauti /a/ na /a:/, na 'e' ambayo hutoa sauti /e/ na /e:/. Kwa kuzingatia ukweli kwamba toni ya kati wakati mwingine huchukuliwa kuwa ni muunganiko wa vokali mbili (yaani; /e/ + /i/ = /ei/ na /e/ + /u/ = /eu/ kama ilivyo katika Kiingereza), basi haingekuwa kosa kabisa kusema kwamba wahariri wa Webster's II New College Dictionary walikuwa sahihi kwa haki yao wenyewe kutumia makroni katika muktadha waliyoitumia, zaidi ya hayo, wachambuzi wa lugha wanakubali kwamba, 'hakuna kitu kama umbo sanifu linapokuja suala la mambo ya lugha' (Laperre 2020) na kama Szczegielniak alivyodokeza kwa ufupi, "tahajia haiwakilishi sauti za lugha kwa uthabiti". Hivyo, katika lugha ya Kiigala, toni ya kati ina maana ya nafasi kati ya vokali ndefu na vokali fupi kama vile sauti ya kati kati ya /a:/ ya juu na /a/ ya chini, yaani; 'a' ambayo ina toni ya juu kuliko /a/ lakini bado ina kiwango cha chini cha sauti kuliko /a:/ inayowakilishwa kama x > /a/ < /a:/.

Mwandishi huyu angependa alfabeti ė ambayo kwa hivyo inatamkwa /ei/ iandikwe kama ȧ, lakini kwa sababu maneno mengi katika lugha tayari yameandikwa kwa herufi 'e' kutamka /ei/, kama katika maneno kama vile ugbede /ugbeidei/ au ele /eilei/, mwandishi kwa hiyo alichagua kudumisha herufi ė ambayo inakaribia zaidi mila iliyo maarufu zaidi sasa. Uamuzi wa kutumia alfabeti ȧ badala ya ė ungepatana kikamilifu na matumizi ya 'o' yenye nukta juu (ȯ) kutoa sauti /eu/. Matumizi ya makroni yenyewe yanakuwa muhimu kwa kuzingatia ukweli kwamba, maneno mengi katika lugha ya Kiigala yanatamkwa ndani ya muktadha huu wa toni ya kati.

Kama ilivyo katika uandishi wa lugha ya Kiyoruba au Kifaransa (Olufemi, 2022; Nolte et al. 2018), aina kali na ya kaburi ya alama za matamshi pia zilitumika ingawa kwa tofauti ya kinyume katika utamkaji kati ya maneno ya Kiigala yaliyoandikwa hapa kwa kutumia umbo hili ambalo ni tofauti na Kifaransa kuhusiana na neno café, katika hali hiyo, é kama ilivyo katika Kifaransa na katika umbo lililotumiwa na Rodriguez (2014) kuhusiana na neno 'éʤú' ∼ /eiʤu:/ (uso), linatamkwa /ei/, zaidi kama neno la Kiingereza rake /reik/, ilhali, tahajia é inavyohusiana na Kiigala na wakati mwingine na Kiyoruba; kama ilivyo kwa neno òré (rafiki) katika (Oshodi, 2016) ∼ /oo˩˩re:/, hata hivyo inatamkwa /e:/ kama katika Kiingereza. Hivyo, ni lazima tukubaliane na Cahill, (2019) kwamba, katika kutumia zana za alama za matamshi, "changamoto ni kwamba alama za toni kwa kawaida hazilingani miongoni mwa wasomi", matokeo yake ni kwamba, herufi é ambayo inatamkwa kama /e:/ katika tukio moja au na msomi mmoja inatamkwa katika tukio lingine au na msomi mwingine kama /ei/ kama ilivyo katika tahajia ya wálé ∼ /wa:lei/ (njoo nyumbani) katika Dalamu (2019) ambayo inakinzana na /e:/ ya Oshodi (2016) hapo juu.

Hapa pia, alama ya mkazo mzito (yaani; à, è, ì, ò, ù) ilitumika kuashiria toni za chini kama ilivyofanywa katika Yip (2002). Matumizi ya alama fulani za vikwazo katika kazi hii yanaweza kuhesabiwa kuwa yasiyofaa kama inavyoonekana sasa kuhusiana na jinsi mkazo mrefu unavyotumika hapa ambao tayari unakinzana na jinsi ulivyotumika katika Kamusi ya Webster's II au jinsi alama hizi zinavyowakilishwa katika lugha nyingine kama vile Kifaransa, Kiholanzi, Kireno, Kiitaliano, na Kimohawk (Finegan, 2012).

Mhakiki anaweza kuuliza kujua kwa nini 'mwandishi huyu hakutumia alama ya mkato mfupi katika kuandika vokali fupi', jambo ambalo tayari limetafakariwa hapo juu. Ili kufidia hitilafu yoyote inayotokana na jinsi mambo fulani yanavyowakilishwa katika kazi hii, zana saidizi zenye maelekezo kuhusu jinsi ya kutamka maumbo ya vikwazo zimetolewa, kwa matumaini kwamba mwandishi wa kipande hiki na wasomaji wanaweza kuwa na uelewa sawa katika kufahamu kile kilichoandikwa. Kwa hiyo, maneno yenye alama hizi ambayo kama mwandishi huyu anavyodhani huenda hayafuati mbinu zilizopo katika baadhi ya lugha, yanaweza kutamkwa; ikiwa si kikamilifu kabisa, yatakuwa 'karibu kamilifu'.

Vokali za Kiigala bado hazijazuiliwa kwa maumbo ya lafudhi yaliyotajwa hapo juu. Katika utafiti huu, imefunuliwa kwamba baadhi ya vokali ni mchanganyiko wa sauti mbili za vokali ileile zilizoinuliwa kwa namna yoyote kati ya vokali fupi au ndefu na kisha kupandishwa au kushushwa toni kwa vokali inayolingana (kama upinde), kama ilivyofanywa kuhusiana na ile toni ya kufunga katika neno Igalaa. Kwa kategoria hii ya vokali, mwandishi alibuni wazo la kuwa na maandishi yenye mkazo mrefu juu yake ambayo yamefunikwa zaidi kwa alama ya mkazo mzito au mkali lakini ni vokali fupi mbili tu ḕ, ḗ na ṑ, ṓ zilizoweza kukidhi hitaji hili, na kwa hiyo mwandishi alifikiria kurekebisha tatizo hili kwa kutumia alama ya kuzunguka yenye aidha alama ya mkazo mkali au mzito juu yake, yaani; ấ, ầ, lakini ni herufi 'a' pekee ndiyo yenye sifa hii kati ya herufi nyingine tatu ambazo bado hazipo zinazowasilisha vokali fupi (a, i. na u). Jambo lililofuata ambalo lingeweza kufanywa lilikuwa kuangalia grafimu zenye alama za matamshi ambazo zina sifa zinazoweza kutatua vyema tatizo la 'u' na 'i' (kuhusiana na kategoria hii ya sauti), lakini mwandishi huyu alikwama tena kwenye wazo la kutumia dieresisi ya 'u' ikiwa imefunikwa kwa alama ya grave au acute, yaani; ǘ na ǜ, lakini alama ya 'i' bado haikuwepo, na kwa hiyo, mwandishi alifikiria kutumia herufi ndogo ya Kigiriki iota yenye psili na varia (ἲ) na ἵ herufi ndogo ya iota yenye psili na oxia ambayo mwandishi aliiona kama chaguo pekee linalofaa kwa sasa kwa matumizi hapa.

2.1 Matumizi ya Kimuktadha ya Vokali

Katika kuandika maumbo ya orthografia ya vokali za Kiigala, mtu angekuwa hivyo na 30 kati ya hizi, huku kila vokali fupi ikitoa vibadala 5 kila moja wakati kutoka kwa glifi ȯ na ė kunaweza kupatikana difthongo 8 za ziada. Katika Webster's II New College Dictionary, herufi za orthografia na sauti za kifonetiki zinazoshikamana au kuwa asili katika vokali ndefu za lugha ya Kiingereza ziliandikwa katika maumbo; /â/ au /ä/ = /a: /, /ê/ = /e: /, /ē/ = /i: /, /ô/ = /o: /, /û/ = /u: /, /ā/ = /ei/, na ō = /eu/. Katika kazi hii toni 5 za juu zilizo hapo juu zilinakiliwa kama á = /a: /, é = /e: /, í = /i: /, ó = /o: /, ú = /u: /. Kuongezwa kwa vokali hizi ndefu ni vokali 5 fupi a = /a/, e = /e/, i = /i/, o = /o/, u = /u/. Pia kuna alama 5 za macron za kutengeneza kati ya vokali hizi, ā, ē, ī, ō, ū, ambazo maumbo yake ya kivokali yameelezwa hapo juu.

Kuna vokali 5 ndefu ambazo zinasisitizwa kwa toni za kushuka, yaani; kama katika à = /aa˩˩/, è = /ee˩˩/, ì = /ii˩˩/, ò = /oo˩˩/, ù = /uu˩˩/. Isipokuwa alama ya othografia 'o' ambayo imefunikwa kwa lafudhi mbili za kaburi na mbili za kikali (yaani; ȍ /oo˩˩˩˩/ na ő /o::/) zinazotumika kueleza lafudhi ya chini-zaidi na ya juu-zaidi ya sauti ya /o/, othografia nyingine nne zenye vokali kuu (a, e, i, na u) ambazo umbo lake la kialama (kama othografia pekee) hueleza viwakilishi 'a' (mimi), 'e' (wewe), 'i' (yeye au kitu), na 'u' (nafsi ya kwanza mimi) ambavyo huwasilisha mkazo na hali, zina sauti za kiambishi cha kupanda-kupanda kama dieresis huku moja, mchanganyiko 'a' (ấ = /a:a:/) ikiwa na mkumbo juu yake na alama ya kikali ikifunika mkumbo huo. Nyingine ni, 'e' iliyounganishwa (ḗ = /e:e:/) ambayo ina alama za mkazo mrefu juu yake na lafudhi ya kikali ikifunika mkazo mrefu, nyingine ina alama ya dieresis yenye lafudhi ya kaburi juu ya alama hiyo (ǘ = /u:u:/), na ya nne ni iota ya Kigiriki yenye psili na varia pamoja na 'i' iliyounganishwa (ἵ = /i:i:/). Kila moja ya vokali hizi 4 huanza na maumbo mafupi ya vokali ambayo hufuatiwa na vokali ndefu zinazolingana za mkumbo sawa wa sauti ndani ya silabi husika, kama vile kumaliza mtiririko wa sauti katikati lakini tena kuukazia mbele (kituo cha muda kinachofuatiwa na mabadiliko katika mtiririko).

Kwa kufuata kinyume, pia kuna sauti 4 za kiambishi cha kushuka-kushuka, 'a' (ầ = /aa˩˩aa˩˩/) ilichukua mkumbo juu yake na alama ya kaburi juu ya mkumbo, 'e' (ḕ = /ee˩˩ee˩˩/) ina alama ya mkazo mrefu juu yake na lafudhi ya kaburi juu ya mkazo mrefu, 'u' (ǜ = /uu˩˩uu˩˩/) ina alama ya dieresis yenye lafudhi ya kaburi juu ya alama hiyo, na ya nne ni iota ya Kigiriki yenye psili na oxia 'i' (ἲ = /ii˩˩ii˩˩/), kufanya 4 kati ya hizi, zote zikiwa na sauti ya vokali safi zilizounganishwa na toni za chini zinazolingana ndani ya silabi husika, kama vile kumaliza mtiririko wa sauti katikati lakini tena kuukazia chini. Hivyo, mwandishi huyu atajaribu kuhalalisha dhana ya vokali nyingine 30 kama ilivyotolewa.

Kwa herufi 'a';

á lȱn (we didn’t go), a lȯ (we went), ā lȯn (let us not go), à l + (ȯ capped with an acute diacritic accent (?) (should we go?), ấ lȱn? (won’t we go?), ầ lȱ? (are we going?).

Kwa herufi 'e';

é wán (you didn’t come), e wā (you came), ē wān (don’t come), è wā? (will you come?), ḗ wān? (won’t you come?), and ḕ d’ōmō? (will you be there?).

Kwa herufi 'i';

í wán (s/he didn’t come), i wā (s/he came), ī wān (s/he should not come), ì wā? (should, she/he come?), ἵ wān? (shouldn’t s/he come?), and ἲ d’ōmō? (is s/he there?).

Kwa herufi ‘o';

óna (as for a man), onā (dream), ōna (road), òkò (millipede), őgbá (front), and ȍgbā (as demarcation of land).

Kwa herufi ‘u';

ú kān (I didn’t say), u ka (I said), ū ka tān’ (let me not say yet), ù ká? (should I say?), ǘ kān? (won’t I say?), ǜ kān? (should I not say?).

2.2 Diphthongs

Katika kuandika tokeni ya Igala, ‘toni za kati' ‘ȯ' na ‘ė' kuhusiana na sauti /eu/ na /ei/ hazibaki sawa kila wakati kwa sababu baadhi ya sauti hizi zinaweza kuwa katika muktadha fupi, za kati au ndefu katika toni, kumaanisha kwamba hadi aina nane za sauti hizi mbili zinaweza kupatikana lakini kwa wakati huu, mwandishi angeweza kupata tu alama nne za kuandika, nazo zikiwa ė = /ei/, ǡ = /eiei/, ȯ = /eu/, na ȱ = /eueu/. Sauti hizi nane hutokana na vokali za kwanza /e/ + /i/ = /ei/ na /e/ + /u/ = /eu/, ambapo kutokana nazo tuna diphthongi 8, ingawa baadhi yazo kwa sasa hazionyeshwi na alama yoyote ya tahajia. Kwa hiyo kungekuwepo haja ya alama za ziada, yaani; tahajia zenye nukta ‘ė' au ‘ȧ' na ȯ zenye alama za mkazo wa chini au wa juu zilizowekwa (kwa njia yoyote) juu ya nukta kwenye kila moja ili kusikika /eieii˩˩/ na /eueuu˩˩/ au /eiei:/ na /eueu:/.

Mwandishi angechukua mfano mmoja tu kuhusu hili kuhusiana na neno ele kama linavyoandikwa kwa sasa ambalo linaweza kumaanisha zawadi au chatu. Hapa, mwandishi ameyaandika ėlė tu kama jambo la lazima kwa maana kama yangeandikwa kwa maana yao halisi, tahajia inayofaa zaidi kwa haya haikuweza kupatikana yote kwenye orodha ya alama za IPA kwenye Microsoft word, lakini hata hivyo (kitendo cha kuyaandika kwa ‘e' yenye nukta, yaani; ėlė) humleta mtu karibu na kutochanganya tahajia ya neno kama ele tu kumaanisha nne, ‘iliyokaangwa vizuri', au zile dhana nyingine mbili (zawadi na chatu). Kwa maana kama mwandishi angeandika neno chatu kwa usahihi, hilo pengine lingekuwa, (ė ikiwa na mkazo wa juu juu ya nukta) au (ė:) + lė, wakati zawadi ingekuwa, (ė ikiwa na mkazo wa juu juu ya nukta) au (ė:) + l + (ė ikiwa na mkazo wa juu juu ya nukta) au (ė:), wakati mafuta ya kokwa ya mchikichi yangekuwa tu ėnė, ‘uwongo' ungekuwa (ė ikiwa na mkazo wa chini juu ya nukta) au (ė:˩) + mī, kama jani lingekuwa (ė ikiwa na mkazo wa juu juu ya nukta) au (ǡ) + nghmi. Vile vile hutumika kwa herufi ȯ ambapo tunda la mkono wa Palmyra linapaswa kuwa (ȯ yenye mkazo juu ya nukta) + d + (ȯ yenye mkazo juu ya nukta), ȯbè (kichuguu), ambacho kinapaswa kuwa (ȯ yenye mkazo chini ya nukta) au (ė:) + bè, huku ‘chungulia' kingekuwa ȯ + p + ė au tu ‘ȯpė kama katika kauli ‘‘kp'ȯpė kā g'ēnē ki yā wā'' (fanya mchungulio tuone anayekuja).

2.3 Elision na aina nyingine za mchanganyiko wa vokali

Kwa kuwa lugha ya Kiigala ‘kwa kiasi fulani' inafuata ‘Kanuni ya Limau ya Kifaransa' au Kanuni ya VCV (irabu – konsonanti – irabu) kwa sababu ni nadra kuwa na hali ambapo irabu hujifuata zenyewe kwa wakati mmoja wakati wa uundaji wa maneno. Hata katika tukio moja ambapo kanuni hii inavunjwa kama itakavyoonyeshwa hapa chini, hii ni zaidi matokeo ya elisioni ya sauti ya konsonanti inayolingana kuliko muundo uliowekwa kiasili kwa neno hilo. Ubaguzi pekee unaojulikana kwa kanuni hii ni kuhusiana na neno ābū linalomaanisha ‘vipi' au ‘nini', au ‘wapi', ambapo, wazungumzaji huwa na mwelekeo wa kutamka kwa makusudi konsonanti /b/ au zote mbili /b/ na irabu /u/ zinazofuata wakati wa mawasiliano kwa maana irabu /a/ pekee, au /a/ pamoja na /u/ kuhusiana na neno mà (wao) hupunguzwa ili wakati inapofuatiwa na viambishi tamati ū (mimi), ē (wewe), ā (sisi), ī (yeye), na mà (wao), neno hilo linasikika kama ā'ū (vipi au nini nilifanya, kama katika sentensi “ā'ū kā?” (vipi au nini nilisema), ā'ē (vipi au nini ulifanya, kama katika sentensi “ā'ē mā?” (vipi unajua), ā'ā (vipi au nini tulifanya, kama katika kauli, “ā'ā chė?” (vipi au nini tulifanya), ā'ī (vipi au wapi alifanya, kama katika sentensi, “ā'ī lė?” (vipi au wapi alienda), na ā'ū mà au ā'mà (vipi au nini wanafanya, kama katika sentensi “ā'ū mà kō” au “ā'mā kō?” (Nini au vipi waliandika?). Ingawa, aina hizi nyingine hutumika kama mbadala wa ābū, hata hivyo hazijumuishi mbadala bora zaidi wa ābū ambao ndio umbo halisi la jinsi neno hilo linavyopaswa kutumika kwa maana hizi ni matoleo potovu tu ya neno hilo ābū.

Zaidi ya hayo, wakati wa kuandika sentensi katika lugha ya Kiigala, herufi fulani huachwa kutoka kwa baadhi ya maneno ili kuepuka utambazaji, uchafu au kurukaruka na mahali pa alfabeti hizi zilizoachwa apostrofi hutumika kama njia ya kuonyesha elisioni hizi. Kistari kifupi kinaweza kutumika kuandika maneno fulani lakini kwa mwandishi ambaye ana uelewa bora wa aina za maneno, matumizi ya kistari kifupi huwa karibu yasiyo ya lazima, lakini badala ya kufanya kosa la kuunganisha maneno au kuunda matatizo ya utata katika matumizi ya maneno kama ilivyobainishwa na Malema et al. (2020), kwa uelewa bora wa aina za maneno asili, ingekuwa bora zaidi maneno haya kuandikwa kwa kistari kifupi. Kwa kuwa vokali hazirudishiwi mwangwi, matumizi ya alama ya umlaut au dieresis kama ilivyo kawaida katika baadhi ya lugha za Ulaya Kaskazini au Kiingereza si ya kawaida kwa kuwa hakukuwa na haja ya vokali zozote mbili kusongamana ili zitamkwe. Zaidi ya hayo, kwa kuwa kwa sasa hakuna othografia za kifonemiki za kienyeji au kwa sababu uundaji wa toleo sanifu la lugha ya Igala bado ni mchakato unaoendelea, kuna haja ya kuangalia dhidi ya mazoezi katika mfumo wa sauti wa lugha ya Kiingereza ambapo vokali zinaweza kutoa sauti nyingi au ambapo sauti fulani hurudiwa hata wakati marudio hayo ni karibu yasiyo ya lazima, kama itakavyokuwa na marudio ya herufi 'l' katika neno 'ball', au marudio ya 'g' katika 'egg'.

Katika Kiingereza, herufi kama zinavyotumika katika maneno fulani kwa kawaida hazifuati muundo mmoja, yaani, herufi 'a' (ambayo inaweza kusikika kama /ei/ kama katika 'cake' au /æ/ kama katika 'can' /kæn/, au /o:/ kama katika ball na kama /a:/ katika 'cart'), herufi 'e' (ambayo inaweza kusikika kama /e/ kama katika 'end' au /i/ kama katika 'elastic' au 'penis'), herufi 'i' (ambayo inaweza kutamkwa kama /i/ kama katika ink au /ai/ kama katika kite), herufi 'o' (ambayo inaweza kusikika kama /o/ kama katika on au /o:/ kama katika off, na kama /u/ au /u:/ kama katika oops au ooze inaporudiwa au kama /Ʌ/, /o/, au /u:/ wakati herufi 'u' inapokuja mara moja mbele ya herufi 'o' katika tahajia kama tulivyo nayo katika maneno coupling au thought au coup), herufi 'u' (ambayo inaweza kutamkwa kama /u/ kama katika book au /u:/ kama katika boom au /Ʌ/ kama katika umbrella, au kama /3:/ kama katika urban wakati r inapowekwa baada ya herufi u). Kama lugha ya Kihungaria au lugha nyingine ambazo hazitegemei pekee matumizi ya alfabeti ya kawaida bali hutumia kiasi fulani cha alama za matamshi ili kupanua wigo wa alfabeti zinazopatikana ili kuchukua herufi zaidi zinazotolewa kwa njia tofauti, wakati wa kuandika lugha ya Igala, si lazima tubadilishe sauti ya monofoni kwa sheria hiyo tu kwamba baadhi ya maneno yenye tahajia sawa yatamkwe tofauti, wala fonimu za ziada haziongezwi kwa vokali au konsonanti kama njia ya kubadilisha sauti ambayo herufi inayobeba inapaswa kutoa kuwa sauti nyingine ya kifonetiki kama tulivyo nayo katika maneno ya Kiingereza plough au bought, na fort na resort. Tatizo hili la matamshi linaweza kusababisha maumivu ya kichwa kwa mwanafunzi mpya wa lugha ya Kiingereza.

3 Hitimisho

Karatasi hii inatoa muhtasari wa vokali zinazotumika katika lugha ya Igala. Vokali ni msingi wa uundaji wa maneno na bila hizo utungaji wa usemi unakuwa hauwezekani. Vokali hizi huchangamana na sauti ya konsonanti kuunda maneno lakini hii haimaanishi kwamba bila sauti za konsonanti, maneno fulani hayawezi kutamkwa au kuandikwa kama ilivyo katika lugha ya Igala ambapo maneno fulani yanaweza kuandikwa bila mchanganyiko wa sauti za konsonanti na sauti za konsonanti kama inavyotumika kwa vokali nne, a, e. i, u ambazo kwa kusimama peke yake humaanisha kitu. Hii kwa kiasi kikubwa ni matokeo ya ukweli kwamba, lugha ya Kiigala ina mfumo mpana wa toni.

4 Mipaka ya Utafiti

Kama lugha ya kiasili isiyo na rasilimali za kutosha ambayo uchunguzi wake wa kimuktadha kama chombo cha kupitisha utamaduni ni mpya kiasi (Ojoajogwu, 2017), mikabala ya kurasimisha lugha ya Kiigala kwa maana ya kisasa zaidi imefanywa kwa uchache lakini pia bila kubagua kwa njia ambazo hazina uzingativu wowote wa kisayansi, hasa kwamba wale waliojaribu kuandika lugha hiyo ama si wataalamu wa lugha wanaoelewa misingi ya kisayansi kuhusu kanuni za lugha kuhusiana na sarufi na muundo na mpangilio wa toni, au walikuwa watu binafsi waliofanya haraka kwa njia ambayo fikra za kina kuhusu kina na upana wa sarufi na toni zote zilizomo katika lugha hiyo hazikushughulikiwa kwa kina kikubwa. Kwa hiyo haikushangaza kwamba miongoni mwa baadhi ya nyenzo zilizopo zenye maneno, sentensi na maana, mara nyingi mtu huona tofauti katika miundo ya alama za matamshi zinazotumiwa kuonyesha toni na matamshi kutoka kwa mwanazuoni mmoja hadi mwingine na katika baadhi ya matukio baadhi ya maandishi ya alama za matamshi ambayo yanaweza kuwakilisha toni fulani huachwa. Kinachoongezwa kwa hili pia ni tofauti kuhusiana na lafudhi na tofauti za kikabila katika toni kutoka eneo moja hadi jingine ambayo imeathiri zaidi matokeo ya kazi ya waandishi mbalimbali. Sababu hii ndiyo inayochangia upungufu wa kwanza wa utafiti huu, kwa sababu kwa kuelewa makosa yaliyomo katika matamshi yasiyofaa yaliyofanywa na waandishi wengine, mwandishi mwenyewe alitengeneza zana tofauti zinazowakilisha toni kadhaa, ambazo ingawa ni za kimantiki kisayansi, hata hivyo, hazihusiani kikamilifu na mbinu zozote zilizotumiwa na waandishi wa awali.

Ingawa, kabla ya kazi hii ya sasa, hakuna jitihada iliyofanywa kuchunguza umbo la mazungumzo la lugha ya Kiigala kutoka msingi kwa namna ya kina zaidi, kama inavyofanywa katika karatasi hii, ukweli ambao ulichangia kupuuzwa kwa baadhi ya miundo ya vokali inayowakilishwa na alama maalum kama ilivyoelezwa hapo juu. Lakini vokali hazikuwa orthografia pekee zilizoachwa nje kwani hata orthografia za konsonanti zinazowasilisha alama moja kama kitengo cha kundi la konsonanti kama vile ‘c' yenye nukta juu, yaani; ċ, au caron au hacek kama inavyoitwa wakati mwingine; č, zote mbili zikiwakilisha sauti /ch/ katika lugha nyingine za dunia, kama ilivyo kwa neno la Kiingereza ‘church', na alama ŋ ambayo inapaswa kuwakilisha kitengo kimoja cha kundi /ng/ kama katika neno ‘ānyīngā' (ukucha wa kidole) ambalo lingepaswa kuwa ‘āɲīŋā' au kundi la ‘ny' (ɲ) halitumiki sana. Ingawa kutotumika kwa orthografia hizi na mwandishi wa awali kulileta kizuizi fulani kuhusiana na jinsi mwandishi huyu angeandika silabi zinazopaswa kubeba orthografia hizi, pia ilikuwa kizuizi cha utafiti huu kwa sababu mwandishi huyu alishindwa kufanya vinginevyo kutoka kwa kile kilichoshikiliwa kwa muda mrefu kutokana na ushawishi wa Kiingereza katika uandishi wa lugha ya Igala. Ingawa tilde ñ ambayo hubeba kundi la /nn/ na ambayo hutumiwa mara kwa mara na baadhi ya waandishi wa awali, ingawa kukiwa na dhana potofu hapa na pale, katika kazi hii, maneno yanayohusu ishara hii hasa hayakutumiwa na kwa sababu hiyo, ishara hiyo ilipitwa, lakini katika jaribio la baadaye, itashughulikiwa kwa uangalifu maalum.

Kinachoongezwa kwenye vikwazo viwili vilivyotajwa hapo juu ni ukweli kwamba baadhi ya orthografia zinazowakilisha vokali hazipo kwa sasa ili kuonyesha sauti hizi kwenye orodha ya alama za IPA kwenye Microsoft word kama ilivyoonyeshwa katika mwili wa kazi, au labda alama hizi zipo lakini kwa sababu ya ujuzi mdogo wa mwandishi huyu kuhusu jinsi baadhi ya alama zinavyotamkwa, hazingeweza kuelezewa na mwandishi. Ukweli huu pia unaleta kizuizi chake chenyewe. Kinachoongezwa kwenye vokali hizi zilizokosekana au vokali ambazo hazijagunduliwa na mwandishi huyu ni ukosefu wa alama moja kwa makundi ya konsonanti ‘kw', ‘kp', nw, na ‘gh'. Mwandishi huyu anafahamu kwamba p yenye nukta juu (ṗ), kwa sasa inatumika kuhusiana na uandishi wa lugha ya Kiyoruba, maendeleo ambayo baadhi ya waandishi wa lugha ya Igala wamezoea kuhusiana na uandishi wa kundi la ‘kp' kama mtu angefanya kwa jina la Kiyoruba ‘Tōṗē' ambalo linatamkwa kama /topke/ au /tōkpē/, lakini uwekaji alama huu wa sauti /kp/ kwa ‘ṗ' unaleta swali kwa kuwa siyo fonimu ya kwanza kwenye kundi hilo. Je, inawezekana basi kwamba uwekaji alama huu ni matokeo ya ukweli kwamba, hakuna ‘k' yenye nukta juu kwenye orodha ya alama za IPA au kwa sababu ‘p' katika mchanganyiko huo huunda fonimu yenye sauti zaidi kuliko ‘k' au ni kwa sababu watu kwa hiari yao, walichagua kuandika hivyo? Kuhusu kundi la 'kw', je, ḵ iliyopigiwa mstari (qaph) inapaswa kubaki tu kama ق kama ilivyo katika tahajia ya Kiarabu au matumizi mengine kama kitengo kimoja cha kuwasilisha kundi la 'kw' yapatikane ndani yake miongoni mwa alama za tahajia za Kilatini kwa lugha zile ambazo mifumo yake ya uandishi bado inaendelea kubadilika?

5 Taarifa ya Kimaadili

Ingawa kuna juhudi ndogo sana kuelekea kuendeleza lugha ya Kiigala kuwa njia rasmi ya mawasiliano ya maandishi au ukweli kwamba juhudi kuelekea uhifadhi wake zinapaswa kupewa kipaumbele juu ya kuzorota kwake au ni muhimu kwa kufanya kazi ya karatasi kama ile iliyofanywa hapa, uangalifu wa ziada lazima uchukuliwe kuelekea kuepuka matumizi mabaya yasiyo ya lazima ya maudhui. Ukweli huo ulichukua nafasi kuu katika uzingatiaji wa mwandishi huyu wakati akikusanya kipande hiki. Kwa hiyo mwandishi huyu anachukua jukumu kwa ukiukaji wowote unaoweza kutokea kutokana na matumizi au utumiaji wa kazi hii.

Abstract

Muhtasari

The aim of this article is to provide some insight into the link between diacritic orthography and the implicit sounds in these orthographies that are applied in writing the Igala Language corpus. 30 vowels were identified (5 short vowels, and 25 mid to long vowels of different variety) plus 8 diphthongs. Examples in the form of sentences and interpretation were provided. The article combines up to seven diacritic forms in order to better tackle the oft encountered problem of pronouncing words in texts written in foreign language by non-native speakers and learners with supporting indicators provided to guide end users on how to pronounce the words using the diacritic forms and vocal representation of these forms that are herein provided in the double slash oral transcription of the words.

In the context of this particular paper thus, Latin alphabets were adopted for fashioning Igala sounds, words, and phrasal forms using diacritic symbols bearing phonemic modes similar to Blench (2011). All the English phonemes were adopted except the letters, Q, V, S, X, and Z which were discarded consonants for lack of relevance as it is with Yoruba (Kasali et al. 2021) while the letter ‘c’ is applied only in combination with the letter ‘h’ to produce the digraph /t∫/ that is used for writing words such as ūchōnā (somebody’s name meaning ‘creator’ or just ‘the creator’ in a more general sense of the word), as can be had with English words as chicken and kitchen. More also, the letter ‘c’ is not used in writing /k/ sounding words that are often written with the letter ‘c’ such as can /kæn/, neither can it be devoted to writing a sound /s/ bearing word such as ceiling for this would have been sílīni /si:lini/ or actually chílīni /t∫i:lini/ because of the still the more total absence of the ‘s’ consonant within the Igala lexical system. Writing vowels during word formation often involves the embracement of any one out of some three alternative styles. The first is the exclusive use of plain alphabets in which case only vowels that are unadorned with glyphs would be put to use. This first form does not embody the use of diacritic in any way and if vowel are to be crafted, that would depend upon the efficacious amalgam of plain alphabets to produce words that are not delineated in the existing letter ordering as in the spelling of the word Igalaa in wiktionary.org in which case the underlined double ‘a’ in that word Igalaa does not merely suggest a long vowel (i.e.; /a:/) but a repetition of the vowels as /a/ + /a/ to give a sound much like a gliding ‘are are’. The second form of these on-hand modes which this author here assume to be actually impractical depend exclusively on the use of diacritic, if not in writing the plain orthographic characters but in the scrawling of the oral parenthesis of words as in the Webster’s II New College Dictionary. The third style involves the commixing of the other two diametrical forms, i.e.; the adoption of both the plain vowels and the diacritic–based vowels. Any of these three forms is cogent enough provided they goose-step along the conventional morphophonemic norms. It is the third form of these approaches-that of mixing plain Latin alphabet with Latin diacritic symbols, that would be utilized in this particular work.

Macrons were therefore used in this work, not for the production of the ā = /ei/ and ō = /eu/ sounds for these are in this work illustrated by the use of the letters ‘o’ and ‘e’ with dots on top of them (i.e.; ȯ = /eu/ and ė = /ei/). Although, there are a few improvements lately, most of the previous writings, following mainly from the recommendation of the 1984 Igala Orthographic Committee which recognized the under dotted ọ and ẹ for the low tones o ∼ /o˩/ | e ∼ /ee˩˩/ (i.e.; as in the spelling of ọjọ /oo˩˩joo˩˩/ in Okwoli (1996) and Ahiaba (2015), and ẹrẹ /ee˩˩ree˩˩/ in Ayegba et al. (2017) for sounding the falling /o/ and /e/ sounds, while the quartet reserved the plain forms of ‘o’ and ‘e’ alphabets for the rising and falling forms of /eu/ and /ei/ sounds as used for Yoruba in aroadedictionary.com, most of these scholars recognized only 7 vowels plus a so called 24 consonant sounds (Ojoaogwu, 2017) or 32 symbols in whole according to Miachi and Armstrong (1986), which meant they left out a number of vowels and therefore downplaying the use of the macron in the work of succeeding writers with the only exceptions being Omachonu (2011) and to an extent, Dawson et al. (2015) who either used it in much of a respect as done in this paper or as was done in the Webster’s II New College Dictionary.

Kwa hiyo makroni zilitumika katika kazi hii, si kwa ajili ya utoaji wa sauti ā = /ei/ na ō = /eu/ kwa maana hizi katika kazi hii zinaonyeshwa kwa kutumia herufi ‘o' na ‘e' zenye nukta juu yake (yaani; ȯ = /eu/ na ė = /ei/). Ingawa, kuna maboresho machache hivi karibuni, maandishi mengi ya awali, yakifuata hasa pendekezo la Kamati ya Orthografia ya Kiigala ya 1984 ambayo ilitambua ọ na ẹ zenye nukta chini kwa toni za chini o ∼ /o˩/ | e ∼ /ee˩˩/ (yaani; kama katika tahajia ya ọjọ /oo˩˩joo˩˩/ katika Okwoli (1996) na Ahiaba (2015), na ẹrẹ /ee˩˩ree˩˩/ katika Ayegba et al. (2017) kwa ajili ya kutamka sauti zinazoshuka za /o/ na /e/, wakati kundi la wanne lilihifadhi maumbo ya kawaida ya alfabeti ‘o' na ‘e' kwa ajili ya maumbo yanayopanda na kushuka ya sauti /eu/ na /ei/ kama inavyotumika kwa Kiyoruba katika aroadedictionary.com, wengi wa wasomi hawa walitambua irabu 7 pekee pamoja na kile kinachoitwa sauti 24 za konsonanti (Ojoaogwu, 2017) au alama 32 kwa jumla kulingana na Miachi na Armstrong (1986), jambo ambalo lilimaanisha waliacha idadi kadhaa ya irabu na hivyo kupunguza matumizi ya makroni katika kazi ya waandishi waliofuata isipokuwa tu Omachonu (2011) na kwa kiasi fulani, Dawson et al. (2015) ambao aidha waliitumia kwa heshima kubwa kama ilivyofanywa katika karatasi hii au kama ilivyofanywa katika Webster's II New College Dictionary.

Thus, the use of the macron in this article follows a model that is similar to its use by Karshima (2012) in respect to the Tiv language or in respect to the Igala word ìkpā (bag) in Rodriguez (2014); not to be confused with íkpa (knock knees), in which ā = /æ/ as in the American English pronunciation of the words ‘plan’ and ‘stand’ in oxfordlearnersdictionary.com as an intermediate tone between the sound /a/ and /a:/ of the IPA sound system, ē = /ɛ/ as in the English word ‘any’, i.e.; an intermediate tone between the sound /e/ and /e:/ of the IPA sound system) as was used in respect to the word ònēkèlē in Rodriguez (2015) which the author of this very paper would here again rewrite as ōnēkēlē (male), ī = /i:/ > /x/ > /i/, i.e.; an intermediate tone between the sound /i/ and /i:/ of the IPA sound system, ō = /ɔ/ as in the cardinal mid-back rounded ‘o’ used in sounding the English word ‘but’ or the Igala word ōkō /ɔkɔ/ in respect to airplane or ship which is in contrast to òkò /o˩koo˩˩/ (millipede) or okā /okæ/ (style, i.e.; acrobatic) or ókō /o:kɔ/ (money), with ‘ō’ being an intermediate tone between the sound /o/ and /o:/ of the IPA sound system, ū = /u:/ > /x/ > /u/ as in Lithuanian, Livonian, and Maori, i.e.; an intermediate tone between the sound /u/ and /u:/ of the IPA sound system. The x in the oral parentheses represents the indefinite phonemic sound for the diacritic orthographies ī & ū ∼ x. Decision to jettison the breve in this paper has a twofold underling. First was to ensure the design of a coherent orthographic system with pure vowels used in their original orthographic forms as it is in most English language texts, notwithstanding whatever graphical modification or characterization that the addition of impending glyphs to the first letter thenceforth might confer. The second reason propped from the need to eschew the immanent supererogatory burden of having to write them with an added character when they could remain plain or without partial alteration and still maintain their functions as short vowels.

Hivyo, matumizi ya maki na mstari wa juu katika makala hii yanafuata mfano unaofanana na matumizi yake na Karshima (2012) kuhusu lugha ya Tiv au kuhusu neno la Igala ìkpā (begi) katika Rodriguez (2014); lisichanganywe na íkpa (miguu inayopinda ndani), ambapo ā = /æ/ kama katika matamshi ya Kiingereza cha Marekani ya maneno ‘plan’ na ‘stand’ katika oxfordlearnersdictionary.com kama sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /a/ na /a:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA, ē = /ɛ/ kama katika neno la Kiingereza ‘any’, yaani; sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /e/ na /e:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA) kama ilivyotumika kuhusu neno ònēkèlē katika Rodriguez (2015) ambalo mwandishi wa karatasi hii yenyewe hapa tena angeandika upya kama ōnēkēlē (mume), ī = /i:/ > /x/ > /i/, yaani; sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /i/ na /i:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA, ō = /ɔ/ kama katika ‘o’ ya kati ya nyuma iliyozungukwa ya msingi inayotumika katika kutamka neno la Kiingereza ‘but’ au neno la Igala ōkō /ɔkɔ/ kuhusu ndege au meli ambalo linatofautiana na òkò /o˩koo˩˩/ (kifaru) au okā /okæ/ (mtindo, yaani; akrobati) au ókō /o:kɔ/ (pesa), na ‘ō’ ikiwa ni sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /o/ na /o:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA, ū = /u:/ > /x/ > /u/ kama katika Kilithuania, Kilivonia, na Kimaori, yaani; sauti ya kati kati ya sauti /u/ na /u:/ za mfumo wa sauti wa IPA. X katika mabano ya mdomo inawakilisha sauti ya kifonemiki isiyojulikana kwa orthografia za kiakribati ī & ū ∼ x. Uamuzi wa kutupilia mbali breve katika karatasi hii una msingi wa namna mbili. Kwanza ilikuwa kuhakikisha uundaji wa mfumo thabiti wa orthografia wenye irabu safi zinazotumika katika maumbo yao ya asili ya orthografia kama ilivyo katika maandishi mengi ya lugha ya Kiingereza, bila kujali marekebisho yoyote ya kimaandishi au sifa ambayo nyongeza ya glifi zinazokuja kwa herufi ya kwanza tangu hapo inaweza kutoa. Sababu ya pili ilitokana na hitaji la kuepuka mzigo wa ndani wa ziada usio wa lazima wa kuziandika kwa herufi iliyoongezwa wakati zingeweza kubaki wazi au bila mabadiliko ya sehemu na bado kudumisha kazi zake kama irabu fupi.

The desuetude towards the breve as done here was not a deliberate attempt to undo the merits of the Webster’s II New College Dictionary, their discretion are still very valid, specifically with respect to the study of the English language. This writer is well abreast with the unequivocal fact that left with a vagaries of indicative keys to choose from, it is always incumbent upon each writer to cherry-pick both the phonotactical and the morphotactical forms that are amenable to the language being worked on (Pretorius and Bosch, 2009), it should however be noted that the use of ā = /ei/ and ō = /eu/, despite not being wrong in any sense, doesn’t nonetheless conforms with the original phonological flow pattern enshrined in their root alphabet ‘a’ which produces the sounds /a/ and /a:/, and ‘e’ which produces the sounds /e/ and /e:/. Bearing in mind the fact that the middle tone is sometimes perceived to be a conflation of two vowels (i.e.; /e/ + /i/ = /ei/ and /e/ + /u/ = /eu/ as it is in English), then it would not be totally wrong to say that the editors of the Webster’s II New College Dictionary were right in their own right to have used the macron in the context they employed it, besides, language analyst do agree that, ‘there is no such thing as a standard form when it comes to matters of language’ (Laperre 2020) and as Szczegielniak laconically hinted, “spelling or orthography does not consistently represent the sounds of language”. Thus, in Igala language, middle tone implies is a position between a long vowel and a short vowel such as the middle sound between the high /a:/ and the low /a/, i.e.; an ‘a’ that has a higher tone than /a/ but yet is still less pitched than /a:/ represented as x > /a/ < /a:/.

Kutotumika kuelekea breve kama ilivyofanywa hapa hakukuwa jaribio la makusudi la kubatilisha sifa za Webster's II New College Dictionary, busara zao bado ni halali sana, hasa kuhusiana na utafiti wa lugha ya Kiingereza. Mwandishi huyu anafahamu vyema ukweli usio na shaka kwamba akiachwa na uteuzi wa vifunguo vya kuashiria visivyotabirika, ni wajibu wa kila mwandishi kuchagua kwa uangalifu maumbo ya kifonotaktiki na kimofotaktiki ambayo yanakubaliana na lugha inayoshughulikiwa (Pretorius na Bosch, 2009), hata hivyo inapaswa kuzingatiwa kwamba matumizi ya ā = /ei/ na ō = /eu/, licha ya kutokuwa makosa kwa namna yoyote, hayalingani hata hivyo na mtiririko asilia wa kifonolojia uliowekwa katika alfabeti yao ya mzizi 'a' ambayo hutoa sauti /a/ na /a:/, na 'e' ambayo hutoa sauti /e/ na /e:/. Kwa kuzingatia ukweli kwamba toni ya kati wakati mwingine huchukuliwa kuwa ni muunganiko wa vokali mbili (yaani; /e/ + /i/ = /ei/ na /e/ + /u/ = /eu/ kama ilivyo katika Kiingereza), basi haingekuwa kosa kabisa kusema kwamba wahariri wa Webster's II New College Dictionary walikuwa sahihi kwa haki yao wenyewe kutumia makroni katika muktadha waliyoitumia, zaidi ya hayo, wachambuzi wa lugha wanakubali kwamba, 'hakuna kitu kama umbo sanifu linapokuja suala la mambo ya lugha' (Laperre 2020) na kama Szczegielniak alivyodokeza kwa ufupi, "tahajia haiwakilishi sauti za lugha kwa uthabiti". Hivyo, katika lugha ya Kiigala, toni ya kati ina maana ya nafasi kati ya vokali ndefu na vokali fupi kama vile sauti ya kati kati ya /a:/ ya juu na /a/ ya chini, yaani; 'a' ambayo ina toni ya juu kuliko /a/ lakini bado ina kiwango cha chini cha sauti kuliko /a:/ inayowakilishwa kama x > /a/ < /a:/.

This writer would have loved to have the alphabet ė which is hereby sounded /ei/ written as ȧ, but because most words in the language are already written with the letter ‘e’ to sound /ei/, as in words such as ugbede /ugbeidei/ or ele /eilei/, the author therefore chose to maintain the letter ė which comes closer to the now more popular tradition. The decision to use the alphabet ȧ instead of ė would have matched perfectly with the use of the over dotted ‘o’ (ȯ) to produce the sound /eu/. The use of the macron itself becomes vital considering the fact that, most words in the Igala language are sounded within this mid tone context.

Mwandishi huyu angependa alfabeti ė ambayo kwa hivyo inatamkwa /ei/ iandikwe kama ȧ, lakini kwa sababu maneno mengi katika lugha tayari yameandikwa kwa herufi 'e' kutamka /ei/, kama katika maneno kama vile ugbede /ugbeidei/ au ele /eilei/, mwandishi kwa hiyo alichagua kudumisha herufi ė ambayo inakaribia zaidi mila iliyo maarufu zaidi sasa. Uamuzi wa kutumia alfabeti ȧ badala ya ė ungepatana kikamilifu na matumizi ya 'o' yenye nukta juu (ȯ) kutoa sauti /eu/. Matumizi ya makroni yenyewe yanakuwa muhimu kwa kuzingatia ukweli kwamba, maneno mengi katika lugha ya Kiigala yanatamkwa ndani ya muktadha huu wa toni ya kati.

As in writing the Yoruba language or French (Olufemi, 2022; Nolte et al. 2018), the acute and the grave forms of diacritic were also used although with a contrastive difference in phonation between the Igala words written here using this form that is different from French as regards to the word café, in which case, the é as in the French and in the form used by Rodriguez (2014) in relation to the word ‘éʤú’ ∼ /eiʤu:/ (face), is pronounced /ei/, more like the English word rake /reik/, whereas, the orthography é as it relates with the Igala and sometimes with the Yoruba; as with the word òré (friend) in (Oshodi, 2016) ∼ /oo˩˩re:/, is nonetheless pronounced /e:/ as in English. Thus, we must agree with Cahill, (2019) that, in using diacritic tools, “the challenge is that the tone marks are usually not consistent among scholars”, with the result that, a letter é that is pronounced as /e:/ in one instance or by one scholar is pronounced at another instance or by another scholar as /ei/ as in spelling of wálé ∼ /wa:lei/ (come home) in Dalamu (2019) which is at variance with Oshodi (2016)’s /e:/ above.

Kama ilivyo katika uandishi wa lugha ya Kiyoruba au Kifaransa (Olufemi, 2022; Nolte et al. 2018), aina kali na ya kaburi ya alama za matamshi pia zilitumika ingawa kwa tofauti ya kinyume katika utamkaji kati ya maneno ya Kiigala yaliyoandikwa hapa kwa kutumia umbo hili ambalo ni tofauti na Kifaransa kuhusiana na neno café, katika hali hiyo, é kama ilivyo katika Kifaransa na katika umbo lililotumiwa na Rodriguez (2014) kuhusiana na neno 'éʤú' ∼ /eiʤu:/ (uso), linatamkwa /ei/, zaidi kama neno la Kiingereza rake /reik/, ilhali, tahajia é inavyohusiana na Kiigala na wakati mwingine na Kiyoruba; kama ilivyo kwa neno òré (rafiki) katika (Oshodi, 2016) ∼ /oo˩˩re:/, hata hivyo inatamkwa /e:/ kama katika Kiingereza. Hivyo, ni lazima tukubaliane na Cahill, (2019) kwamba, katika kutumia zana za alama za matamshi, "changamoto ni kwamba alama za toni kwa kawaida hazilingani miongoni mwa wasomi", matokeo yake ni kwamba, herufi é ambayo inatamkwa kama /e:/ katika tukio moja au na msomi mmoja inatamkwa katika tukio lingine au na msomi mwingine kama /ei/ kama ilivyo katika tahajia ya wálé ∼ /wa:lei/ (njoo nyumbani) katika Dalamu (2019) ambayo inakinzana na /e:/ ya Oshodi (2016) hapo juu.

Here too, the grave accent (i.e.; à, è, ì, ò, ù) was used to indicate the low tones as was done in Yip (2002). The use of certain diacritic marks in this work might be counted as inappropriate as is now apparent with respect to how the macron is used here which is already in conflict with how it was used in the Webster’s II Dictionary or how any of these marks are represented in other languages such as French, Dutch, Portuguese, Italian, and Mohawk (Finegan, 2012).

Hapa pia, alama ya mkazo mzito (yaani; à, è, ì, ò, ù) ilitumika kuashiria toni za chini kama ilivyofanywa katika Yip (2002). Matumizi ya alama fulani za vikwazo katika kazi hii yanaweza kuhesabiwa kuwa yasiyofaa kama inavyoonekana sasa kuhusiana na jinsi mkazo mrefu unavyotumika hapa ambao tayari unakinzana na jinsi ulivyotumika katika Kamusi ya Webster's II au jinsi alama hizi zinavyowakilishwa katika lugha nyingine kama vile Kifaransa, Kiholanzi, Kireno, Kiitaliano, na Kimohawk (Finegan, 2012).

A critic might ask to know why ‘this author did not use the breve diacritic in writing the short vowels’, which has already been reflected upon above. To cover any anomaly that emanates from how certain things are represented in this work, enabling tools with instructions regarding how to pronounce the diacritic forms is provided, with the hope that the writer of this piece and the readers might be on a common wavelength in comprehending what has been put down. Hence, words having these marks which as this writer suppose might not follow the existing methods in some languages, can be pronounced; if not so perfectly, it would be ‘nearly perfect’.

Mhakiki anaweza kuuliza kujua kwa nini 'mwandishi huyu hakutumia alama ya mkato mfupi katika kuandika vokali fupi', jambo ambalo tayari limetafakariwa hapo juu. Ili kufidia hitilafu yoyote inayotokana na jinsi mambo fulani yanavyowakilishwa katika kazi hii, zana saidizi zenye maelekezo kuhusu jinsi ya kutamka maumbo ya vikwazo zimetolewa, kwa matumaini kwamba mwandishi wa kipande hiki na wasomaji wanaweza kuwa na uelewa sawa katika kufahamu kile kilichoandikwa. Kwa hiyo, maneno yenye alama hizi ambayo kama mwandishi huyu anavyodhani huenda hayafuati mbinu zilizopo katika baadhi ya lugha, yanaweza kutamkwa; ikiwa si kikamilifu kabisa, yatakuwa 'karibu kamilifu'.

The Igala vowels are still yet not limited to the above mentioned accent forms. In this study, it is revealed that some vowels are a combination of double sound of the same vowel either ways raised between short or long vowel and then pitched lowly or highly with the corresponding vowel (like a bend), as was done with respect to that closing tone in the word Igalaa. For this category of vowels, the author came up with the idea of having orthographies with macrons on top of them that are further capped with a grave or the acute diacritic but only two of the short vowels ḕ, ḗ and ṑ, ṓ could meet this requirement, and so the author thought of remedying this problem with the use of the circumflex having either the acute or the grave diacritic mark on top of them, i.e.; ấ, ầ, but only the letter ‘a’ has this feature out of the other three still missing letters conveying the short vowels (a, i. and u). The next that could be done was to check for diacritic graphemes that have the features that can best solve the ‘u’ and the ‘i’ problem (regarding this category of sound), but this author once again stumbled upon the idea of using the ‘u’ dieresis capped with either the grave or the acute mark, i.e.; ǘ and ǜ, but the ‘i’ symbols was still missing, and so, the author thought of using the Greek small letter iota with psili and varia (ἲ) and ἵ small letter iota with psili and oxia which the author found as the only current fitting option for use here.

Vokali za Kiigala bado hazijazuiliwa kwa maumbo ya lafudhi yaliyotajwa hapo juu. Katika utafiti huu, imefunuliwa kwamba baadhi ya vokali ni mchanganyiko wa sauti mbili za vokali ileile zilizoinuliwa kwa namna yoyote kati ya vokali fupi au ndefu na kisha kupandishwa au kushushwa toni kwa vokali inayolingana (kama upinde), kama ilivyofanywa kuhusiana na ile toni ya kufunga katika neno Igalaa. Kwa kategoria hii ya vokali, mwandishi alibuni wazo la kuwa na maandishi yenye mkazo mrefu juu yake ambayo yamefunikwa zaidi kwa alama ya mkazo mzito au mkali lakini ni vokali fupi mbili tu ḕ, ḗ na ṑ, ṓ zilizoweza kukidhi hitaji hili, na kwa hiyo mwandishi alifikiria kurekebisha tatizo hili kwa kutumia alama ya kuzunguka yenye aidha alama ya mkazo mkali au mzito juu yake, yaani; ấ, ầ, lakini ni herufi 'a' pekee ndiyo yenye sifa hii kati ya herufi nyingine tatu ambazo bado hazipo zinazowasilisha vokali fupi (a, i. na u). Jambo lililofuata ambalo lingeweza kufanywa lilikuwa kuangalia grafimu zenye alama za matamshi ambazo zina sifa zinazoweza kutatua vyema tatizo la 'u' na 'i' (kuhusiana na kategoria hii ya sauti), lakini mwandishi huyu alikwama tena kwenye wazo la kutumia dieresisi ya 'u' ikiwa imefunikwa kwa alama ya grave au acute, yaani; ǘ na ǜ, lakini alama ya 'i' bado haikuwepo, na kwa hiyo, mwandishi alifikiria kutumia herufi ndogo ya Kigiriki iota yenye psili na varia (ἲ) na ἵ herufi ndogo ya iota yenye psili na oxia ambayo mwandishi aliiona kama chaguo pekee linalofaa kwa sasa kwa matumizi hapa.

2.1 Contextual Usage of Vowels

2.1 Matumizi ya Kimuktadha ya Vokali

In writing the orthographic forms of Igala vowels, one would thus have 30 of these, with each of the short vowels producing 5 variants each while from the glyph ȯ and ė can be gotten an additional 8 diphthongs. In the Webster’s II New College Dictionary, orthographic characters and phonetic sounds adhering or being inherent in the long vowels of the English language were written in the forms; /â/ or /ä/ = /a: /, /ê/ = /e: /, /ē/ = /i: /, /ô/ = /o: /, /û/ = /u: /, /ā/ = /ei/, and ō = /eu/. In this work the above 5 high tones were replicated as á = /a: /, é = /e: /, í = /i: /, ó = /o: /, ú = /u: /. Added to these long vowels are the 5 short vowels a = /a/, e = /e/, i = /i/, o = /o/, u = /u/. There are also 5 macron diacritic to produce the intermediate of these vowels, ā, ē, ī, ō, ū, whose vocal forms have been explained above.

Katika kuandika maumbo ya orthografia ya vokali za Kiigala, mtu angekuwa hivyo na 30 kati ya hizi, huku kila vokali fupi ikitoa vibadala 5 kila moja wakati kutoka kwa glifi ȯ na ė kunaweza kupatikana difthongo 8 za ziada. Katika Webster's II New College Dictionary, herufi za orthografia na sauti za kifonetiki zinazoshikamana au kuwa asili katika vokali ndefu za lugha ya Kiingereza ziliandikwa katika maumbo; /â/ au /ä/ = /a: /, /ê/ = /e: /, /ē/ = /i: /, /ô/ = /o: /, /û/ = /u: /, /ā/ = /ei/, na ō = /eu/. Katika kazi hii toni 5 za juu zilizo hapo juu zilinakiliwa kama á = /a: /, é = /e: /, í = /i: /, ó = /o: /, ú = /u: /. Kuongezwa kwa vokali hizi ndefu ni vokali 5 fupi a = /a/, e = /e/, i = /i/, o = /o/, u = /u/. Pia kuna alama 5 za macron za kutengeneza kati ya vokali hizi, ā, ē, ī, ō, ū, ambazo maumbo yake ya kivokali yameelezwa hapo juu.

There are 5 long vowels that are stressed with falling tones, i.e.; as in à = /aa˩˩/, è = /ee˩˩/, ì = /ii˩˩/, ò = /oo˩˩/, ù = /uu˩˩/. With the exception of the orthographic symbol ‘o’ which is capped with the double grave and double acute accent (i.e.; ȍ /oo˩˩˩˩/ and ő /o::/) used to express the extra-low and the extra-high accent of the /o/ sound, the other four orthographies bearing the primary vowels (a, e, i, and u) whose logographic form (as lone orthographies) expresses the pronouns ‘a’ (me), ‘e’ (you), ‘i’ (s/he or it), and ‘u’ (first person me) which do convey emphasis and mood, have dieresis-like rise-rise inflective sounds with one, the combine ‘a’ (ấ = /a:a:/) having a circumflex on top of it with an acute glyph capping the circumflex. Others are, the combined ‘e’ (ḗ = /e:e:/) which has a macron glyphs on top of it with an acute accents capping the macron, one other has a dieresis glyph with a grave accent on top of the glyph (ǘ = /u:u:/), and the fourth is the Greek iota with psili and varia with the combined ‘i’ (ἵ = /i:i:/). Each of these 4 vowel begin with short forms of vowels that are succeeded by corresponding long vowels of the same sound bracket within given syllables, more like ending the sound flow in the middle but yet again stressing it forward (an intermittent break that is followed by a change in flow).

Kuna vokali 5 ndefu ambazo zinasisitizwa kwa toni za kushuka, yaani; kama katika à = /aa˩˩/, è = /ee˩˩/, ì = /ii˩˩/, ò = /oo˩˩/, ù = /uu˩˩/. Isipokuwa alama ya othografia 'o' ambayo imefunikwa kwa lafudhi mbili za kaburi na mbili za kikali (yaani; ȍ /oo˩˩˩˩/ na ő /o::/) zinazotumika kueleza lafudhi ya chini-zaidi na ya juu-zaidi ya sauti ya /o/, othografia nyingine nne zenye vokali kuu (a, e, i, na u) ambazo umbo lake la kialama (kama othografia pekee) hueleza viwakilishi 'a' (mimi), 'e' (wewe), 'i' (yeye au kitu), na 'u' (nafsi ya kwanza mimi) ambavyo huwasilisha mkazo na hali, zina sauti za kiambishi cha kupanda-kupanda kama dieresis huku moja, mchanganyiko 'a' (ấ = /a:a:/) ikiwa na mkumbo juu yake na alama ya kikali ikifunika mkumbo huo. 2.2 Diphthongs

2.2 Diphthongs

In writing the Igala token, the ‘mid-tones’ ‘ȯ’ and ‘ė’ with respect to the sounds /eu/ and /ei/ do not always remain the same for some of these sounds could be in context short, intermediate or long in tone, meaning that up to eight varieties of these two sounds can be gotten but in the main time, the author could only come about four glyphs for writing them, that being ė = /ei/, ǡ = /eiei/, ȯ = /eu/, and ȱ = /eueu/. These eight sounds are generated from the first vowels /e/ + /i/ = /ei/ and /e/ + /u/ = /eu/, out of which we have 8 diphthongs, although some of these are not currently portrayed by any orthographic symbol. There would thus be the need for additional symbols, i.e.; dotted ‘ė’ or ‘ȧ’ and ȯ orthographies with a grave or acute accents placed (either ways) on top of the dots on each to sound /eieii˩˩/ and /eueuu˩˩/ or /eiei:/ and /eueu:/.

2.3 Elision and other forms of Vowel Combination

2.3 Elision na aina nyingine za mchanganyiko wa vokali

Since the Igala language ‘somewhat’ follows the ‘French Lemon Rule’ or the VCV (vowel – consonant – vowel) Rule for it is rare to have situation where vowels follow themselves concurrently during words formation. Even in one instance where this rule is broken as shall be demonstrated below, this is more the result of an elision of a corresponding consonant sound than a naturally fixed format for that word. The only known exception to this rule is in respect to the word ābū which means ‘how’ or ‘what’, or where’, in which case, speakers tend to deliberately elicit the /b/ consonant or both the /b/ and and the succeeding /u/ vowels during communication for only the vowel /a/, or /a/ with /u/ in respect to the word mà (them) is educed so that when succeeded with the suffices ū (I or me), ē (you), ā (we), ī (she/he/it), and mà (them), the word becomes sounded as ā’ū (how or what did I, as in the sentence “ā’ū kā?” (how or what did I say), ā’ē (how or what did you, as in the sentence “ā’ē mā?” (how do you know), ā’ā (how or what did we, as in the statement, “ā’ā chė?” (how or what did we do), ā’ī (how or where did she/he/it, as in the sentence, “ā’ī lė?” (how or where did she/he/it go), and ā’ū mà or ā’mà (how or what do they, as in the sentence “ā’ū mà kō” or “ā’mā kō?” (What or how did they write?). Although, these other forms are used as substitute for ābū, they don’t however constitute a better alternative to ābū which is the actual form of how the word should be used for these are only corrupt versions of that word ābū.

Kwa kuwa lugha ya Kiigala ‘kwa kiasi fulani' inafuata ‘Kanuni ya Limau ya Kifaransa' au Kanuni ya VCV (irabu – konsonanti – irabu) kwa sababu ni nadra kuwa na hali ambapo irabu hujifuata zenyewe kwa wakati mmoja wakati wa uundaji wa maneno. Hata katika tukio moja ambapo kanuni hii inavunjwa kama itakavyoonyeshwa hapa chini, hii ni zaidi matokeo ya elisioni ya sauti ya konsonanti inayolingana kuliko muundo uliowekwa kiasili kwa neno hilo. Ubaguzi pekee unaojulikana kwa kanuni hii ni kuhusiana na neno ābū linalomaanisha ‘vipi' au ‘nini', au ‘wapi', ambapo, wazungumzaji huwa na mwelekeo wa kutamka kwa makusudi konsonanti /b/ au zote mbili /b/ na irabu /u/ zinazofuata wakati wa mawasiliano kwa maana irabu /a/ pekee, au /a/ pamoja na /u/ kuhusiana na neno mà (wao) hupunguzwa ili wakati inapofuatiwa na viambishi tamati ū (mimi), ē (wewe), ā (sisi), ī (yeye), na mà (wao), neno hilo linasikika kama ā'ū (vipi au nini nilifanya, kama katika sentensi “ā'ū kā?” (vipi au nini nilisema), ā'ē (vipi au nini ulifanya, kama katika sentensi “ā'ē mā?” (vipi unajua), ā'ā (vipi au nini tulifanya, kama katika kauli, “ā'ā chė?” (vipi au nini tulifanya), ā'ī (vipi au wapi alifanya, kama katika sentensi, “ā'ī lė?” (vipi au wapi alienda), na ā'ū mà au ā'mà (vipi au nini wanafanya, kama katika sentensi “ā'ū mà kō” au “ā'mā kō?” (Nini au vipi waliandika?). Ingawa, aina hizi nyingine hutumika kama mbadala wa ābū, hata hivyo hazijumuishi mbadala bora zaidi wa ābū ambao ndio umbo halisi la jinsi neno hilo linavyopaswa kutumika kwa maana hizi ni matoleo potovu tu ya neno hilo ābū.

More also, when writing sentences in the Igala language, certain letters are omitted from some words to avoid creepiness, muddiness or jumpiness and in the place of these omitted alphabets the apostrophe is used as a way of showing these elisions. The hyphen can be used to write certain words but for a writer who has a better understanding of the parts of speech, the use of the hyphen becomes almost unnecessary, but rather than committing the error of agglutinating the words or creating ambiguity problems in word usage as was observed by Malema et al. (2020), with a better understanding of inherent speech parts, it would have been preferable to have these words hyphenated. Since vowels are not reechoed, the use of the umlaut or dieresis as is common with some Northern European languages or English is not common since there was no need for any two vowels to be cluttered to be vocalized. More so, since there are currently no homegrown phonemic orthographies at the moment or because the development of a standardized version of the Igala language is still an evolving process, there is the need to check against the practice in the English language sound system where vowels could produce multiple sounds or where certain sounds are repeated even when the repetition is almost nearly unnecessary, as one will have with the repetition of the letter ‘l’ in the word ‘ball’, or the repetition of the ‘g’ in ‘egg’.

(2020), kwa uelewa bora wa aina za maneno asili, ingekuwa bora zaidi maneno haya kuandikwa kwa kistari kifupi. Zaidi ya hayo, kwa kuwa kwa sasa hakuna othografia za kifonemiki za kienyeji au kwa sababu uundaji wa toleo sanifu la lugha ya Igala bado ni mchakato unaoendelea, kuna haja ya kuangalia dhidi ya mazoezi katika mfumo wa sauti wa lugha ya Kiingereza ambapo vokali zinaweza kutoa sauti nyingi au ambapo sauti fulani hurudiwa hata wakati marudio hayo ni karibu yasiyo ya lazima, kama itakavyokuwa na marudio ya herufi 'l' katika neno 'ball', au marudio ya 'g' katika 'egg'.

In English, letters as used in certain words do not usually follow a uniform pattern, i.e., letter ‘a’ (which could sound as /ei/ as in ‘cake’ or /æ/ as in ‘can’ /kæn/, or /o:/ as in ball and as in /a:/ in ‘cart’), letter ‘e’ (which could sound as /e/ as in ‘end’ or /i/ as in ‘elastic’ or ‘penis’), letter ‘i’ (which could pronounced as /i/ as in ink or /ai/ as in kite), letter ‘o’ (which could sound like /o/ as in on or /o:/ as in off, and as /u/ or /u:/ as in oops or ooze when repeated or as /Ʌ/, /o/, or /u:/ when letter ‘u’ comes immediately in front of letter ‘o’ in a spelling as we have in the words coupling or thought or coup), letter ‘u’ (which could be pronounced as /u/ as in book or /u:/ as in boom or /Ʌ/ as in umbrella, or as /3:/ as in urban when r is placed after letter u). Like the Hungarian language or other languages that do not depend exclusively on the use of the plain alphabet but employs some measure of diacritic to widen the range of available alphabets so as to accommodate more letters that are produced distinctly, while writing the Igala language, we must not necessarily change the sound of a monophony by that mere rule that some words having similar spelling be pronounced differently, neither are additional phoneme added to a vowel or a consonant as a way of changing the sound that the carrying letter is supposed to produce into another phonetic sound as we have with the English words plough or bought, and fort and resort. This problem of pronunciation could pose some headache to a new learner of the English language.

3 Conclusion

3 Hitimisho

This paper provides an overview of vowels used in the Igala language. Vowels are central to word formation and without them speech making becomes impossible. These vowels combine with the consonant sound to form words but this is not to say that without the consonant sounds, certain words cannot be verbalized or written as in the case of the Igala language where certain words can be written without the combination of consonant sounds with the consonant sounds as it applies to the four vowels, a, e. i, u which by standing alone means something. This is largely the result of the fact that, the Igala language has a wide ranging tone system.

Karatasi hii inatoa muhtasari wa vokali zinazotumika katika lugha ya Igala. Vokali ni msingi wa uundaji wa maneno na bila hizo utungaji wa usemi unakuwa hauwezekani. Vokali hizi huchangamana na sauti ya konsonanti kuunda maneno lakini hii haimaanishi kwamba bila sauti za konsonanti, maneno fulani hayawezi kutamkwa au kuandikwa kama ilivyo katika lugha ya Igala ambapo maneno fulani yanaweza kuandikwa bila mchanganyiko wa sauti za konsonanti na sauti za konsonanti kama inavyotumika kwa vokali nne, a, e. i, u ambazo kwa kusimama peke yake humaanisha kitu. Hii kwa kiasi kikubwa ni matokeo ya ukweli kwamba, lugha ya Kiigala ina mfumo mpana wa toni.

4 Limitation of the Study

4 Mipaka ya Utafiti

As an under-resourced indigenous language whose contextual study as a culture transmitting agent is relatively new (Ojoajogwu, 2017), approaches towards formalization of the Igala language in a more modern sense has been done scantily but also indiscriminately in ways that are devoid of any serious scientific consideration, more so that those who made attempt at writing the language are either not language specialist who understand scientific rudiments regarding the rules of language as it relates with grammar and tone pattern and structure, or were individuals who were too hasty in a way that deep thinking regarding the depth and breadth of both grammar and tones contained in the language were not attended to at great depth. It was therefore not surprising that among some of the available materials that contain words, sentences and meanings, one often see variations in diacritic forms used to demonstrate tones and pronunciation from scholar to scholar and in some cases certain diacritic orthographies that can represent some tones are left out. Added to this is also a variation with respect to accent and tribal differences in tones from one area to the other which has further influence the work output of the various writers. This factor therefore contribute to the first limitation of this very study, because understanding the inherent flaws regarding the inappropriate diacritic orthographies adopted by other writers, the very writer developed separate tools representing several tones, which while being scientifically logical, nonetheless, confine exclusively not to any previous methods used by the earlier writers.

Kama lugha ya kiasili isiyo na rasilimali za kutosha ambayo uchunguzi wake wa kimuktadha kama chombo cha kupitisha utamaduni ni mpya kiasi (Ojoajogwu, 2017), mikabala ya kurasimisha lugha ya Kiigala kwa maana ya kisasa zaidi imefanywa kwa uchache lakini pia bila kubagua kwa njia ambazo hazina uzingativu wowote wa kisayansi, hasa kwamba wale waliojaribu kuandika lugha hiyo ama si wataalamu wa lugha wanaoelewa misingi ya kisayansi kuhusu kanuni za lugha kuhusiana na sarufi na muundo na mpangilio wa toni, au walikuwa watu binafsi waliofanya haraka kwa njia ambayo fikra za kina kuhusu kina na upana wa sarufi na toni zote zilizomo katika lugha hiyo hazikushughulikiwa kwa kina kikubwa. Kwa hiyo haikushangaza kwamba miongoni mwa baadhi ya nyenzo zilizopo zenye maneno, sentensi na maana, mara nyingi mtu huona tofauti katika miundo ya alama za matamshi zinazotumiwa kuonyesha toni na matamshi kutoka kwa mwanazuoni mmoja hadi mwingine na katika baadhi ya matukio baadhi ya maandishi ya alama za matamshi ambayo yanaweza kuwakilisha toni fulani huachwa. Kinachoongezwa kwa hili pia ni tofauti kuhusiana na lafudhi na tofauti za kikabila katika toni kutoka eneo moja hadi jingine ambayo imeathiri zaidi matokeo ya kazi ya waandishi mbalimbali. Sababu hii ndiyo inayochangia upungufu wa kwanza wa utafiti huu, kwa sababu kwa kuelewa makosa yaliyomo katika matamshi yasiyofaa yaliyofanywa na waandishi wengine, mwandishi mwenyewe alitengeneza zana tofauti zinazowakilisha toni kadhaa, ambazo ingawa ni za kimantiki kisayansi, hata hivyo, hazihusiani kikamilifu na mbinu zozote zilizotumiwa na waandishi wa awali.

Although, prior to this current work, no effort was made to study the oral form of the Igala language from the basic in a more comprehensive form, such as is done in this paper, a fact which accounted for the neglect of some vowel forms represented by special symbols as stated above. But vowels were not the only orthographies left out for even consonant orthographies that present a single symbol as a unit of consonant cluster such as the ‘c’ with an over dot, i.e.; ċ, or the caron or hacek as it is at times called; č, both of whom represent the sound /ch/ in other world languages, as with respect to the English word ‘church’, and the symbol ŋ which is supposed to represent a single unit of the cluster /ng/ as in the word ‘ānyīngā’ (finger nail) which ought to have been ‘āɲīŋā’ or the ‘ny’ cluster (ɲ) is hardly used. While the non-use of these orthographies by previous writer posed some limitation with respect to how this very writer would have written syllables that should carry these orthographies, it was also a limitation of this study because this very writer failed to do otherwise from what has been held down for so long owing to English influence on the writing of the Igala language. While the tilde ñ which bear the /nn/ cluster and which is occasionally used by some of the earlier writers, albeit with some misconception here and there, in this work, words boiling around this very sign were not used and for that reason, the sign was bypassed, but in future attempt, it shall be treated with special consideration.

Ingawa, kabla ya kazi hii ya sasa, hakuna jitihada iliyofanywa kuchunguza umbo la mazungumzo la lugha ya Kiigala kutoka msingi kwa namna ya kina zaidi, kama inavyofanywa katika karatasi hii, ukweli ambao ulichangia kupuuzwa kwa baadhi ya miundo ya vokali inayowakilishwa na alama maalum kama ilivyoelezwa hapo juu. Lakini vokali hazikuwa orthografia pekee zilizoachwa nje kwani hata orthografia za konsonanti zinazowasilisha alama moja kama kitengo cha kundi la konsonanti kama vile ‘c' yenye nukta juu, yaani; ċ, au caron au hacek kama inavyoitwa wakati mwingine; č, zote mbili zikiwakilisha sauti /ch/ katika lugha nyingine za dunia, kama ilivyo kwa neno la Kiingereza ‘church', na alama ŋ ambayo inapaswa kuwakilisha kitengo kimoja cha kundi /ng/ kama katika neno ‘ānyīngā' (ukucha wa kidole) ambalo lingepaswa kuwa ‘āɲīŋā' au kundi la ‘ny' (ɲ) halitumiki sana. Ingawa kutotumika kwa orthografia hizi na mwandishi wa awali kulileta kizuizi fulani kuhusiana na jinsi mwandishi huyu angeandika silabi zinazopaswa kubeba orthografia hizi, pia ilikuwa kizuizi cha utafiti huu kwa sababu mwandishi huyu alishindwa kufanya vinginevyo kutoka kwa kile kilichoshikiliwa kwa muda mrefu kutokana na ushawishi wa Kiingereza katika uandishi wa lugha ya Igala. Ingawa tilde ñ ambayo hubeba kundi la /nn/ na ambayo hutumiwa mara kwa mara na baadhi ya waandishi wa awali, ingawa kukiwa na dhana potofu hapa na pale, katika kazi hii, maneno yanayohusu ishara hii hasa hayakutumiwa na kwa sababu hiyo, ishara hiyo ilipitwa, lakini katika jaribio la baadaye, itashughulikiwa kwa uangalifu maalum.

Added to the above two limitations is the fact that some orthographies representing vowels do not currently exist to exhibit these sounds on the IPA list of symbols on Microsoft word as pointed out in the body of the work, or perhaps these symbols exists but because of the limited knowledge of this author regarding how some of the symbols are pronounced, they could not be accounted for by the author. This fact also poses a limitation of its own. Added to this missing vowels or undiscovered vowels by this author is the absence of a single symbol for the consonant clusters ‘kw’, ‘kp’, nw, and ‘gh’. This writer is aware that the over dotted p (ṗ), is currently used with respect to writing the Yoruba language, a development that some writers of Igala language have become accustomed to with respect to writing the ‘kp’ cluster as one would have with the Yoruba name ‘Tōṗē’ which is pronounced as /topke/ or /tōkpē/, but this symbolization of the /kp/ sound with ‘ṗ’ calls for question since it is not the first phone on the cluster. Could it then be that this symbolization is the result of the fact that, there is no over dotted ‘k’ on the IPA list of symbols or because the ‘p’ in the combination forms a more voiced phoneme than the ‘k’ or it is because the people by choice, chose to write like that? As for the ‘kw’ cluster, should the under lined ḵ (the qaph) merely remain as ق as it is in the Arabic orthography or another use as a single unit for presenting the ‘kw’ cluster be found in it within the Latin orthographic symbols for those languages whose writing systems are still evolving?

Kinachoongezwa kwenye vikwazo viwili vilivyotajwa hapo juu ni ukweli kwamba baadhi ya orthografia zinazowakilisha vokali hazipo kwa sasa ili kuonyesha sauti hizi kwenye orodha ya alama za IPA kwenye Microsoft word kama ilivyoonyeshwa katika mwili wa kazi, au labda alama hizi zipo lakini kwa sababu ya ujuzi mdogo wa mwandishi huyu kuhusu jinsi baadhi ya alama zinavyotamkwa, hazingeweza kuelezewa na mwandishi. Ukweli huu pia unaleta kizuizi chake chenyewe. Kinachoongezwa kwenye vokali hizi zilizokosekana au vokali ambazo hazijagunduliwa na mwandishi huyu ni ukosefu wa alama moja kwa makundi ya konsonanti ‘kw', ‘kp', nw, na ‘gh'. Mwandishi huyu anafahamu kwamba p yenye nukta juu (ṗ), kwa sasa inatumika kuhusiana na uandishi wa lugha ya Kiyoruba, maendeleo ambayo baadhi ya waandishi wa lugha ya Igala wamezoea kuhusiana na uandishi wa kundi la ‘kp' kama mtu angefanya kwa jina la Kiyoruba ‘Tōṗē' ambalo linatamkwa kama /topke/ au /tōkpē/, lakini uwekaji alama huu wa sauti /kp/ kwa ‘ṗ' unaleta swali kwa kuwa siyo fonimu ya kwanza kwenye kundi hilo. Je, inawezekana basi kwamba uwekaji alama huu ni matokeo ya ukweli kwamba, hakuna ‘k' yenye nukta juu kwenye orodha ya alama za IPA au kwa sababu ‘p' katika mchanganyiko huo huunda fonimu yenye sauti zaidi kuliko ‘k' au ni kwa sababu watu kwa hiari yao, walichagua kuandika hivyo? Kuhusu kundi la 'kw', je, ḵ iliyopigiwa mstari (qaph) inapaswa kubaki tu kama ق kama ilivyo katika tahajia ya Kiarabu au matumizi mengine kama kitengo kimoja cha kuwasilisha kundi la 'kw' yapatikane ndani yake miongoni mwa alama za tahajia za Kilatini kwa lugha zile ambazo mifumo yake ya uandishi bado inaendelea kubadilika?
